# Legal Bright Lines

## Korea (행정사법, 변호사법, 표시광고법)

**CAN:** Display published requirements, requirement-matching quizzes, date calculators, checklists, info products.

**NEVER say:** "you qualify", "you are eligible", "recommended visa", "official requirements", "guaranteed".

**NEVER do:** File applications, store HiKorea credentials, broker 행정사, auto-fill government forms.

Every quiz/tool must disclaim: "Based on published requirements. Not legal advice."

## Taiwan (Immigration Act §56, Attorney Act §127)

Penalties: NT$200K-1M per violation. Up to 1 year imprisonment for unlicensed consulting.

**CAN:** Published requirements with source links, comparison tables (factual, no ranking by "fit"), document checklists (client-side only), TECO routing, generic transition paths, day counters (arithmetic only), community forums with disclaimers.

**NEVER:** Match scores/percentages/probability, rank by "fit" or "suitability", say "you qualify/eligible/recommended", auto-fill government forms, scrape NIA/BOCA, store passport/ARC numbers server-side, AI chatbot for eligibility questions, use "consulting" (諮詢) for any feature.

**Taiwan Quiz:** No scores. Output = side-by-side "Published Requirement" vs "Your Answer". Client-side only. Include "This is not an eligibility assessment."

**Taiwan Disclaimers:** Every page — English AND 繁體中文. Must state LocalNomad is not a licensed 移民業務機構. Quiz results: disclaimer ABOVE and BELOW.

**Taiwan Data:** All user data client-side only (localStorage). Never transmit personal immigration data to backend.
