## Notes

- Our JSON covers 7 of 27 sub-categories, selected for relevance to the LocalNomad audience (professional immigrants, investors, overseas Koreans, retirees).
- F-5-7 (외국적동포) is distinct from F-5-6 (재외동포): F-5-6 is for F-4 holders who have resided 2+ years; F-5-7 is for foreign nationals of Korean descent who meet all requirements for nationality acquisition under the Nationality Act.
- F-5-13 (연금수혜자) is the official sub-category for retired pension recipients. There is no generic "retiree" F-5 sub-category.
- F-5-11 (특정분야능력소유자) qualification is based on outstanding ability in specialized fields, not a fixed income multiple. Criteria include SCI publications, Fortune 500 experience, top-200 university tenure, or significant IP holdings.
- F-5-23 (공익사업 은퇴이민 투자자) is a separate retiree-investor category requiring public interest investment of KRW 300M+.
