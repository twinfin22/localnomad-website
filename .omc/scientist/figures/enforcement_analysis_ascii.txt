## Cache Lookup Order: Hallucination Risk Gradient

  Cache Hit -----> Tracker Hit -----> Known URL -----> Search
  LOWEST RISK      LOW RISK           MEDIUM RISK      HIGHEST RISK
  (pre-verified)   (same-session)     (pre-vetted URL)  (4 error points)
  
  Risk amplifiers at each stage:
  - Cache:   Stale data, cache poisoning
  - Tracker: Content reuse from wrong page section  
  - Known:   Government page changed since last reference update
  - Search:  Wrong result selection, content misinterpretation,
             domain confusion, semantic distortion
