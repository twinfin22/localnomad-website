## What Changed January 1, 2026

Taiwan's neighbors are competing hard for foreign talent — and Taiwan watched, then decided to move faster.

The shift is clear: from "fine, you can stay" to "please stay." Four changes took effect January 1:

- **1-year PR fast-track** for Gold Card holders earning NT$6M+ annually (was 3 years)
- **Spouse open work permit** — apply directly to MOL, no employer sponsorship needed
- **Pension contributions** start on the first day of employment (was: after APRC only)
- **Simplified family sponsorship**: income proof removed for dependents, parents 65+ now eligible

---

## 1-Year PR Fast-Track

The headline: **foreign professionals earning NT$6M or more annually can apply for permanent residency (APRC) after 1 year of continuous Taiwan residence.** Previously, everyone waited 3 years.

What APRC actually gives you:

- **Unrestricted work rights**: any employer, any field, or self-employment, no separate work permit
- **Indefinite stay**: no visa expiration as long as you maintain 183+ days/year in Taiwan
- **Property ownership**: purchase real estate without restrictions
- **NHI continuation**: National Health Insurance rolls forward automatically

The 183-day rule matters. This isn't an absentee PR card. Leave for more than 6 months and your APRC may be cancelled. (Honestly, if you're spending half the year somewhere else, Taiwan probably wasn't home anyway.)

Below NT$6M annually? The 3-year path is unchanged. The fast-track is only for high earners. Official details at [goldcard.nat.gov.tw/en/tags/aprc/](https://goldcard.nat.gov.tw/en/tags/aprc/).

## Spouse Open Work Permit

Before January 2026, a Gold Card holder's spouse needed employer sponsorship to work legally. That employer had to register, the permit was tied to that specific job, and switching meant reapplying. Many spouses just didn't bother.

Now spouses may apply directly to the Ministry of Labor (MOL) for an independent open work permit, with no employer sponsorship required.

## Pension and Family Changes

**Pension from day one.** Gold Card holders are now enrolled in Taiwan's mandatory labor pension (勞工退休金, labor pension) from the first day of employment. Employers contribute 6% of monthly salary; employees may voluntarily contribute up to 6%. Before this change, contributions only started after APRC was granted.

For someone earning NT$100,000 monthly: that's NT$6,000 in employer contributions per month, starting immediately. Over a year, NT$72,000 that was previously missed.

**Family sponsorship got simpler:**

- Income proof removed for dependent children and spouses (marriage certificate + passport now sufficient)
- Parents 65+ are newly eligible for dependent visas
- Processing time: 30–45 days → 15–20 days

## FAQ

**Does this affect DN Visa holders?**

No. The Digital Nomad Visa (DNV) is a separate track. The January 2026 changes apply only to Gold Card holders. DNV holders who want Gold Card benefits need to apply for the Gold Card separately, if they meet the eligibility criteria.

**How long does Gold Card processing take?**

60+ business days (approximately 2–4 months) from submission to approval, as of early 2026. Application fee is USD 100-310 (depending on nationality and duration), which covers the combined work permit and residence card. Apply at [goldcard.nat.gov.tw](https://goldcard.nat.gov.tw/en/).
