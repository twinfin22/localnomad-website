<TldrBox>
Pick Seoul if regular in-person meetings and the most connected transit grid matter. Pick Busan if you want a private-room search that is currently less expensive in the Seomyeon examples below, while staying in a dense city district. Pick Jeju only after you price mobility separately: the island's stay listings can be cheaper, but a car changes the month’s cost and daily plan.
</TldrBox>

This is for one foreign remote worker: a furnished private room or unit, regular video calls, and 30 nights from **1 November to 1 December 2026**. It is not a promise of a quiet room, fast in-room internet, or a particular community experience. Confirm those with the host or operator before paying.

## Start With Your Constraint, Not the City Name

| If this is the constraint | Start with | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You need frequent meetings across the metro area | Seoul — Mapo/Hongdae | Seoul’s [Climate Card](https://english.seoul.go.kr/policy/transportation/climate-card/) is an official unlimited-transit product; check its current coverage and eligibility before buying. |
| You want a dense, walkable base and do not need Seoul meetings every week | Busan — Seomyeon | Seomyeon is a rail-connected central district. The 30-night listings below put the selected rooms 300–600 m from Seomyeon Station. |
| Your work is mostly asynchronous and you are willing to plan trips around island mobility | Jeju City or Seogwipo | Jeju is an island choice: do not assume a car-free routine will resemble an urban commute. Price transport before choosing a neighbourhood. |

None of these are visa advice. If a 30-day stay changes your immigration, work, tax, or insurance position, check the applicable authority and your employer or adviser. For the Korea workation route, start with our [F-1-D guide](/en/blog/guides/korea-f1d-workation-visa-2026), which is updated separately from this draft.

## Work Setup: Verify the Four Things That Matter

For a daily video-call schedule, do not book based on a Wi-Fi icon alone. Ask before the cancellation deadline:

1. Is the room private for the whole reservation, and can the host confirm the desk and chair shown are in that room?
2. Can they share the internet type, a recent speed-test result, and whether the network is shared across units?
3. Are there quiet hours, construction notices, or a common area immediately beside the room?
4. What happens if a service outage makes the room unusable for work?

For an outside desk, use the operator’s current rules rather than a review from last year. [WeWork lists Busan coworking locations](https://www.wework.com/l/coworking-space/busan), and [Hoppin Busan publishes a coworking page](https://www.hoppin.life/742cd948-22e9-4f95-9e71-e5583f0e747d). Their existence is evidence of an operator, not a claim about noise level, day-pass availability, or community quality. Request the current plan, opening hours, call-booth policy, and guest rules directly.

## Neighbourhood Choice

### Seoul: Mapo and Hongdae

Choose this base when a metro-wide meeting or event is part of the job, not just a nice-to-have. Mapo/Hongdae puts you in a dense accommodation search area and near multiple rail connections. The trade-off is that a cheap dorm search result is not a substitute for a private, call-ready room; keep the room type constant when you compare listings.

For arrival logistics, cross-check the [Korea arrival checklist](/en/blog/tips/korea-arrival-checklist-2026) and the [Seoul coliving guide](/en/blog/guides/coliving-seoul-guide-2026). Neither replaces the listing’s current cancellation and utility terms.

### Busan: Seomyeon and Gwangalli

Seomyeon is the practical first search when you want a central station base. In the observed results, both chosen private-room examples identify themselves as within 300–600 m of Seomyeon Station. Gwangalli is a separate accommodation search: do not transfer Seomyeon price or walking-distance claims to it.

If repeat social contact matters, use published events and operator calendars as leads, then check whether they are recurring and open to visitors. A listing or a coworking landing page cannot establish that a particular community will be active during your month.

### Jeju: Jeju City and Seogwipo

Jeju City and Seogwipo solve different geography problems. Start with the side of the island where your recurring commitments are, then run the same 30-night search in both. The Jeju City examples above show why an island-wide average is misleading.

#### Car-free Jeju

Use this route only after mapping each recurring destination to a bus stop and checking the relevant timetable. Keep a separate allowance for taxis when a meeting cannot move. This draft does not state a car-free monthly transport total because no comparable public 30-day itinerary price was verified.

#### Jeju with a car

Keep these lines separate: vehicle rental, insurance/excess reduction, fuel or charging, parking, and any one-way or age-related fee. Do not multiply a search-engine “from” daily rate by 30 and call it a quote. We did not verify an all-in 30-day vehicle price for the same dates, so it is intentionally absent from the city totals.

## Season and Moving Burden

The selected period crosses November into December. Recheck weather-sensitive routes, heating terms, and cancellation windows at booking time. Moving between cities also has a cost in time and luggage handling; this comparison does not treat an intercity move as free just because the accommodation price is lower.
