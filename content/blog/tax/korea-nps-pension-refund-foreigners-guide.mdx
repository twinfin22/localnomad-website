Leaving Korea does not automatically entitle you to a National Pension refund. Check whether your nationality qualifies under a refund agreement or reciprocity, or whether your coverage was under an eligible work status. If eligible, you can arrange a bank payment, apply for same-day airport cash in advance, or file from abroad. The departure-based claim generally expires five years after the right arises. The eligibility and claim rules below were checked in November 2026.

> This guide covers the departure-based lump-sum refund. Confirm your eligibility and exact deadline with the [National Pension Service (NPS)](https://www.nps.or.kr/eng/ntnlpnsplan/frgnrlpsmrfnd/getOHAI0015M0.do?menuId=MN24001368) before leaving. In Korea you can call 1355.

## What Is NPS, and Why Is It Taking Your Money?

Korea's National Pension (국민연금) is social insurance. Coverage for foreign workers depends on their circumstances, including nationality, visa status and any applicable social security agreement. Being employed in Korea does not by itself establish either enrollment or refund eligibility. Check your contribution record with NPS.

In 2026, the [workplace contribution rate](https://www.nps.or.kr/pnsinfo/ntpsklg/getOHAF0016M0.do) is **9.5% of the NPS standard monthly income**, split into 4.75% each for employee and employer (checked November 2026). The standard amount and the rate in effect in each contribution month are what matter, not necessarily the gross salary printed on your payslip.

## Who Can Claim? Countries, Visas, and Agreements

Start with the [NPS payment list](https://www.nps.or.kr/eng/ntnlpnsplan/frgnrlpsmrfnd/getOHAI0015M0.do?menuId=MN24001368) dated June 15, 2026 (checked November 2026). It concerns the refund, not every agreement Korea has signed. An agreement about pension coverage or avoiding double contributions does not automatically confer a departure refund.

A minimum insured period is counted from NPS coverage, not simply time spent in Korea.

### Track 3: E-8, E-9, and H-2 Visa Holders

This route is based on the **insured period under the qualifying status**, not nationality. E-9 is non-professional employment and H-2 is visiting employment. The E-8 reference in the English guide is to the former training-employment status. The [Korean-language NPS guidance](https://www.nps.or.kr/pnsinfo/ntpsklg/getOHAF0084M0.do?menuId=MN24001125) specifically excludes the current E-8 seasonal-work status from this visa-based refund route (checked November 2026). It does not rule out a separate nationality-based route.

### USA Special Case: Totalization or Lump-Sum

The U.S. is on the refund-agreement list, but a refund can have pension consequences. Under the [Korea–U.S. agreement](https://www.nps.or.kr/eng/soclsecuagre/ctrtcny/getOHAI0025M0.do?div=03&menuId=MN24001394), periods for which you received a lump-sum refund cannot be counted toward totalization. Ask NPS which option applies to your record before claiming, particularly if you have coverage in both countries.

### If None of These Apply

You may not qualify for the departure-based lump-sum benefit even though you paid NPS contributions. Ask NPS about possible pension rights and your official coverage record instead of treating the contributions as automatically refundable.

## How Much Will You Get Back?

If you qualify, the gross refund is the **eligible contributions actually paid, including the employer portion for workplace coverage, plus statutory interest**. See [Article 77 of the National Pension Act](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsEfInfoP.do?lsiSeq=284451) and the [NPS calculation guidance](https://www.nps.or.kr/pnsinfo/ntpsklg/getOHAF0084M0.do?menuId=MN24001125) (checked November 2026). NPS calculates the interest by contribution month. Its published 2026 three-year fixed-deposit reference rate is 2.2%; it is not a flat 2.2% added to the entire balance.

For example, if your NPS standard monthly income was ₩3.5 million for all 12 months of 2026 and all 12 months qualify, the gross contributions alone would be **₩3,990,000** (₩3.5 million × 9.5% × 12), before interest and any withholding. If you paid into NPS in more than one year, check the rate in effect for each period.

### The Tax Withholding: Variable, Not Fixed

[NPS explains the retirement-income withholding calculation](https://www.nps.or.kr/pnsinfo/ntpsklg/getOHAF0088M0.do), which considers items such as taxable contributions and statutory deductions. There is no reliable flat percentage to use for every foreign applicant. Ask NPS for the withholding amount and receipt; check any home-country reporting or treaty treatment with the relevant tax authority or a qualified adviser.

## Method 1: Cash at Incheon Airport (Same Day)

Request airport payment at an NPS office or counseling center **other than the airport center**, within one month of departure. Bring your passport, residence card, flight booking and bank-account proof. Keep the certificate NPS gives you and provide a backup Korean or overseas account. Your former employer must report loss of coverage to NPS by the day before you fly.

On departure day, start at the **NPS Incheon Airport Center, Terminal 1, first floor between exits 1 and 2 (booths 7–8)**, even if your flight leaves from Terminal 2. Show your passport and application certificate. Take the payment direction to the appropriate Woori Bank exchange counter **before** immigration and collect your foreign currency at the designated Woori Bank point **after** immigration.

<Callout type="warning">
The NPS airport center is open 09:00–18:00 on service days. Eligible flights depart 10:30–24:00 from T1 or 11:00–24:00 from T2. Service is unavailable on weekends, public holidays and December's last business day. Bank-counter locations and hours differ by terminal and payment amount; [check the NPS airport instructions](https://www.nps.or.kr/eng/ntnlpnsplan/frgnrlpsmrfnd/getOHAI0015M0.do?menuId=MN24001368) before choosing this option (checked November 2026).
</Callout>

## Method 2: Bank Transfer After Departure

Arrange a Korean account payment or overseas remittance with NPS before you leave if airport cash is impractical. The [NPS application checklist](https://www.nps.or.kr/eng/ntnlpnsplan/frgnrlpsmrfnd/getOHAI0015M0.do?menuId=MN24001368) lists the claim form, passport, residence card, bank-account proof and evidence that you will leave within a month. Ask what your bank needs to receive an international transfer. If you have already left, use one of the overseas filing options below. Do not plan around a guaranteed processing time.

## Method 3: Claim From Abroad (The "I Forgot" Option)

The [NPS overseas filing instructions](https://www.nps.or.kr/eng/ntnlpnsplan/frgnrlpsmrfnd/getOHAI0015M0.do?menuId=MN24001368) allow direct postal filing or a designated representative in Korea. For postal filing, prepare the claim form, passport documentation, bank-account proof and overseas remittance application. A representative needs identity documentation, and the claim needs an agent section or signed authorization.

Depending on where documents were issued, you may need notarization, an apostille or Korean consular attestation, plus a notarized Korean translation. NPS lists some countries where a home-country social insurance agency can accept the application under a separate filing arrangement; that route can waive some document formalities. It does not itself confer refund eligibility. Confirm the destination office and formalities with NPS before mailing.

## Country Spotlight: What to Know Before You Go

**United States, Canada and Australia:** All appear on the refund agreement list. A person with coverage in two systems should check pension implications before claiming.

**United Kingdom, Japan and mainland China:** None appears on the June 15, 2026 NPS lump-sum refund agreement or reciprocity list. Do not assume a refund based on nationality alone; check whether a qualifying insured visa period applies.

**Philippines, Indonesia and Thailand:** The Philippines is listed under an agreement; Indonesia is listed under reciprocity with no minimum stated; Thailand is listed under reciprocity with at least one year of insured coverage. These are different routes, not interchangeable labels (checked November 2026).

## The 5-Year Clock

[Article 115 of the National Pension Act](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsEfInfoP.do?lsiSeq=284451) and [NPS guidance](https://www.nps.or.kr/pnsinfo/ntpsklg/getOHAF0084M0.do?menuId=MN24001125) set the ordinary departure-claim limitation period at **five years from when the right to payment arose** (checked November 2026). Ask NPS to confirm that date from your record; it is not automatically the date of your final paycheck. A later qualifying age-based or survivor claim is separate and has its own conditions. The ordinary departure deadline should not be treated as a flexible filing date.

## FAQ

### Can all foreigners get a pension refund from Korea?

No. Your NPS coverage and the reason you claim matter, plus a refund agreement, reciprocity or qualifying insured visa period.

### How long does the refund take?

Airport cash is designed for the day of departure if you file in advance and meet the flight, employment and counter requirements. For other routes, ask NPS for a current estimate for your documents and payment method.

### How much tax is withheld from the refund?

There is no universal rate. Ask NPS for its calculation and withholding receipt.

### Is the USA eligible?

Yes, the United States is on the agreement list. If you have coverage in both countries, check what a refund would do to your totalization record first.

### What is the deadline to claim?

Generally five years after the departure-based right to payment arises. Ask NPS to confirm the date instead of using your last contribution date by default.

### Can I claim without visiting an NPS office in Korea?

Yes. From abroad, you can mail the required documents or have a representative file for you. In some countries, the local social insurance institution can help with filing.

### What if my employer never paid into NPS on my behalf?

Ask NPS for your official coverage and payment record. If it does not match your payslips, raise the discrepancy before estimating your refund.
