A Korean number is no guarantee that a given app will accept it.

Check that your phone is unlocked and eSIM-compatible. Ask when the plan starts, when you must activate it, how you can extend it and whether the carrier will reissue the QR. Keep the profile installed while troubleshooting unless instructed otherwise by the carrier.

## If You Need Resident Phone Verification

Getting a text code to verify possession of a number is different from **본인인증**, where a service checks your identity against your line's registration. The carrier's passport and entry check for a visitor SIM does not make it a resident line.

1. If your status requires it, make a foreign-resident registration appointment through [HiKorea](https://www.hikorea.go.kr/). Confirm Form 34 and the proof-of-address, photo and visa-specific documents with your immigration office. Do not book around a guaranteed issue date.
2. When you have your Residence Card, ask the carrier about an eligible plan in your exact registered name. If you want to keep a visitor number, ask before buying a new line. A [Mobile Residence Card](https://www.moj.go.kr/bbs/immigration_eng/229/476646/download.do) is for registered foreign residents; do not assume it can be issued on the day of your first application.
3. Ask the bank which identification, phone verification and account-purpose evidence it accepts through the channel you intend to use. Bring the physical card to a branch if acceptance of mobile ID has not been confirmed.

[KakaoBank's onboarding guide](https://blog.kakaobank.com/posts/foreign-account-opening) requires an eligible postpaid line in the applicant's name when opening an account as a foreign resident. That is a KakaoBank requirement, not a blanket rule that no prepaid line ever supports any form of identity verification. A branch may use a different process; ask the branch in advance. Our [KakaoBank and Shinhan comparison](/en/blog/tips/korea-first-bank-account-kakaobank) explains the two app flows.

## Visiting for One Month?

You may be fine with a visitor eSIM, your own payment card and T-money or EZL for transit. If you need a local prepaid spending card, compare [WOWPASS and T-money](/en/blog/tips/korea-wowpass-vs-tmoney-vs-cashbee-2026).
