The [WOWPASS issuer's guide](https://wowpass.io/en-US/guide) explains where each balance works. Insert the card at the register for shopping and tap at the transit gate. Some compatible NFC phones support a [transfer to T-money in the WOWPASS app](https://wowpass-event.oopy.io/8e7d0b2f-b0ec-41da-bec3-383d89f4345e). Otherwise, top up transit separately with won. Do not count on a WOWPASS kiosk taking a foreign bank card to load either balance.

## Landing Without Won?

At the AREX stations for Incheon Airport **T1 and T2**, [the operator's starter card](https://www.arex.or.kr/contentView.do?contentNo=CT202611230000000001&etcNo=&menuNo=MN202602270000000001) costs **₩15,000: ₩5,000 for the T-money card and ₩10,000 for rides**. You can pay for this purchase with a foreign credit card. Tap on the all-stop airport train; buy a separate ticket for the Express. This is a specific starter-card purchase, not a promise that other machines accept foreign cards for reloads. [Designated new Seoul Metro machines](https://global.seoul.go.kr/web/news/senw/bordContDetail.do?brd_no=5&lang=EN&mode=W&post_no=4D43379888720160E063C0A8A0232A9B) also accept foreign cards for single tickets and visitor Climate Card passes, which are different products.

For WOWPASS, check the issuer's pickup instructions if reserving an airport package. For walk-up issuance use a listed, supported kiosk and have your passport ready. Use the [issuer's kiosk directory](https://www.wowexchange.net/exchange/location/list?lang=en_US), not an old description of a convenience store near the gate. See the [Korea Tourism Organization transport-card guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=140663) for alternatives.
**Apple Pay and transit:** These are three different checks. At a shop, ask if the terminal takes contactless payments for your card network; carry a backup. To top up a Tmoney card added directly in Apple Wallet, [Apple requires an eligible Korea-issued funding card](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105079). For visitors, the separate [MobileTmoney app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mobiletmoney/id1470361790) has a **Foreign** flow without signup and lists Apple Pay top-ups with Mastercard, American Express, UnionPay and JCB. Check your card in the app or use a physical transit card. Neither Tmoney setup is the visitor Climate Card.

## What to Do With Leftover Balance

- **WOWPASS spending:** check fees and limits, verify your card in the app, then withdraw won at a supported WOWPASS kiosk.
- **WOWPASS transit:** use the T-money refund route, not the WOWPASS cash-withdrawal menu.
- **Physical T-money or EZL:** ask a participating store about small-balance refunds. For larger balances or an unfamiliar card, check the issuer's limits and where to go before your last day.

If your own card pays for meals, you do not need to switch just because you are staying two weeks. Get the transit card you can conveniently reload, and check its balance the night before an early flight.
