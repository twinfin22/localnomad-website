## Before You Fly

Check the [Korea Visa Portal](https://www.visa.go.kr/) for your passport and activity. Visa-free eligibility and the permitted stay are nationality-specific. For visa-free passengers who need K-ETA, get it before travel. The [temporary K-ETA exemption](https://www.k-eta.go.kr/portal/board/viewboarddetail.do?bbsSn=332884) for covered passports extends through December 31, 2027 (Korea time). If you need an [e-Arrival Card](https://www.e-arrivalcard.go.kr/portal/guide/eacGuide.do?locale=EN), it is free and opens three days before arrival. A valid K-ETA normally exempts you from it. If you plan to work, check the [F-1-D and visitor comparison](/en/blog/comparisons/korea-f1d-workation-visa-do-you-need-it-2026) before assuming your entry route allows it.

- Save your accommodation's Korean address, phone number and check-in instructions offline. A station exit number helps on foot; a full street address is better for a taxi.
- Download Naver Map for directions and Papago for translations. Keep a screenshot of your route in case your phone is offline at the terminal.
- Decide whether you need data only, local calls/texts or resident identity verification. Those are different products. See our [SIM and banking guide](/en/blog/tips/korea-arc-phone-bank-catch-22-guide-2026).

## At Incheon: Data and Transport

Use your pre-purchased eSIM or visit a carrier counter. If you need outgoing calls, ask for a plan that includes them and complete the carrier's entry check. Test data and save your QR before leaving.

## The First Day: Settle In

Try your own card at a staffed retailer and keep a small cash backup. If you choose WOWPASS, check its **spending balance** and **T-money balance** separately before riding. See the [full card comparison](/en/blog/tips/korea-wowpass-vs-tmoney-vs-cashbee-2026).

Check the nearest station entrance and convenience store, then confirm your accommodation's Wi-Fi and late check-in arrangement. For a late arrival or large luggage, compare the price of a taxi with the last train and the walk from the station.

## If You're Moving, Not Just Visiting

For a stay requiring foreign-resident registration, check the documents and deadline for your status at [HiKorea](https://www.hikorea.go.kr/). Generally, foreign nationals staying more than 90 days must [register within 90 days of entry](https://www.immigration.go.kr/bbs/immigration/47/447103/download.do). Book the appointment early and confirm the address document your host can supply. Ask your chosen bank and carrier which identification and phone plans they accept; a tourist 010 number is not the same as resident phone verification.
