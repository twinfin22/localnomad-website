The Ministry of Justice's [F-1-D rules](https://www.immigration.go.kr/bbs/immigration/214/608294/artclView.do) use **1–2× the previous year's Korean per-capita GNI**, not a single ₩88 million cutoff. They also require qualifying overseas work and insurance of at least ₩100 million. Eligible visitors in Korea may apply to change status; approval is not automatic. For example, the [Korean Embassy in Singapore](https://overseas.mofa.go.kr/sg-en/brd/m_2444/view.do?seq=761493) lists a 2026 GNI base of ₩52,416,000: ₩52,416,000 at 1×, ₩78,624,000 at 1.5× and ₩104,832,000 at 2×. Confirm the applicable figure and region with your mission. Read our [visa-route comparison](/en/blog/comparisons/korea-f1d-workation-visa-do-you-need-it-2026) and confirm with the appropriate mission.

## A Worked Budget for One Month in Seoul

Use these numbers to build a budget for your dates. The room, food, workspace and deposit are examples, not market averages.

| Expense | Assumption | 30 days |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Room | Example fee-inclusive quote | ₩1,500,000 |
| Meals and groceries | ₩20,000 × 30 | ₩600,000 |
| Subway | 40 × ₩1,550 adult base fare | ₩62,000 |
| Mobile data | KT 30-day tourist data plan | ₩71,500 |
| Workspace | 20 café sessions × ₩5,000 | ₩100,000 |
| **Spending total** | Excludes deposit, flights, insurance and taxis | **₩2,333,500** |
| Example refundable deposit | Track separately; check the refund terms | ₩500,000 |
| **Cash to budget for the month** | Spending + deposit | **₩2,833,500** |

The full amount may not be due on day one. Ask which charges are due when you book, when you move in and when you leave. For nightly rates, compare checkout totals, not the first price in search results. For a lease, ask about rent, management fees, utilities, cleaning and move-out terms. Add distance supplements, taxis or coworking if you will use them. Sources: [Seoul subway fares](https://english.seoul.go.kr/policy/transportation/modes-of-transport/subway/) and [KT's published rate sheet](https://roaming.kt.com/download/esim_manual_jpn.pdf).

## First Week in Korea

Save your address in Korean and sort out a visitor eSIM and transport before worrying about a bank account. If your status requires foreign-resident registration, book through [HiKorea](https://www.hikorea.go.kr/) and confirm that the host can supply the right address paperwork. Check with your chosen bank and carrier about IDs, name matching and plans; a tourist 010 number does not guarantee bank verification.

See the [arrival checklist](/en/blog/tips/korea-arrival-checklist-2026) and [phone and bank guide](/en/blog/tips/korea-arc-phone-bank-catch-22-guide-2026).

## Money & Payments

Travel with your usual payment card and a cash backup. A physical T-money or EZL card can cover most daily rides. If you want a local prepaid spending card as well, WOWPASS combines two separate balances for shopping and transit. See the [card comparison](/en/blog/tips/korea-wowpass-vs-tmoney-vs-cashbee-2026).

Seoul ended reloads of the old 30-day prepaid Climate Card on August 31, 2026. Visitors can compare the [1-, 2-, 3-, 5- and 7-day passes](https://english.seoul.go.kr/policy/transportation/climate-card/) with pay-as-you-go fares for covered routes. The replacement Climate Pass has separate Seoul residency and registration requirements. The short-term card cannot be used to **board** AREX at Incheon Airport. It costs ₩5,000, ₩8,000, ₩10,000, ₩15,000 or ₩20,000 for 1, 2, 3, 5 or 7 days, respectively. Airport buses, GTX and the Shinbundang Line are excluded. [Designated new machines on Seoul Metro Lines 1–8](https://global.seoul.go.kr/web/news/senw/bordContDetail.do?brd_no=5&lang=EN&mode=W&post_no=4D43379888720160E063C0A8A0232A9B) accept foreign cards for single tickets and visitor passes; that is different from tapping a foreign bank card at the gate.
**Apple Pay and transit:** These are three different checks. At a shop, ask if the terminal takes contactless payments for your card network; carry a backup. To top up a Tmoney card added directly in Apple Wallet, [Apple requires an eligible Korea-issued funding card](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105079). For visitors, the separate [MobileTmoney app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mobiletmoney/id1470361790) has a **Foreign** flow without signup and lists Apple Pay top-ups with Mastercard, American Express, UnionPay and JCB. Check your card in the app or use a physical transit card. Neither Tmoney setup is the visitor Climate Card.

## Housing Reality

For a month in Seoul, price three things: a serviced room, a sharehouse room and a goshiwon with the facilities you need. Do not assume that a deposit is refundable without checking the contract. Longer leases may use **jeonse**, a large lump-sum deposit, or **wolse**, monthly rent plus a deposit. Neither tells you what a furnished short stay will cost.

Hongdae and Yeonnam are worth comparing if you want cafés and nightlife. Look near Sinchon if you want to stay around the university area, or near Gangnam if that is where your meetings are. Compare the actual door-to-door commute before paying. See our [Seoul housing guide](/en/blog/guides/coliving-seoul-guide-2026) for minimum stays, fees and bathroom checks.

## Healthcare & Tax

Check the medical and repatriation coverage your status requires. For longer stays, confirm National Health Insurance eligibility, dates and premiums with [NHIS](https://www.nhis.or.kr/nhis/foreigners/index.do) instead of assuming all visa holders get the same coverage on the same date.

The 183-day rule is not a universal tax-free allowance. Residence, where income arises, treaty rules and the treatment of foreign-source income can matter. Consult Korea's [National Tax Service](https://www.nts.go.kr/english/main.do) or a qualified tax adviser before planning a stay around a day count. Our [tax guide](/en/blog/tax/183-day-tax-trap-digital-nomads) explains what to ask about.

## Coworking & Cafe Culture

If you spend the day on calls, ask a coworking operator whether booths are included, whether booking is required and how early the space opens. If you just need a desk for a few hours, compare a day pass or study café with your usual café spend. Check each venue's laptop and seating policy.
