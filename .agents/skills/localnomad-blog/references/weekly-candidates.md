# Weekly candidate brief

Produce a short Korean report of candidate topics, not articles. For each candidate record: proposed English title, actual schema-compatible category/country/tags, likely reader question, why it is timely or due for a refresh, evidence from performance/questions/existing coverage, overlap risk, source links, and missing research.

Include a mix only when the evidence supports it. If search-volume data is unavailable, say that it was not measured. Separate a confirmed update trigger from an editorial idea.
