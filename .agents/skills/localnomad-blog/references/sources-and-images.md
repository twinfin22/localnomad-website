# Sources and images

Prefer the primary authority for a claim: issuing government agency, official program or payment provider, carrier/property/workspace operator, or original price page. Clearly identify information that is venue-specific, country-specific, time-bounded, or unverified.

For legal, immigration, tax, insurance, finance, safety, and payment claims, avoid eligibility guarantees and workaround advice. State the scope and direct readers to the responsible authority for confirmation.

For a new cover, provide candidates with the image URL, creator/licence or use terms, source page, and a short relevance note. Do not download, purchase, or alter external media without approval. Retain existing covers unless a replacement is requested.
