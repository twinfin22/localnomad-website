# Writing rules

Write for a foreign visitor or remote worker making a concrete decision. Give the article one role: procedural guide, comparison, troubleshooting path, or place-based planning aid. Do not invent a personal experience, test result, price, availability, or support-flow screen.

Use tables only when they make a decision easier. Explain limitations and the next verification point alongside a recommendation. Do not force FAQs, a fixed length, anecdotes, or keyword density. Link internally when it is directly useful and do not claim a search-volume result without a source.

For translations, synchronize conditions, exceptions, units, dates, and source scope before stylistic edits. Preserve the translation's existing voice unless the user asks for a rewrite.
