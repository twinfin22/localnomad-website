Add a new B2B landing page to the existing LocalNomad website.

Context:
- This page targets businesses and global teams exploring or entering the Korean market.
- It is NOT for tourists or individual nomads.
- The tone should be calm, confident, and execution-oriented (no hype, no community language).

Route:
- Create a new page at /business (or /b2b if /business conflicts).
- Use the existing site layout, header, footer, typography, color tokens, and button styles.

Page Structure (MANDATORY):

1) Hero Section
- Headline:
  "Korea Market Entry, Explained and Represented on the Ground."
- Subheadline:
  "We help global teams explore, test, and enter Korea — by thinking clearly, speaking publicly, and executing locally."
- Primary CTA: "Start with Discovery"
- Secondary CTA: "See How We Work"
- CTAs should link to the contact or intro-call flow already used on the site.

2) Problem Section
- Headline:
  "Entering Korea fails when no one can explain you properly."
- Content should explain:
  - Decks without speakers
  - Research without real-world answers
  - Meetings without local context
  - Events without proper representation

3) Why Us Section
- Headline:
  "What We Actually Do"
- Three pillars:
  - We clarify your story
  - We speak on your behalf
  - We turn reactions into strategy
- Focus on public speaking, representation, and real-time feedback.
- Do NOT mention community building.

4) Service Section — Three Offers

A) Discovery
- Position as the first step.
- Focus on:
  - Market reality check
  - Messaging gaps
  - Go / No-Go clarity
- Outcome-driven, not execution-heavy.

B) Entry Sprint (2–4 weeks)
- Core offer.
- Emphasize:
  - Representing the team at events or sessions
  - Public speaking and facilitated discussions
  - Hosting or co-hosting focused events
  - Collecting real audience reactions
- Deliverables:
  - Summary of reactions
  - Objections and signals
  - Strategic recommendations

C) Execution Partner (Short-term)
- Extension after Entry Sprint.
- Emphasize:
  - Temporary, scoped, on-the-ground representation
  - Meetings, events, coordination
- Explicit boundary:
  - Not a long-term agency
  - Not outsourcing
  - Clear scope and timeline only

5) How It Works
- 4 steps:
  1. Intro call
  2. Discovery
  3. Entry Sprint or Execution Partner
  4. Clear wrap-up and next decision

6) Who This Is Not For
- Explicitly list:
  - Legal/accounting services
  - Guaranteed outcomes
  - Long-term outsourced operations
- This section is required for lead quality.

7) Final CTA Section
- Copy:
  "If Korea is on your roadmap, let’s start with a clear conversation."
- CTA:
  "Start with Discovery"

Implementation Requirements:
- Use existing components where possible.
- Create new components only if necessary (e.g. BusinessHeroSection).
- Ensure full responsiveness (desktop/mobile).
- Ensure Light/Dark mode compatibility.
- Keep spacing, button styles, and typography consistent with the rest of the site.
- No emojis.
- No casual or marketing-heavy language.

Deliverables:
- New B2B page fully integrated into the site.
- Route added and accessible.
- Clean, readable component structure.
- List all new or modified files at the end.
