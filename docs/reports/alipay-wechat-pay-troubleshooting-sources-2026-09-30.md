## Editorial decisions

- The draft distinguishes merchant payments from person-to-person transfers, red packets, and wallet funding. The reviewed visitor sources document the former but do not promise the latter for overseas-card users.
- The draft does not state a fixed acceptance rate, verification time, fee outcome, app menu path, or card-network list beyond the cited source. It directs readers to the final in-app and issuer terms.
- No image is assigned to the draft. A future cover-image candidate must have separately verified licence, attribution, and usage terms before it is added.
