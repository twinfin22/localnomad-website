## National rule versus local submission requirement

The common fact table is the nationwide rule. The Embassy in the Philippines additionally requests a detailed original employment certificate and business-registration evidence; the Thailand notice lists a current bank statement and may require a tuberculosis certificate. These are examples of jurisdictional submission requirements, not additions generalized to every applicant.

## Deliberately not stated as fact

- A universal F-1-D processing time or fee.
- Automatic National Health Insurance eligibility.
- Permission for visitor-status remote work, Korean-client freelancing, Korean business registration, or a particular independent-contracting arrangement.
- Tax outcome or public-insurance outcome for a specific person.

These require confirmation from the jurisdictional mission / immigration office (1345 inside Korea) and, where relevant, a qualified tax or insurance adviser.
