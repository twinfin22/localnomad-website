# Cycle 1 Audit: User Perspective (Linh)

**Persona**: Linh, 29, Vietnamese, E-7-1 visa holder, 4 months until expiry, TOPIK 3
**Date**: 2026-02-12
**Auditor role**: Real user walking through the site looking for help with my specific situation

---

## 1. Home Page (`app/page.tsx` -> `app/[lang]/page.tsx` -> `app/[lang]/[country]/page.tsx`)

### What I see

The root `app/page.tsx` (line 11) redirects to `/{defaultLocale}`, which is the global landing page at `app/[lang]/page.tsx`. This page shows country cards (Korea, Taiwan) with service listings. Korea shows Visa Guide, Area Guide, and Info Bundles as available. Clicking Korea takes me to `app/[lang]/[country]/page.tsx` -- the country hub -- which has three service cards linking to `/visa`, `/areas`, and `/bundles`.

### Does it feel welcoming?

**Partially.** The flow is clean and the i18n structure works. But:

- **No Vietnamese language option.** The site has en, ja, zh-tw (from `messages/` directory) but not vi. For someone with TOPIK 3, I can read intermediate Korean and basic English, but a Vietnamese option would significantly reduce friction. There is no indication Vietnamese is coming.
- The home page headline (`messages/en.json` line 38): "Your Toolkit for Living in Asia" -- this is neutral and honest. Good.
- The subheadline (`messages/en.json` line 39): "Navigate visas, find neighborhoods, and settle in with confidence." -- "with confidence" is a soft promise but not misleading.

### Marketing claims to flag

**File**: `components/sections/why-section.tsx` (lines 9-11, 23-25)

1. **"Verified Information"** with description "No more outdated blog posts or AI hallucinations. Every guide is personally researched and regularly updated by locals who actually live here."
   - **Concern**: The word "Verified" implies official validation. "Personally researched" claims direct authorial effort for every guide. If the visa data was partly generated by AI (which it appears to be based on the structured JSON format), this claim is misleading. The Terms of Service page (`app/(legal)/terms/page.tsx` line 130) even hedges on "human-verified" with quotes, suggesting the team knows this is a stretch.

2. **"Save 40+ Hours"** -- unsubstantiated quantitative claim. Where does this number come from? This appears in both `why-section.tsx` line 23 and `components/sections/faq-section.tsx` line 20.

3. **`messages/en.json` line 268**: Same "AI hallucinations" claim in all three language files.

**Verdict**: The home page is clean in structure but the "Why" section has marketing claims that could erode trust for someone who finds an error in the visa data later.

**LegalDisclaimer** at line 214 is present (`variant="box"` with "Important Notice" heading). Links to immigration.go.kr and HiKorea. Good.

## 3. E-7 Journey Page & Data (`data/visas/en/e-7.json`, `components/visa/journey/VisaJourneyPage.tsx`)

### Does it help with employer changes?

**Minimally.** The FAQ in `e-7.json` lines 178-181:
> "Can I change jobs on an E-7 visa?" -> "Yes, but you must report the change to immigration and get your visa updated with the new employer as sponsor. Don't start working for the new company until this is complete."

This is correct but **dangerously incomplete** for my situation:
- **No mention of the 고용변동신고 (Employment Change Report)** that the current employer must file
- **No mention of the 근로계약서 (Employment Contract)** from the new employer that must be submitted
- **No timeline** -- how many days do I have? (It's 15 days to report to immigration)
- **No mention of whether I can change during extension** -- I have 4 months left, can I change employers AND extend at the same time?
- **No mention of the 직종 (occupation category)** constraint -- E-7-1 holders can only change to jobs within their approved occupation category

### Korean document names

**Completely absent.** The document list in `e-7.json` lines 64-115 uses only English names:
- "Employment Contract" -- should also show 고용계약서 / 근로계약서
- "Company Business Registration" -- should also show 사업자등록증명서
- "Career Certificate / Employment History" -- should also show 경력증명서
- "Tax Payment Certificates" -- should also show 납세증명서

**This is a critical gap.** When I go to the immigration office (출입국관리사무소), the staff will use Korean names. When my HR department prepares documents, they use Korean forms. Without Korean names, I cannot cross-reference what the site tells me with what I actually need to bring.

### After Approval section

`components/visa/journey/steps/StepAfterApproval.tsx` (lines 24-34) filters warnings for holder-relevant terms (change, address, tax, extension, report, renew). For E-7, the warnings from data are:
- "Working without proper visa is grounds for deportation"
- "Don't start working until visa is approved"
- "Changing jobs without updating visa is a violation"

The third one matches the filter (contains "change"). The first two match "work"/"deport" but are filtered by StepApply, not StepAfterApproval. So only the job change warning appears in After Approval. **This is insufficient** -- there is no mention of address change reporting (14 days), no mention of the 2-month-before-expiry rule for extensions.

### Extension & Renewal section

Lines 90-119 show extension period, max stay, and fee from the data. The E-7 data says:
- Extension: "1-3 years per extension"
- Max: "No maximum (renewable indefinitely while employed)"
- Fee: "60,000 per extension"

**Missing**: When to start the extension process (the tips mention "2 months before" but tips are not surfaced in After Approval -- they are filtered by eligibility-related keywords in StepQualify).

The tip "Start extension process 2 months before visa expires" (e-7.json line 204) is matched by `StepQualify.tsx` line 27-34 which looks for salary/company/sponsor/employer/negotiate keywords. It does NOT match, so this critical tip is effectively **invisible** in the journey UI.

## 5. Dashboard (`components/visa/dashboard/DashboardClient.tsx`)

### Can I advance state?

**No.** The dashboard is **read-only from Supabase**. Looking at `DashboardClient.tsx`:
- Lines 389-394: Fetches `visa_progress` from Supabase with `.select('*').eq('user_id', user.id).single()`
- Lines 417-421: Fetches `checklist_items` from Supabase
- **There is no mutation function.** No `update`, no `insert`, no way to change state from PREPARING to SUBMITTED, from SUBMITTED to UNDER_REVIEW, etc.
- The `NextActionCard.tsx` shows state-appropriate guidance (lines 39-142) but no button or mechanism to actually advance the state.

**This means**: I sign up, get a dashboard, but I am permanently stuck in whatever state the database has. If I start with NO_VISA/PREPARING, there is no way to mark myself as SUBMITTED. The entire state machine is theoretical.

### Auth wall still there?

**Yes.** `DashboardClient.tsx` lines 472-497: If `!user`, shows "Sign in to continue" with Sign In / Create Account buttons linking to `/auth/login` and `/auth/signup`. The dashboard is completely gated behind authentication.

**However**: The EmptyState (lines 46-109) for logged-in users with no progress is well-designed. It offers "Find My Visa" and "Browse All Visas" with quick-access cards for E-7 and F-1-D. This is a reasonable empty state.

### Dashboard disclaimer

Lines 278-284: "This dashboard tracks your self-reported progress. It is not connected to HiKorea or any government system." -- **Excellent disclaimer.** Clear, honest, visible.

## 6. Document Checklist & Export (`components/visa/DocumentChecklist.tsx`)

### Does export have disclaimer?

**No.** Lines 226-256 define the export function. The exported text file contains:
```
${visa.name} (${visa.shortName}) - Document Checklist
Generated: ${date}

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
[X] Document Name
...
```

**No disclaimer is included in the exported file.** A user could print this, bring it to immigration, and treat it as an authoritative checklist. If a document is missing from the data, the user has no indication that the list may be incomplete.

The on-screen disclaimer (`LegalDisclaimer`) is present on the journey page but NOT on the checklist page itself. Looking at the checklist page component, there is no `<LegalDisclaimer />` rendered within `DocumentChecklist.tsx`.

### Checklist quality for E-7

The E-7 documents in `e-7.json` lines 64-115:
- Passport -- required
- Visa Application Form -- required
- Passport Photo -- required
- Employment Contract -- required
- Company Business Registration -- required
- Degree Certificate -- required
- Career Certificate -- required
- Tax Payment Certificates -- optional (for extensions)

**Missing for E-7 renewal/extension** (my situation with 4 months left):
- 외국인등록증 (Alien Registration Card / ARC)
- 재직증명서 (Certificate of Employment from current employer)
- 소득금액증명원 (Income Certificate from NTS)
- 건강보험 납부확인서 (Health Insurance Payment Certificate)
- 체류지 입증서류 (Proof of Residence)

The checklist only covers initial application documents, not extension documents. For someone like me who needs to renew, this is a major gap.

## Total Score: 59/100

### Summary

The site has solid bones -- the journey flow, legal disclaimers, and quiz language are well-executed. But for someone in my specific situation (E-7 holder, Vietnamese, 4 months from expiry, TOPIK 3, considering employer change), the gaps are significant:

1. **I cannot find my exact situation** -- no "changing employers" or "visa expiring soon" entry point
2. **I cannot read Korean document names** -- making the checklist useless at the actual immigration office
3. **I am invisible in the nationality list** -- Vietnamese workers are a huge population but not listed
4. **The dashboard is a dead end** -- I sign up, see a nice UI, but cannot advance my status
5. **The export gives me a false sense of completeness** -- no disclaimer, no Korean names, no extension documents

The biggest risk is that I trust this site, prepare based on its checklist, go to immigration, and discover I am missing documents or have the wrong document names. The disclaimers exist but the UX flow creates an authority that the disclaimers try to disclaim.
