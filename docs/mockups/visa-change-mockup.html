E-7 → F-2: Change from Professional Employment to Residency

One of the most common in-country status changes for long-term foreign professionals in Korea.

The points-based F-2 track uses a scoring system covering income level, Korean language ability, education, and community contributions. The exact threshold and scoring weights are set by Korea Immigration Service and may be updated.
Job changes during the review period may complicate your application. Inform immigration if your employment situation changes after submission.
F-2 visa holders can work in most occupations without employer sponsorship. That's a big upgrade from E-7, which ties you to a specific employer and role.
Document requirements may differ between immigration offices. Confirm the checklist directly with HiKorea (hikorea.go.kr) or the office you plan to visit.

E-7 → F-2 is commonly step 2 of a longer residency pathway. Many foreign professionals follow this sequence:

H-1→ E-7→ F-2→ F-5 (Permanent Residence)

F-5 (Permanent Residence) generally requires 5+ years of legal stay with a qualifying status. F-2 is one of the recognized prior statuses. This is a long-term planning consideration, not a guaranteed sequence.
