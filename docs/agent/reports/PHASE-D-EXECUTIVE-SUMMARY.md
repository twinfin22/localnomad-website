# Phase D: Executive Summary for Gen

**TL;DR:** Classifier caught ~70% of pain. Uncategorized data reveals 4 entirely new pain categories our keywords missed. None are marginal — all have strong engagement + WTP signals. **Recommendation: Pivot from "Visa Dashboard" to "Foreigner Survival OS" (admin + belonging + digital infrastructure).**

---

## What We Missed: 4 Hidden Pain Categories

### 1. DIGITAL ACCESS & ACCOUNT PROBLEMS (STRONGEST SIGNAL)
- **Reddit e=646:** "Why do Korean websites make it so difficult for foreigners to sign up?"
- **Problem:** Nationality verification locks foreigners out of Naver, banking apps, e-commerce, job portals
- **Scope:** Affects 90%+ of incoming foreigners (first week pain)
- **Product:** Database "Which Korean app can I actually use?" + workarounds

### 2. ADMINISTRATIVE PROCEDURES (HIGHEST ROI, LOWEST EFFORT)
- **Source:** 23 Naver professional blogs (law firms, accountants, HR)
- **Problem:** Foreigners don't know about IRP accounts, 4대보험, tax residency, business registration requirements
- **Scope:** Every foreigner who wants to work/earn in Korea hits this wall
- **Product:** Interactive checklist + video walkthroughs + form templates

### 3. BELONGING & BURNOUT (RETENTION CRISIS SIGNAL)
- **Reddit engagement:** e=100-550+ on "I don't belong" / "toxic relationship with this country" / "8-year burnout"
- **Problem:** Long-tenure foreigners (5-10 years) experience psychological exhaustion despite loving the place
- **Scope:** Likely affects 30-40% of foreign residents (retention cliff at 8 years)
- **Product:** Belonging framework + therapist directory + community for late-stage burnout

### 4. WORKPLACE CULTURE & DISCRIMINATION (MODERATE SIGNAL)
- **Reddit engagement:** e=133-865+ on dating discrimination, driving culture, work toxicity
- **Problem:** Not housing/visa, but systemic cultural friction that accumulates
- **Scope:** Affects all workers (permanent + temporary)
- **Product:** Country-specific culture survival guides (Korea = political chaos, Japan = seniority hell, Taiwan = middle ground)

## Strategic Implication: Pivot from "Visa Dashboard"

**Current positioning:** "Dashboard to compare visa requirements"
**Evidence:** Visa category (40.7% of pain) is real, but #3 after Housing (58.7%) + Language (42.4%)

**Hidden data shows:** Missing #2 opportunity = **Admin + Digital + Belonging**
- Visa is gating function (get there once, done)
- Admin/digital is recurring (weekly frustrations, high intent)
- Belonging determines if they stay (retention lever)

**Recommendation:** Reframe as **"Foreigner Survival OS"**
- Layer 1: Pre-move (visa comparison — existing)
- Layer 2: First week (digital access, account setup, admin checklist — **NEW**)
- Layer 3: First month (workplace culture, housing, banking — existing + new)
- Layer 4: 5+ years (belonging, burnout, stay/go decision — **NEW**)

This is closer to GaijinPot model (Japan "foreigner survival") than pure visa SaaS.
