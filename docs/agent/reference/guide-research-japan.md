## SECTION 4: BANKING IN JAPAN

### 4.1 Opening a Bank Account

**Eligibility:**
- Tourist visa holders: Very difficult (no Residence Card issued)
- Digital Nomad Visa holders: Difficult (no Residence Card issued for this visa as of 2025)
- Long-term residents (3+ months with Residence Card): Possible
- *Source: Japan Dev 2025, E-Housing 2025*

### 4.2 Banks That Accept Foreigners

#### **SBI Shinsei Bank (Online-focused)**
- **English support:** 24/7 foreign currency exchange
- **Services:** Yen deposit accounts, foreign currency deposits, loans, cards
- **Requirement:** Proof of employment or 6-month stay
- **Note:** SBI Shinsei GoRemit (international transfer service) was discontinued October 20, 2025; now directs to SBI Remit Co., Ltd.
- *Source: Western Union, MailMate, Japan Dev 2025*

#### **Japan Post Bank (Most Accessible)**
- **Easiest for new residents or tourists**
- **Network:** One of the largest ATM networks in Japan (post office locations nationwide)
- **Services:** Basic banking, simpler online access
- **Advantage:** Eased application process for foreigners
- *Source: MATCHA, Japan Handbook 2025*

#### **Rakuten Bank (Online-friendly)**
- **English-friendly, app-based**
- **Good for: Residents with long-term stay*
- *Source: Japan Dev 2025*

#### **Sony Bank (Online-friendly)**
- **Note:** New account applications via English flow ended in 2025
- **For: Existing customers only going forward*
- *Source: MailMate 2025*

#### **Other Options**
- Mizuho Bank
- MUFG Bank
- Regional banks (require residence card)

### 4.3 Remittance Options (2025-2026)

#### **Wise (Recommended for Transparency)**
- **Best for:** Foreign currency transfers
- **Features:** Transparent fees, mid-market rates, no foreign exchange markup
- **Methods:** Bank transfer or debit/credit card
- **Speed:** Seconds if using funds already in Wise account; slower via bank transfer
- **Limits for Japan residents:** Up to ¥150,000,000 equivalent (Large Amount transfer)
- **Note:** Wise serves 16 million users globally as of 2025
- *Source: Wise 2025*

#### **SBI Remit Co., Ltd. (New Default)**
- **Note:** SBI Shinsei GoRemit discontinued October 20, 2025
- **Use:** International transfers from Japan
- *Source: MailMate 2025*

#### **Bank Direct Transfer**
- **Cost:** Cheap but slower
- **Speed:** 1-3 business days internationally
- *Source: E-Housing 2025*

### 4.4 Cashless Payments in Japan

**Primary Methods:**
- IC cards (Suica, PASMO, Icoca)
- Credit cards (widespread acceptance in major cities)
- Mobile payments (PayPay, Rakuten Pay, LINE Pay)
- Cash still important in rural areas and smaller shops
- *Source: Japan Travel 2025*

## SECTION 7: HEALTHCARE

### 7.1 National Health Insurance (NHI) Enrollment

**Eligibility:**
- Every person staying 3+ months must enroll (no exceptions by nationality)
- If not covered by employer's Shakai Hoken, you must join NHI

**Enrollment Process:**
- **Timeline:** Within 14 days of registering address with municipality
- **Location:** Local municipal office
- **Required documents:**
  - Residence Card
  - Passport
  - Proof of address (lease, juminhyo/residence certificate)
  - My Number Card (if issued)

**Cost (2025-2026):**
- Based on previous year's income
- Example: ~¥400,000/year at ¥4M income; ~¥940,000/year at ¥8M income
- Coverage: 70% of outpatient and inpatient costs
- *Source: E-Housing 2025, Kantenna 2025*

### 7.2 Recent Changes (Critical for 2026-2027)

#### **My Number Card Integration (December 2025)**
- Physical NHI cards being phased out
- **My Number Card now acts as health insurance card** at clinics and hospitals
- *Source: Kantenna 2025*

#### **Prepayment Policy (New in 2026)**
- Local governments now permitted to require foreign residents to prepay NHI premiums
- Goal: Reduce unpaid bills
- Adoption varies by municipality (starting as early as April 2026)
- *Source: Unseen Japan, Kantenna 2025*

#### **Visa Renewal Compliance (June 2027)**
- **CRITICAL:** Foreign residents with unpaid NHI premiums or National Pension contributions will be denied visa renewal/status changes (starting June 2027)
- Formally announced November 4, 2025; adopted January 23, 2026
- *Source: Kantenna 2025*

### 7.3 Healthcare Providers with English

**Hospitals with English service:**
- Tokyo: Many international clinics, major hospitals (Roppongi, Shibuya areas)
- Osaka: Growing English-friendly options
- Fukuoka: Increasing English services for expats
- *Note: Specific hospital names require current 2026 verification*

### 7.4 Private Health Insurance Alternative

**For Digital Nomad Visa Holders:**
- Digital Nomad Visa requires proof of health insurance covering minimum ¥10,000,000
- International private insurance options available
- Must cover medical treatment, injury, death
- *Source: MOFA Japan, multiple visa guides*

## SECTION 9: TAX IMPLICATIONS

### 9.1 Digital Nomad Visa Tax Treatment

**Key Rule:**
- **No Japanese income tax liability** while on digital nomad visa
- Condition: Income earned from overseas sources only
- Rationale: 6-month stay is under 183-day threshold; also you're not a Japanese tax resident
- *Source: Digitalnomadtax.eu 2025, Rippling 2025, Wise 2025*

### 9.2 Tax Residency

**How to become tax resident (avoid this on DN visa):**
- Must live in Japan for 1+ year continuously
- Digital nomad visa only lasts 6 months (non-renewable)
- After visa expires, must leave for 6 months before reapplying
- *Source: Multiple guides 2025*

### 9.3 Other Taxes to Consider

**Consumption Tax:**
- Japan has 10% consumption tax (similar to VAT)
- Applied to goods and services
- *Source: Tax guides 2025*

**Municipal Tax:**
- Applies if you become a resident (3+ months with Residence Card)
- Does NOT apply to Digital Nomad Visa holders with proper income sourcing
- *Source: Oyster HR 2025*

### 9.4 Professional Recommendation

- Consult tax professional regarding your specific situation
- Tax treaties between countries can be complicated
- Keep documentation of income sources
- *Source: All tax guides 2025*

## SECTION 10: DIGITAL NOMAD VISA SPECIFICS

### 10.1 Official Requirements (2025)

**Income Requirement:**
- Minimum annual income: ¥10,000,000 (~USD 66,400 / EUR 62,000)
- Source: Employment or self-employment both qualify
- Must be from overseas sources only

**Health Insurance Requirement:**
- Proof of health insurance covering minimum ¥10,000,000
- Must cover: Medical treatment, injury, death
- International private insurance acceptable
- *Source: MOFA Japan, visa guides 2025*

**Passport Requirements:**
- Valid for at least 6 months beyond intended stay
- 1.5+ blank pages
- From one of 49 eligible countries/territories

**Documentation Required:**
- Valid passport
- Certificate of Eligibility (COE) if using COE route
- Passport-style photo (4.5 × 4.5 cm, white background, within 6 months)
- Letter from employer/clients confirming:
  - Remote work arrangement
  - Annual income (¥10,000,000+)
  - Contract duration
- *Source: MOFA Japan, Rippling 2025, Oyster HR 2025*

### 10.2 Eligible Countries/Territories (49 Total)

**Examples:** US, UK, EU member states, Australia, Canada, Singapore, South Korea, Japan, New Zealand, Norway, and others
- *Source: MOFA Japan, Wise 2025*

### 10.3 Visa Limitations (CRITICAL)

**Non-renewable:**
- 6-month maximum stay
- Cannot extend within Japan
- Must leave country for minimum 6 months before reapplying

**No Residence Card:**
- Holders do NOT receive Residence Card
- Impacts:
  - Cannot open standard bank accounts easily
  - Cannot sign up for standard 2-year mobile phone contracts (difficult)
  - No access to resident-only government services
  - *Source: Rippling 2025, E-Housing 2025, Japan Dev 2025*

**Work Restrictions:**
- Can ONLY work for overseas employers/clients
- NO local Japanese employment permitted
- NO business activities with Japanese entities
- *Source: MOFA Japan, all visa guides 2025*

### 10.4 Application Routes

1. **COE Route (Certificate of Eligibility):** Apply through Japanese embassy/consulate in home country
2. **Direct Application:** Apply at port of entry (less common, more complex)
3. **Processing time:** Typically 1-2 weeks for COE; varies by embassy
- *Source: Visa guides 2025*

## SECTION 12: LEGAL GOTCHAS & COMPLIANCE

### 12.1 Working on Tourist Visa (Enforcement)

**Legal status:**
- Tourist visa technically does NOT permit employment or work in Japan
- **However:** Many nomads use tourist visa for remote work with overseas employers
- **Enforcement:** Generally lenient IF:
  - Work is for overseas company only
  - No income from Japan sources
  - Not engaging with Japanese clients locally
- **Risk:** Technically violates visa terms; enforcement rare but possible

**Better option:** Use Digital Nomad Visa instead (official route since April 2024)
- *Source: Multiple guides, MOFA Japan 2025*

### 12.2 Residence Registration & My Number Card

**Residence Registration (Juminhyo):**
- Required within 14 days of moving to an address
- Needed for: Bank accounts, health insurance enrollment, library cards
- Digital Nomad Visa holders: Do NOT get Residence Card, which complicates registration process

**My Number Card (Individual Number Card):**
- **New integration (June 14, 2026):** Tokutei Zairyū Card combines Residence Card + My Number Card into ONE card
- **Current use (2025):** My Number Card now works as health insurance card (as of December 2025)
- Enrollment in National Health Insurance links to My Number Card
- *Source: Kantenna 2025, Trust Administrative Scrivener 2025*

### 12.3 Application ID & Status Tracking

**DO NOT do:**
- Store passport numbers on server
- Store ARC (Alien Registration Certificate) numbers online
- Store application IDs on server
- Scrape government websites (NIA, BOCA) for status

**Keep personal immigration data:**
- Client-side only (localStorage if needed)
- Never transmit to backend
- *Note: Relevant for Taiwan info products; Japan less strict but good practice*

### 12.4 Enforcement Updates (2026)

**New visa renewal compliance (June 2027):**
- Unpaid NHI premiums = visa denial
- Unpaid National Pension = visa denial
- Affects visa renewal and status changes
- *Source: Kantenna 2025, Japanese government announcement January 23, 2026*

## SECTION 13: CULTURAL TIPS & SOCIAL NORMS

### 13.1 Garbage Separation System

**Critical for cohabitation:**
- Improper disposal is #1 cause of neighbor friction
- Each municipality has different rules (no universal standard)
- Common categories: Burnable, non-burnable, recyclable, oversized

**Rules:**
- **Timing:** Only put out garbage early morning on collection day (6-8 AM)

- **Consequence:** Violating rules can disrupt community harmony significantly
- *Source: E-Housing 2025, MATCHA 2025*

- Over 200 earthquakes recorded annually

- Ranges from barely noticeable tremors to large events

**Government systems:**
- **Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA):** Official authority for:
  - Typhoon forecasts
  - Tsunami warnings
  - Earthquake alerts
  - Volcanic activity updates
- **Earthquake Early Warnings (EEW):** Sent via TV, radio, mobile apps
- **Tsunami Warnings:** Immediate alerts to coastal areas

- Strengthened since 2011 Great East Japan Earthquake
- Modern buildings engineered for seismic activity
- Coastal communities: upgraded seawalls, evacuation routes

**Preparedness actions:**
- Regular earthquake drills (common in buildings, neighborhoods)
- Awareness of evacuation routes
- Emergency supplies (water, food, first aid)
- *Source: Japan Travel 2025, Japan Safety Guide 2025*
