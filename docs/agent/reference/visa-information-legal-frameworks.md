# Legal Frameworks for Visa Information Websites
## Research Report: Japan, China, Thailand, Indonesia, Malaysia

**Date:** March 3, 2026
**Context:** LocalNomad is a visa information website (not consulting, not filing applications). This document establishes what information websites CAN and CANNOT do under each country's law.

---

## JAPAN

### Relevant Laws
- **行政書士法 (Gyoseishoshi Law / Administrative Scrivener Act)** — Article 1, Articles 19, 23
- **弁護士法 (Attorney Act)** — Legal services regulation
- **Immigration Control and Refugee Recognition Act (ICRRA)** — Cabinet Order No. 319 (1951)

### Legal Framework for Visa Information Websites

#### What LocalNomad CAN Do
- ✅ Display published visa requirements from official government sources (MOJ, Immigration Bureau)
- ✅ Create visa comparison tables (factual comparisons only, no ranking by "fit")
- ✅ Provide visa application checklists (user self-checks, client-side storage)
- ✅ Show generic visa transition paths (not personalized eligibility)
- ✅ Offer date calculators for residence status periods (arithmetic only)
- ✅ Host educational content about visa types and categories
- ✅ Link to official government portals and application resources

#### What LocalNomad MUST NEVER Do
- ❌ **Provide consulting** — Even if labeled "information," answering "what visa do I need?" or "do I qualify?" constitutes 行政書士 (administrative scrivener) work
- ❌ **File applications** — Cannot submit forms to Immigration Bureau on user's behalf
- ❌ **Determine eligibility** — Cannot say "you are eligible," "you qualify," "recommended visa"
- ❌ **Store government credentials** — Cannot save/cache passport numbers, visa application IDs, or residence card numbers
- ❌ **Auto-fill government forms** — Cannot pre-populate MOJ or Immigration Bureau forms
- ❌ **Create personalized recommendations** — Cannot say "based on your profile, apply for X"
- ❌ **Charge for visa guidance** — Cannot monetize application assistance under any name (fees, memberships, products)

### Penalties for Violations

**Unauthorized 行政書士 Practice (Article 19 violation):**
- **Individuals:** Up to 1 year imprisonment OR fine up to ¥1,000,000
- **Organizations:** Fine up to ¥1,000,000
- **Both penalty rules apply** (個人 + 法人, "both punished rule")

**Note (January 1, 2026):** Amended Gyoseishoshi Law took effect, strengthening enforcement. The amended Article 19 closes loopholes by prohibiting compensation "under any name whatsoever"—including memberships, consulting fees, product fees, or administrative fees.

### Required Disclaimers

Every Japan visa page must display:

> "LocalNomad is an information website only. This content is not legal advice and does not constitute 行政書士 (administrative scrivener) services. For visa eligibility determination or application assistance, consult a licensed 行政書士, immigration lawyer (弁護士), or official government resources at the Immigration Bureau (出入国在留管理庁)."

## CHINA

### Relevant Laws
- **Exit and Entry Administration Law of the People's Republic of China**
- **Exit and Entry Administration Regulations of Foreigners**
- **Rules for the Administration of Employment of Foreigners in China**
- **Administrative Measures for Internet-based Services** (analogous to medical/healthcare online services)

### Legal Framework for Visa Information Websites

#### What LocalNomad CAN Do
- ✅ Display published visa types and categories from National Immigration Administration (NIA)
- ✅ Show visa application requirements (as published on en.nia.gov.cn)
- ✅ Provide visa comparison tables (factual breakdowns only)
- ✅ Link to official NIA forms and government application portals
- ✅ Explain visa processing timelines and general procedures
- ✅ Host educational content about China's visa system

#### What LocalNomad MUST NEVER Do
- ❌ **Provide personalized visa matching** — Cannot tell users "you should apply for X visa"
- ❌ **Answer eligibility questions** — Cannot assess "do I qualify?" based on user profile
- ❌ **File applications** — Cannot submit forms to NIA or local Public Security Bureau
- ❌ **Store sensitive data** — Cannot save passport numbers, application IDs, employment details
- ❌ **Auto-fill government forms** — Cannot pre-populate NIA or PSB applications
- ❌ **Use AI chatbot for visa consulting** — Cannot deploy conversational AI to answer personalized visa questions
- ❌ **Call service "consulting"** — Cannot use term "咨询" (consulting) to describe website features

### Penalties for Violations

**Unlicensed immigration consulting:**
- China does not have a single unified "immigration consulting" licensing regime like Japan's 行政書士. However:
- Penalties vary by context (unauthorized legal services, fraud, operating unlicensed service business)
- Violators may face administrative fines, business closure, or criminal liability for fraud/deception
- **Recommended approach:** Treat as unlicensed professional service; apply cautious disclaimer standards

### Required Disclaimers

Every China visa page must display:

> "LocalNomad provides published visa information for educational purposes only. We do not provide immigration consulting (咨询), legal advice, or visa eligibility assessment. This is not a substitute for consultation with the National Immigration Administration (国家移民管理局) or official government resources. Visa requirements and policies change frequently; verify all information directly with official sources before applying."

## THAILAND

### Relevant Laws
- **Immigration Act, B.E. 2522 (1979)**
- **Immigration Act regulations and amendments** (ongoing updates through 2025)
- **Electronic Visa Systems Regulations** (for official e-visa portal procedures)

### Legal Framework for Visa Information Websites

#### What LocalNomad CAN Do
- ✅ Display published visa categories (Tourist, Non-Immigrant, others) from MOI/Immigration Bureau
- ✅ Show visa requirements from official sources (Royal Thai Police, MFA)
- ✅ Provide visa comparison tables (factual information only)
- ✅ Explain visa application timelines and general procedures
- ✅ Link to official Thai e-visa portal (thaievisa.go.th)
- ✅ Host checklists for document requirements
- ✅ Display entry requirements and border procedures

#### What LocalNomad MUST NEVER Do
- ❌ **Provide visa consulting** — Cannot recommend visa types based on user situation
- ❌ **Assess eligibility** — Cannot say "you qualify for" or "you are eligible for"
- ❌ **File applications** — Cannot submit to Immigration Bureau on user's behalf
- ❌ **Store sensitive information** — Cannot cache passport numbers, visa stamps, or personal details
- ❌ **Auto-fill government forms** — Cannot pre-populate official visa applications
- ❌ **Charge for guidance** — Cannot monetize visa selection or application assistance
- ❌ **Offer visa services**—Cannot operate as unlicensed immigration agent/broker

### Penalties for Violations

**Unlicensed immigration services (Immigration Act violations):**
- Administrative penalties and business closure orders from Immigration Bureau
- Fines for operating without proper licensing
- Reputational damage and potential criminal liability for fraud/misrepresentation
- Specific article penalties vary; not publicly detailed in available sources

### Required Disclaimers

Every Thailand visa page must display:

> "LocalNomad is an informational resource only. We do not provide immigration consulting, legal advice, or visa eligibility determination. Visa requirements may change; always verify with the Royal Thai Police Immigration Bureau (www.immigration.go.th) or the Ministry of Foreign Affairs before applying. For personalized visa assistance, consult an immigration lawyer or authorized visa service provider."

## INDONESIA

### Relevant Laws
- **Law Number 6 of 2011 on Immigration (Undang-Undang No. 6 Tahun 2011 tentang Keimigrasian)**
- **Directorate General of Immigration Regulations**

### Legal Framework for Visa Information Websites

#### What LocalNomad CAN Do
- ✅ Display visa types and published requirements from the Ministry of Law and Human Rights
- ✅ Show visa categories (Free Entry, Visa on Arrival, e-Visa, work visas, etc.)
- ✅ Provide visa comparison tables with factual information
- ✅ Link to official eVisa website (evisa.imigrasi.go.id)
- ✅ Explain visa processing procedures and timelines
- ✅ Host educational content about Indonesia's immigration system
- ✅ Provide checklists for required documents

#### What LocalNomad MUST NEVER Do
- ❌ **Provide consulting** — Cannot advise "which visa you need"
- ❌ **Assess eligibility** — Cannot say "you qualify for" or "you are eligible"
- ❌ **File applications** — Cannot submit e-visa or visa applications on user's behalf
- ❌ **Store personal data** — Cannot save passport numbers, visa application IDs, or personal information
- ❌ **Auto-fill government forms** — Cannot pre-populate official visa applications
- ❌ **Charge for application assistance** — Cannot monetize visa selection or filing
- ❌ **Operate as unlicensed immigration agent** — Cannot broker visa services

### Penalties for Violations

**Violation of Law No. 6/2011 on Immigration:**
- **Administrative violations:** Fines up to IDR 500,000,000 (500 million rupiah)
- **Failure to fulfill obligations:** Up to 3 months imprisonment OR fine up to IDR 25,000,000
- **Misrepresentation/false documentation:** Additional penalties under Criminal Code
- **Deportation and blacklisting:** Possible for foreign nationals who violate law

### Required Disclaimers

Every Indonesia visa page must display:

> "LocalNomad is an information website for educational purposes only. We do not provide immigration consulting, legal advice, or visa eligibility assessment. For official visa applications, visit evisa.imigrasi.go.id or the Directorate General of Immigration website. Visa requirements change frequently; verify all information with official sources before applying. We are not licensed immigration agents."

## MALAYSIA

### Relevant Laws
- **Immigration Act 1959/63 (Act 155)**
- **Passport Act 1966 (Act 150)**
- **Immigration Regulations and Orders** (ongoing amendments through 2025)

### Legal Framework for Visa Information Websites

#### What LocalNomad CAN Do
- ✅ Display visa and pass categories from official sources (Immigration Department)
- ✅ Show published entry requirements and eligibility criteria
- ✅ Provide visa comparison tables (factual information only)
- ✅ Link to official Immigration Department portal and forms
- ✅ Explain visa application procedures and timelines
- ✅ Host checklists for required documents
- ✅ Provide educational content about Malaysia's immigration system

#### What LocalNomad MUST NEVER Do
- ❌ **Provide consulting** — Cannot recommend visa types based on user profile
- ❌ **Assess eligibility** — Cannot say "you qualify for" or "you are eligible"
- ❌ **File applications** — Cannot submit visa/pass applications to Immigration Department
- ❌ **Store sensitive information** — Cannot save passport numbers, personal details, or application IDs
- ❌ **Auto-fill government forms** — Cannot pre-populate official visa applications
- ❌ **Charge for application assistance** — Cannot monetize visa guidance or filing services
- ❌ **Operate as unlicensed immigration agent** — Cannot broker visa services without proper licensing

### Penalties for Violations

**Violation of Immigration Act 1959/63:**
- Administrative penalties including visa cancellation and deportation
- Fines for violations (specific amounts vary by violation type)
- Possible imprisonment for serious violations (overstay, fraud, etc.)
- Business closure orders for unlicensed immigration service providers
- Potential criminal liability under Penal Code for fraud/misrepresentation

### Required Disclaimers

Every Malaysia visa page must display:

> "LocalNomad is an informational resource only. We do not provide immigration consulting, legal advice, or visa eligibility assessment. Visa requirements are determined by the Immigration Department of Malaysia and may change without notice. Always verify requirements with the official Immigration Department portal before applying. For professional visa assistance, consult an immigration lawyer or authorized service provider."

## KEY PRINCIPLES FOR LocalNomad

1. **Information ≠ Consulting**
   - Display published requirements ✅
   - Answer "what does this requirement mean?" ✅
   - Answer "what visa do I need?" ❌

2. **Never Assess Eligibility**
   - "Here are the published requirements" ✅
   - "You are eligible for X" ❌
   - "You qualify for Y" ❌
   - Match percentages / scores ❌

3. **Client-Side Data Only**
   - All quiz/calculator data stored in `localStorage` only
   - Never transmit personal visa data to backend
   - No server-side storage of passport/application info

4. **Disclaimers Every Page**
   - Every country-specific visa page displays country disclaimer
   - Every quiz/calculator tool includes disclaimer
   - Every comparison table includes disclaimer

5. **No Monetization of Consulting**
   - Cannot charge for visa selection
   - Cannot charge for application guidance
   - Cannot monetize eligibility "scoring"
