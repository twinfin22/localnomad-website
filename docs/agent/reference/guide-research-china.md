### THE GREAT FIREWALL: VPN & INTERNET

#### Legal Status (2025-2026)
- **For Foreigners:** Using VPN for personal access is tolerated; no foreigner has been punished as of 2026
- **Gray Area:** VPNs not explicitly illegal but only government-approved VPNs officially permitted
- **Business Use:** Using VPN for illegal activities or unlicensed VPN business is prohibited
- **Enforcement:** Minimal for tourists; higher risk for long-term residents engaged in political activity
- **Source:** Travel China Cheaper VPN Guide 2026, Privacy Journal

#### Great Firewall Technical Reality (2025-2026)
- **Sophistication Level:** AI-powered traffic analysis, machine learning VPN protocol detection, deep packet inspection
- **Detection Methods:** IP blacklisting, protocol fingerprinting, coordinated blocking during sensitive periods
- **Detection Speed:** Regular VPNs detected and blocked within hours to days
- **New Technology:** CRRC unveiled 600 km/h maglev train (unrelated but shows tech advancement)

#### Blocked Services
- Google (Gmail, Maps, Search, Drive, YouTube)
- WhatsApp
- Facebook, Instagram, Twitter/X
- Telegram
- Most Western social media
- Many news websites
- Cloudflare-protected sites occasionally

#### VPN Solutions (Still Working Early 2026)
- **Most Reliable:** ExpressVPN, NordVPN, Surfshark (with obfuscation technology)
- **Alternative Options:** Mullvad VPN, LetsVPN (more niche, harder to detect)
- **Setup Timing Critical:** Activate eSIM-based VPN before arrival in China (QR codes won't load once inside firewall)

#### Chinese Alternatives
- **Search:** Baidu (Chinese search engine with similar functionality to Google)
- **Maps:** Amap, Baidu Maps
- **Messaging:** WeChat (dominates; can use for calls, messaging, payments)
- **Social Media:** Weibo, Douyin (Chinese TikTok)
- **Email:** QQ Mail (Tencent)
- **Cloud Storage:** Baidu Cloud, Aliyun

#### Internet Speed (Inside Firewall)
- Fast for domestic sites (Chinese content)
- Degraded or blocked for international content
- 4G/5G: Wide availability in cities
- Home broadband: Widely available

### BANKING: ACCOUNTS, WECHAT PAY, ALIPAY

#### Opening a Bank Account as Foreigner

**Visa Requirements (Critical):**
- **Z-Visa (Work):** Smoothest process, demonstrates stable income
- **X-Visa (Student):** Generally qualifies, may need university docs
- **Q-Visa (Family Reunion):** Usually qualifies with documentation
- **M-Visa (Business):** May work but longer verification; short-term accounts only
- **L-Visa (Tourist):** Typically does NOT qualify for standard banking

**Required Documents:**
- Valid passport
- Valid visa (see above)
- Proof of residence (landlord letter, utility bill, apartment registration)
- Employment letter or student enrollment (depending on visa)
- Chinese phone number (SMS verification requirement)

**Critical:** Must obtain Chinese SIM card BEFORE attempting bank account opening

**Banks Accepting Foreigners:**
- Industrial and Commercial Bank of China (ICBC)
- Agricultural Bank of China (ABC)
- Bank of China (BOC)
- China Construction Bank (CCB)
- Shanghai Pudong Development Bank
- Bank of Communications (BoCom)

**Source:** Travel China With Me 2026, LTL Chengdu/Beijing

**What Changed (April 2024 onwards):**
- April 2024: Tencent enhanced international services
- Overseas users can now create accounts using country phone numbers
- International payment card linking now enabled

### PUBLIC SECURITY BUREAU (PSB) REGISTRATION

#### The 24-Hour Rule

**Requirement:** Register temporary residence with PSB within 24 hours of arrival in China

**Applies To:** All foreigners staying anywhere except hotels/dorms (hotels register on your behalf)

**What Happens:**
1. Arrive in China
2. Check into accommodation (non-hotel)
3. Go to local PSB within 24 hours with: Passport, visa, accommodation proof, completed form
4. Officer registers your temporary residence
5. Receive confirmation (usually in passport or separate document)

**Time Period:** Each full 24-hour period counts as one day toward threshold

#### Exceptions
- **Hotels:** Hotel staff handle registration automatically
- **University Dorms:** University handles registration
- **Short stays:** Some cities have grace periods for transit (rare)

#### Penalties for Non-Compliance

**Minor Violation:**
- Warning for first-time minor violations

**Financial Penalties:**
- Fine up to 2,000 RMB (~$274 USD) for non-registration
- Additional penalties if other violations compound

**Serious Consequences:**
- Cannot apply for new visa, stay certificate, or residence permit without current registration
- Cannot obtain police certificate for unregistered periods
- Difficulty with exit from China if flagged
- Security bureau may refuse services

#### Current Status (2025-2026)
- Enforcement remains active and consistent
- PSB increasingly digitizing registration process in major cities
- Wechat mini-programs now available in some cities for registration
- Mobile police units in tourist areas handle registration

**Source:** China-Briefing 2025, China Scholar, Visa Hunter, Apply For China 2025-2026

### TAX RESIDENCY & INDIVIDUAL INCOME TAX (IIT)

#### The 183-Day Rule (2025-2026)

**Basic Rule:**
- Fewer than 183 days in China = Tax on China-source income only
- 183+ days in a year = Tax resident status

**Critical for Foreigners:**
- One full 24-hour calendar day = counts as 1 day
- Less than 24 hours in a day = does not count
- All time in China counts (not just work days)

#### The Six-Year Rule (KEY UPDATE FOR 2025)

**The Rule:**
- 183+ days/year for over 6 consecutive years = Subject to worldwide income tax from Year 7
- Applies to China-source income for years 1-6
- Applies to worldwide income from year 7 onward

**Reset Mechanism:**
- If you spend 30+ consecutive days outside China in any tax year, the count resets
- Useful for planning tax residency

**2025 Critical Threshold:**
- Expatriates who maintained tax residency from 2019-2024 (6 years) hit the worldwide income threshold in 2025
- Marks 2025 as a critical year for many long-term residents
- Worldwide income includes: Salary, remote work income, rental income, investment income, etc.

#### Individual Income Tax (IIT) Rates

**Standard IIT Rate:** 3%-45% progressive brackets (varies by income)

**China-Source Income:**
- Salary/wages
- Self-employment income
- Rental income from China property
- Investment income from China securities
- Royalties from China works

**Foreign-Source Income (Year 7+):**
- Salary from non-China employer (if working remotely for foreign company)
- Rental income from foreign property
- Investment income from foreign securities
- Remote work income

**Filing Requirements:**
- Annual filing typically by March 31 of following year
- Need Chinese tax ID number
- Professional tax accountant recommended
- Can backdate filings but penalties apply

**Tax Credits:** China offers foreign tax credits for taxes paid to other countries (prevents double taxation)

#### Compliance Tips

**For Digital Nomads Working Remotely:**
1. **Visa Status:** Stay under 183 days if possible (tourist/M-visa approach)
2. **Record Keeping:** Maintain detailed records of travel dates
3. **Professional Help:** Hire expat tax accountant familiar with both home country and China tax law
4. **Reporting:** Keep receipts for all deductible business expenses

**Common Mistakes:**
- Assuming tourist visa exempts from tax residency counting
- Not understanding that remote work for foreign company is still taxable China income
- Failing to plan exit before hitting 6-year mark
- Not understanding residency reset mechanisms

**Source:** PwC China Tax Summaries, China-Briefing Six-Year Rule 2025, OECD China Residency, Moore Kingston Smith 2025 Update, China Law Help

### WEATHER & SEASONAL GUIDE

#### Seasonal Air Quality Patterns

**Best Season Overall:** Spring and early autumn (March-May, September-October)
- Mild temperatures
- Lower pollution
- Comfortable humidity

**Winter (October-February):**
- Highest average AQI: 89 (poor air quality)
- Temperature inversions trap smog
- Coal/biomass heating in northern regions increases pollution
- Worst months: January, December (serious pollution weather)
- Particularly bad in Beijing, northern China
- Better: Southern regions (Hainan, Yunnan, Guangxi)

**Summer (June-August):**
- Best air quality: Average AQI 67
- Lowest pollution of four seasons
- Hot and humid in most regions
- High precipitation

**Autumn (September-October):**
- Excellent timing: Good air quality, mild temperatures
- Ideal for visiting most regions

**Spring (March-May):**
- Good overall conditions
- March-April: Dust storms possible from Gobi Desert (affects north)
- Otherwise comfortable temperatures and lower pollution

**Regional Variations:**
- **North (Beijing, Shanxi):** More seasonal variation; winter pollution worst
- **South (Shanghai, Guangzhou, Yunnan):** Less seasonal variation
- **Tier-1 cities:** Generally better air management; south better than north in winter

#### Monthly Breakdown by City

**Beijing & Northern China:**
- Winter months (Oct-Feb): Avoid if possible due to smog
- Spring (Mar-May): Dust storms possible
- Summer (Jun-Aug): Hot and humid; good air

**Shanghai & Eastern China:**
- Winter: Mild but smoggy
- Spring: Good, mild

- Autumn: Excellent

**Chengdu, Sichuan:**
- Winter: Cool, frequent fog (not smog)
- Year-round: More stable, less seasonal variation
- Summer: Hot

**Yunnan (Dali, Lijiang):**
- Overall: Mild year-round
- Best: March-May, September-November

### VISA & LEGAL GOTCHAS

#### Working Remotely on Tourist/Business Visa

**Legal Status:** Technically illegal but enforcement varies

**Reality (2025-2026):**
- Many remote workers operate on tourist or business visas
- Enforcement low if you don't draw attention
- No official "digital nomad visa" exists
- Officially: Z visa (work visa) required for any employment

**Risk Assessment:**
- **Low Risk:** Freelance work, personal projects, no employer presence in China
- **Medium Risk:** Remote employment for foreign company (tax implications)
- **High Risk:** Working for Chinese employer without work permit (active enforcement)

**Recommendation:**
- Consult immigration lawyer for your specific situation
- Consider Z visa if staying 6+ months and employable
- Understand you're in gray area and accept risk

#### Internet Censorship & Compliance

**Blocked Content:**
- Political content critical of government
- Sensitive topics around Tibet, Xinjiang, Taiwan
- Religious content (some)
- VPNs themselves (discussed above)

**Compliance for Businesses:**
- Cannot bypass censorship for business purposes
- Content must comply with Chinese law
- User-generated content must be monitored
- Data localization requirements for some services

**For Remote Workers:**
- VPN use for personal browsing is tolerated
- Do not engage in activism online (high risk)
- Assume all digital communications monitored
- Avoid sensitive political discussions in emails/messaging

#### Data Privacy Laws

**Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL - 2021):**
- China requires consent for personal data collection
- Strict restrictions on data transfers outside China
- Heavy penalties for violations
- Enterprises handling 1+ million people's data subject to security assessment

**Implications for Remote Workers:**
- If your work involves customer data, ensure PDPL compliance
- Data residency: May need to keep Chinese user data in China
- Encryption standards must meet Chinese requirements

#### Prohibited Business Activities

**Do NOT:**
- Export encryption technology
- Engage in unauthorized telecommunications
- Provide financial services without license
- Operate education business without license
- Practice law/provide legal services
- Operate medical services without license
- Conduct journalism without license/affiliation

#### Tax Residency & Employment Tax

**Already Covered Above:** See 183-day rule and IIT sections

#### Exit Permits

**Requirements:**
- Must have PSB registration current to obtain exit permit
- Police certificate may be withheld if you have unpaid fines or legal issues
- Proper visa status required

**Source:** HROne legal article, HiredChina remote work policy, China Law Help, multiple visa/legal guides 2025-2026
