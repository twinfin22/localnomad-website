# Visa Transition Paths Research (Japan & China)

**Date**: March 3, 2026
**Research Scope**: 10 visa types across Japan (6) and China (4)

## Overview

This document maps official and commonly-practiced visa transition pathways based on government immigration regulations and published guidelines. Transitions are only included when they are officially permitted or commonly documented in immigration case law.

---

## JAPAN VISAS (6 types)

### 1. Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services
**Type ID**: `engineer-specialist`
**Official Name**: 技術・人文知識・国際業務

**Paths To (From → This visa)**:
- Student visa → Engineer (graduates securing employment)

**Paths From (This visa → To)**:
- Engineer → HSW (Highly Skilled Professional) [common upgrade]
- Engineer → Business Manager (starting own business)
- Engineer → Permanent Residence (via HSP points or 10-year rule)

**Key Notes**:
- Most common work visa in Japan for professionals
- 1-5 year validity, renewable
- Can apply for PR under HSP criteria without first changing to HSP visa status
- From April 2027: must hold 5-year visa (not 3-year) to qualify for PR

**Sources**:
- [Japan Dev - Engineer Visa](https://japan-dev.com/blog/getting-a-visa-as-an-engineer-in-japan)
- [Baker McKenzie - HSP HSW Fast-track PR](https://english.visajapan.jp/koudo.html)

---

### 2. Highly Skilled Professional (HSP/HSW)
**Type ID**: `hsw`
**Official Name**: 高度専門職

**Paths To**:
- Engineer/Specialist → HSW (points-based upgrade)
- SSW1 → HSW (skilled worker advancement)

**Paths From**:
- HSW → Permanent Residence (1-3 year fast-track: 80+ points = 1 year, 70+ points = 3 years)

**Key Notes**:
- Fast-track permanent residence: 1 year at 80+ points, 3 years at 70+ points (vs. 10-year standard)
- Points system evaluates income, education, age, work experience, and contributions
- Allows flexible work authorization, business establishment, and dependent inclusion
- Holders on Engineer/Specialist visa can apply for PR directly under HSP criteria

**Sources**:
- [MOFA Japan - Highly Skilled Professional](https://www.mofa.go.jp/j_info/visit/visa/long/visa16.html)
- [English VisaJapan - HSP PR Fast-track](https://english.visajapan.jp/koudo.html)

---

### 3. Specified Skilled Worker 1 (SSW1)
**Type ID**: `ssw1`
**Official Name**: 特定技能1号

**Paths To**:
- Student visa → SSW1 (if in shortage occupation field)

**Paths From**:
- SSW1 → SSW2 (after 3+ years experience with demonstrated skill level)
- SSW1 → Engineer/Specialist (insufficient: lacks academic/specialist credentials)
- SSW1 → Permanent Residence (via 10-year standard route or SSW pathway under review)

**Key Notes**:
- Maximum 5 years on SSW1
- For shortage occupations (construction, hospitality, agriculture, nursing care, etc.)
- Requires skill certification and Japanese language proficiency (N4 for most sectors)
- Support requirements: Japanese language training, life support, workplace liaison
- Pathway to PR being formalized but not yet fully established in published guidelines

**Sources**:
- [MOFA Japan - Specified Skilled Worker](https://www.mofa.go.jp/mofaj/ca/fna/ssw/us/overview/)
- [ISA Japan - SSW FAQ](https://www.ssw.go.jp/en/faq/)
- [JoBins Media - SSW Path to PR](https://media.jobins.com/2025/08/15/ssw-1-vs-ssw-2-visa-path-to-japan-permanent-residence/)

---

### 4. Specified Skilled Worker 2 (SSW2)
**Type ID**: `ssw2`
**Official Name**: 特定技能2号

**Paths To**:
- SSW1 → SSW2 (after 3+ years on SSW1 with skill verification)

**Paths From**:
- SSW2 → Permanent Residence (potential pathway, still being formalized)

**Key Notes**:
- Unlimited stay duration (no expiry like SSW1's 5-year max)
- Can sponsor family members (spouse and children)
- Fewer restrictions on work authorization than SSW1
- Path to PR not yet fully established in published guidelines but under development

**Sources**:
- [ISA Japan - SSW Support Website](https://www.ssw.go.jp/en/about/visa/)
- [Team APAC - SSW2 Overview](https://teamapac.com/glossaries/skilled-worker-visa-ssw-2/)

### 5. Digital Nomad Visa (Designated Activities)
**Type ID**: `digital-nomad-jp`
**Official Name**: 特定活動（デジタルノマド）

**Paths To**: None (closed system)

**Paths From**: None (no direct transitions to other visa types)

**Key Notes**:
- Introduced March 31, 2024
- 6-month temporary visa only (non-renewable; must reapply)
- For remote workers earning ≥10 million JPY annually
- Requires private health insurance (≥10 million JPY coverage)
- **Critical limitation**: No residence card issued; difficult to secure housing/bank accounts
- Cannot transition to work visa while in Japan; must exit and reapply for other visas
- Status: Closed system with no published transition pathways

**Sources**:
- [MOFA Japan - Digital Nomad Visa](https://www.mofa.go.jp/ca/fna/pagewe_000001_00046.html)
- [GetGoldenVisa - Digital Nomad 2026](https://getgoldenvisa.com/japan-digital-nomad-visa)
- [Rippling Blog - Digital Nomad Limitations](https://www.rippling.com/blog/japan-digital-nomad-visa)

### 6. Business Manager Visa (Entrepreneur/Company Director)
**Type ID**: `business-manager`
**Official Name**: 経営・管理

**Paths To**:
- Engineer/Specialist → Business Manager (starting own company)

**Paths From**:
- Business Manager → Permanent Residence (10-year standard route)

**Key Notes**:
- **Major 2025 Overhaul (Effective Oct 16, 2025)**:
  - Minimum capital: ¥30 million (was ¥5M)
  - 1+ full-time employee requirement (foreign employees do NOT count)
  - Business plan must be certified by professional expert
  - 3+ years management experience or equivalent education required
  - Japanese language proficiency mandatory
- Transition from Engineer visa: timing critical—must file startup notification before employment ends (3-month gap = legal issues)
- Transitional measures until Oct 16, 2028 for existing holders

**Sources**:
- [Japan Business Visa Center - 2025 Overhaul](https://japan-businessvisa.com/en/en-changed-in-october-2025experts-thoroughly-explain-new-rules-for-the-japan-business-visa-business-manager-visa-for-foreign-entrepreneurs/)
- [KPMG - Business Manager Reforms](https://kpmg.com/xx/en/our-insights/gms-flash-alert/flash-alert-2025-195.html)
- [Baker McKenzie - Oct 2025 Changes](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/02/japan-revises-business-manager-visa-requirements)

### 7. Permanent Residence (Japan)
**Type ID**: `permanent-jp`

**Paths To** (visas that can transition TO PR):
- Engineer/Specialist
- HSW (Highly Skilled Professional)
- SSW1 & SSW2 (under development)
- Business Manager (10-year route)

**Paths From**: None (terminal status)

**Key Notes**:
- **Standard Route**: 10 years residence in Japan (minimum 5 on work/residence visa)
- **HSP Fast-track**:
  - 1 year at 80+ points
  - 3 years at 70+ points
- Can apply under HSP criteria without first changing to HSP visa
- **April 2027 Policy Change**: 3-year visa holders no longer qualify for PR; must hold 5-year visa instead
- Additional requirements: basic Japanese language ability, no criminal record, stable income

**Sources**:
- [English VisaJapan - HSP PR Fast-track](https://english.visajapan.jp/koudo.html)
- [GlobeVisa - Japan PR Policy Tightening 2027](https://www.globevisa.com/post/japan-s-permanent-residency-policy-officially-tightens-the-era-of-3-year-visa-to-pr-is-coming-to-end/)

## CHINA VISAS (4 types)

### 1. Z Visa (Work Visa)
**Type ID**: `z-visa`

**Paths To** (visas that can transition FROM to get Z):
- X1 Student → Z Visa (graduates with job offer)

**Paths From** (visas reachable FROM this):
- Z → R Visa (upgrade to talent/high-skilled status)
- Z → Permanent Residence (long-term work history pathway)

**Key Notes**:
- Standard employment visa for salaried workers
- Initial 30-day entry; must obtain Temporary Residence Permit (TRP) upon arrival (90 days - 5 years)
- Requirements: valid job offer, employment contract, relevant qualifications
- **Critical**: Cannot change visa type while in China; must exit and reapply
- Sponsored by Chinese employer with valid work permit approval
- Multiple renewals possible throughout employment

**Sources**:
- [VisaHQ - China Z Visa](https://www.visahq.com/china/work-visa-z/)
- [TravelChinaGuide - Work Visa](https://www.travelchinaguide.com/embassy/visa/work.htm)

---

### 2. R Visa (Talent Visa)
**Type ID**: `r-visa`

**Paths To**:
- Z Visa → R Visa (upgrade after demonstrating tier A talent qualification)

**Paths From**:
- R → Permanent Residence (preferred pathway for high-value contributors)

**Key Notes**:
- **High-level talent classification** (Tier A under new tiered system)
- Fields: science, technology, education, finance, high-demand expertise
- Requires government **Confirmation Letter for High Level Foreign Talents** from relevant ministry
- Multiple-entry validity: 5-10 years
- Per-entry stay: up to 180 days
- Significantly more prestigious and flexible than Z visa
- Shorter path to PR given government talent recognition

**Sources**:
- [VisaHQ - China R Visa](https://www.visahq.com/china/talent-visa-r/)
- [TravelChinaGuide - Talent Visa](https://www.travelchinaguide.com/embassy/visa/talent.htm)
- [China Briefing - Revised Entry-Exit Rules 2025](https://www.china-briefing.com/news/chinas-entry-exit-k-visa-rules-2025/)

---

### 3. X1 Visa (Student Visa - Long-term)
**Type ID**: `x1-visa`

**Paths To**: None (entry visa only)

**Paths From** (transitions away):
- X1 → Z Visa (upon graduation with job offer from Chinese employer)

**Key Notes**:
- For degree-seeking programs lasting 180+ days
- Valid for study duration (typically 1-4 years)
- Work restrictions: primarily academic; part-time work and internships may be authorized off-campus
- **Critical transition requirement**: Must secure formal job offer from Chinese employer
- Different visa type; cannot change status within China—must exit and apply for Z visa
- Graduates from Shanghai universities: special fast-track for Shanghai Free Trade Zone/Zhang Jiang High-Tech employers (can bypass 2-year experience requirement)

**Sources**:
- [TravelChinaGuide - Student Visa](https://www.travelchinaguide.com/embassy/visa/student.htm)
- [OnlineVisaGuide - Student Work Rules 2025](https://www.onlinevisaguide.com/article/details/3239/can-you-work-on-a-china-student-visa-in-2025-updated-rules-and-guidelines)
- [Beijing.gov - Student Visa Guidelines](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/studyinginbeijing/visaapplications/202306/t20230614_3134241.html)

---

### 4. Permanent Residence (China)
**Type ID**: `permanent-cn`

**Paths To** (visas that lead TO PR):
- Z Visa (long-term work history)
- R Visa (preferred pathway for talents)

**Paths From**: None (terminal status)

**Key Notes**:
- Indefinite residence status
- **Eligibility** (not fully detailed in published English sources):
  - Long-term work visa holders (typical requirement: 3-8 years on Z visa with stable income/contribution)
  - High-level talent (R visa holders with government recognition)
  - Investment/business contributions to Chinese economy
  - Marriage to Chinese citizen
  - Family relationships/ties to China
- Specific requirements vary by provincial authorities
- More accessible to R visa holders (high-level talent) than standard Z visa workers
- **Gap in official documentation**: Exact timeline and criteria not fully published in English; varies by province

**Sources**:
- [VisaHQ - China Talent Visa](https://www.visahq.com/china/talent-visa-r/)
- [China Legal Experts - Work Permit Guide](https://www.chinalegalexperts.com/news/china-work-permit-and-visa-application-and-requirements)

## Key Research Findings

### Japan Highlights:
1. **Strong HSP Fast-track**: Engineer → HSP → PR achievable in 1-4 years (vs. 10-year standard)
2. **Business Manager Overhaul**: Oct 2025 reforms significantly raised barriers (¥30M capital, mandatory employee)
3. **April 2027 Policy Shift**: 3-year engineer visas no longer qualify for PR; must hold 5-year visa
4. **Digital Nomad Isolation**: No published transition pathways; treated as closed temporary status
5. **SSW → PR Pathway Developing**: Not yet fully established; under development by ISA

### China Highlights:
1. **Visa Type Locked**: Cannot change visa status while in China; must exit and reapply
2. **R Visa Preference**: Talent visa holders have clearer/shorter path to PR
3. **Student → Work Clear Pathway**: X1 → Z documented, with fast-track for Shanghai graduates
4. **PR Criteria Opaque**: English-language sources lack specific timelines/requirements for Z → PR
5. **Provincial Variation**: Permanent residence eligibility varies by city/province (Shanghai ≠ other regions)

---

## Research Limitations

1. **China PR Criteria**: Official PR requirements for Z and R visa holders not fully detailed in English-language government sources. Local immigration authorities (each province/city) have discretion. Recommend consulting with Chinese immigration lawyer for specific cases.

2. **SSW → PR (Japan)**: Pathway acknowledged as "under development" by ISA; no finalized timeline published as of March 2026.

3. **Cross-Country Transitions**: No official Japan → China or China → Japan transition pathways exist. Individuals must apply through standard international visa procedures.

4. **Digital Nomad (Japan)**: No official documentation of transitions to other visas; consultants suggest exiting Japan and reapplying as standard route.
