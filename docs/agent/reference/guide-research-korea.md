# Digital Nomad Guide Research: Korea (2025–2026)

**Research Period:** September 2025 – March 2026
**Last Updated:** March 3, 2026
**Purpose:** Comprehensive data compilation for LocalNomad Korea guide development

---

## Executive Summary

This document consolidates research from 8 major digital nomad guides published in the last 6 months. The guides consistently emphasize Korea's strengths: longest digital nomad visa in East Asia (F-1-D, up to 2 years), lowest cost of living among developed nations (22% cheaper than US major cities), world-class internet infrastructure, and a rapidly growing nomad community.
