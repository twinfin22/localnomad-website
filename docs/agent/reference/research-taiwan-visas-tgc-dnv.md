## 1. Taiwan Employment Gold Card (TGC)

### 1.1 Overview

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Official Name (EN)** | Taiwan Employment Gold Card |
| **Official Name (ZH-TW)** | 就業金卡 |
| **Administering Agency** | National Immigration Agency (NIA, 內政部移民署) |
| **Type** | 4-in-1 Combined Permit |
| **Duration** | 1 year / 2 years / 3 years (applicant chooses) |
| **Renewal** | Yes, apply within 4 months before expiration |
| **Employer Sponsorship** | NOT required (self-sponsored) |
| **Application Platform** | [Foreign Professionals Online Application Platform](https://coa.immigration.gov.tw/coa-frontend/four-in-one/entry/golden-card) |
| **Total Cards Issued** | 14,907 (as of Oct 31, 2025) |
| **Currently Valid Cards** | 8,318 (as of Jan 31, 2026) |

### 1.2 What's Included (4-in-1)

1. **Open Work Permit** — No employer tie, free to job-hop, freelance, start business, multi-employer
2. **Resident Visa** — Legal long-term stay (180+ days)
3. **Alien Resident Certificate (ARC)** — Physical ID for banking, housing, telecom, daily life
4. **Re-entry Permit** — Unlimited entry/exit during validity period

### 1.3 Key Benefits

#### Tax Benefits
- First-time Taiwan employment + 183 days/year residency → **50% income tax exemption on salary above NT$3M/year** for first 5 years
- Conditions: No Taiwan household registration in prior 5 years, not a tax resident before TGC
- Overseas-sourced income fully excluded from basic tax calculation

#### National Health Insurance (NHI)
- **Day-1 access** — No 6-month waiting period (normal foreigners must wait 6 months)
- Dependents (spouse, children) also get immediate NHI access

#### Family Support
- Spouse + minor children → Dependent ARC
- Parents/grandparents (both sides) → Multiple-entry Visitor Visa, up to 1 year stay
- **2026 Update**: Spouses can now directly apply for **open work permits**

#### APRC (Permanent Residency) Fast-Track
- Normal foreigners: 5 years continuous residency
- TGC holders: **3 years** (HK/Macau excluded)
- Taiwan PhD holders: Additional 2-year reduction
- Taiwan Master's holders: Additional 1-year reduction
- **High earners (NT$6M+/year): Only 1 year** residency required

### 1.4 Industry Fields (12 Total)

Applicants must choose **ONE field** and **ONE sub-track** within it.

#### Field 1: Science & Technology (科技)
| Track | Key Requirement |
|-------|----------------|
| 1. High Earner | Monthly salary ≥ NT$160,000 in tech industry |
| 2. Advanced Tech | 3+ years in software, nano, MEMS, optoelectronics, ICT, telecom, defense |
| 3. Emerging Tech | 3+ years in AI, IoT, AR/VR, blockchain, robotics, additive manufacturing + commercialization |
| 4. International Award | Nobel, Tang Prize, Wolf Prize, Fields Medal, etc. |
| 5. National Academy Member | Member of national science academy |
| 6. Senior Academic | Chair/Full/Associate Professor or Researcher, 3+ years |
| 7. PhD Researcher | PhD + 4+ years R&D or tech management |
| 8. Rare Talent | Unique skills, 3+ years experience |
| 9. Scarce Skills | PhD + skills scarce in Taiwan, 3+ years |
| 10. Startup IPO | Led overseas startup to IPO — C-suite or core R&D |

**Critical Document Rule**: Employment certificates must include full name, company name, position, actual employment period, and official seal/signature. Offer letters are NEVER accepted.

#### Field 2: Digital (數位)
Four pathways:
1. **Experience**: 8+ years in digital economy
2. **Salary**: Monthly ≥ NT$160,000
3. **Academic**: PhD from world top-500 university (QS/THE/U.S. News)
4. **Hybrid**: PhD + MODA-recognized award OR 4+ years experience

#### Field 3: Finance (金融)
Six pathways:
1. **Executive**: Chairman/GM/Director or 5+ years manager-level at financial institution
2. **Certification**: 3+ years management + CFA/CFP/FRM/CIIA/CIA/PRM/Actuary
3. **Strategic Industry**: 3+ years in fintech, digital economy, green energy, asset management
4. **Salary**: Monthly ≥ NT$160,000
5. **Recommendation**: Official recommendation from FSC, banking association, securities association
6. **FSC Review**: Recognized potential to contribute to Taiwan's financial industry

#### Field 4: Education (教育)
- PhD from world top-500 university, OR
- 3+ years full-time professor/researcher at top-500 university (within last 5 years), OR
- Yushan Fellow Program recipient, OR
- 5+ years teacher/researcher + monthly salary ≥ NT$160,000

#### Field 5: Architecture (建築) — EXCEPTION: AND logic
Must satisfy ALL simultaneously:
- Licensed architect (Taiwan or overseas) OR 5+ years architecture design/supervision
- AND monthly salary ≥ NT$160,000

#### Field 6: Culture and Arts (文化藝術)
- International arts organization member, OR
- 10+ years at international arts NGO, OR
- Domestic/international award winner, OR
- Special verification by Ministry of Culture

#### Field 7: Sports (體育)
- Olympic event top 8, OR
- Continental/World Championship top 3, OR
- National team coach/referee at qualifying events

#### Field 8: Biotechnology (生物科技)
- PhD + 4+ years OR international invention award, OR
- 8+ years cross-disciplinary experience, OR
- Monthly salary ≥ NT$160,000

#### Field 9: Economy (經濟)
- Senior marketing/tech executive in advanced industry, OR
- 8+ years experience, OR
- Monthly salary ≥ NT$160,000

#### Field 10: National Defense (國防)
- 5+ years in defense, aerospace, military equipment design/project management

#### Field 11: Law (法律)
- Specific requirements per Ministry of Justice guidelines

#### Field 12: Practitioners of Ancestor Rites (宗教)
- Specific requirements per Ministry of Interior guidelines

### 1.5 Application Process

#### Step 1: Create Account
- Platform: Foreign Professionals Online Application Platform
- **Name must match passport exactly** (letter-perfect)
- No Mainland Chinese email services allowed
- Password must change every 3 months

#### Step 2: Choose Application Path
- Location: Overseas vs. Domestic (in Taiwan)
- **Cannot convert from**: Blue-collar work visa, APEC Business Travel Card, Chinese language study visa, Working Holiday visa → Must exit Taiwan and re-apply

#### Step 3: Upload Documents
- Passport (6+ months validity, color copy)
- Photo (2-inch, white background, within 6 months)
- Field-specific qualification documents
- Tax documents: W-2 (US), P60 (UK), T4 (Canada), PAYG (Australia), PIT-11 (Poland), etc.
- **Non-Chinese/English documents**: Must include certified translation
- **Special country documents** (Afghanistan, Philippines, Vietnam, Indonesia, China): Must have ROC diplomatic post attestation/verification

#### Step 4: Multi-Agency Review (30-60 business days total)
1. Relevant ministry reviews qualification (1-2 weeks)
2. Overseas: MOFA passport inspection (1 week)
3. NIA final background check + card printing (1-2 weeks)

#### Document Supplement Deadlines
- Domestic applicants: 3 days
- Overseas documents: Up to 6 months
- Failure to comply → Rejection, no refund

### 1.6 Fee Structure

| Target Group | Location | 1-Year (NTD) | 2-Year (NTD) | 3-Year (NTD) |
|-------------|----------|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| US passport | Overseas | 7,790 | 8,790 | 9,790 |
| US passport | Domestic | 3,700 | 4,700 | 5,700 |
| Non-US passport | Both | 3,700 | 4,700 | 5,700 |
| HK/Macau | Both | 3,100 | 3,100 | 3,100 |
| ARC renewal | Domestic | 1,500 | 2,500 | 3,500 |

**Payment**: JCB, VISA, MasterCard only (NO AMEX, NO China UnionPay). Also: Taiwan virtual account, convenience store payment.
**Non-refundable** regardless of outcome.

#### Post-Approval (Overseas Applicants)
1. Print "Resident Authorization Certificate" in color from portal
2. Enter Taiwan with certificate
3. Visit NIA service center within 30 days to collect physical Gold Card

### 1.7 Renewal Process

- Apply within **4 months** before expiration
- Log into platform → "Application for Extension as a Non-Expired Card Holder"
- Must still meet original eligibility conditions
- Previously uploaded docs auto-retrieved (but may need re-upload if field changed)
- Alternative: If unable to work, apply for 6+6 months job-seeking ARC within 3 months of expiry

### 1.8 Common Rejection Reasons

1. **Wrong visa status**: Entering visa-exempt but selecting wrong category → auto-rejection
2. **Ineligible current visa**: APEC, blue-collar, Working Holiday, language study → cannot convert
3. **Salary track**: Payslips without employer letterhead, missing 6-month continuity
4. **Industry track**: Generic CVs instead of concrete evidence (patents, DOIs, awards)
5. **Document issues**: Non-translated documents, missing attestation from designated countries
6. **Portal problems**: Session timeout (30 min/page), symbols in username
7. **Deadline failure**: Missing 30-day domestic / 6-month overseas supplement deadline

### 1.9 Community Tips (from taiwangoldcard.com, Forumosa, expat blogs)

1. **Document narrative**: Create a "story" with your documents — explain your expertise as if to someone unfamiliar
2. **Cover letter**: Write one highlighting uniqueness (like a job interview), even though not required
3. **Salary track tip**: Choose any 1 year within 3 years before application for tax return — pick your best year
4. **Don't wait for physical card**: Download Resident Authorization Certificate to enter Taiwan immediately
5. **Banking prep**: Get a Taiwan phone number first (needed for OTP); open account near registered address
6. **Consider consultant**: Borderline applicants report processing time cut in half with professional help
7. **Community**: Join taiwangoldcard.com Slack/LINE, monthly networking events, holders from 100+ countries
8. **Uber tip**: Optimal for foreign credit card users in Taiwan
9. **Housing**: Join "Rental Apartments in Taiwan" Facebook group; UR House for larger units
10. **Tax filing**: Community CPAs available; file taxes properly to maintain 50% exemption benefit

## 2. Taiwan Digital Nomad Visa (DNV)

### 2.1 Overview

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Official Name (EN)** | Digital Nomad Visitor Visa |
| **Official Name (ZH-TW)** | 數位遊牧停留簽證 |
| **Visa Code** | DN (informal) |
| **Administering Agencies** | NDC (eligibility review) + BOCA (visa issuance) |
| **Launch Date** | January 1, 2025 |
| **Initial Duration** | 6 months |
| **Maximum Stay** | 2 years (via 6-month extensions) — Updated Jan 8, 2026 |
| **Employer Sponsorship** | NOT required |
| **Application** | Overseas: ROC diplomatic missions / Domestic: BOCA offices |

### 2.2 Critical Legal Restriction

**⚠️ MUST NEVER work for Taiwan-based employers or companies.**

DNV is exclusively for:
- Employees of companies headquartered OUTSIDE Taiwan
- Independent freelancers serving global (non-Taiwan) clients

To work for a Taiwan employer → Must obtain separate Work Permit or TGC.

### 2.3 Eligibility Requirements

#### Country Requirement — Full Visa-Exempt Country List (BOCA Official, 2025-2026)

**90-Day Visa-Exempt Entry (64 jurisdictions):**

Andorra, Australia, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Canada, Chile, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Eswatini, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Guatemala, Haiti, Honduras, Hungary, Iceland, Ireland, Israel, Italy, Japan, Republic of Korea, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Marshall Islands, Monaco, Netherlands, New Zealand, Nicaragua, North Macedonia (until Mar 31, 2025 — check extension status), Norway, Palau, Paraguay, Poland, Portugal, Romania, San Marino, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Tuvalu, United Kingdom, United States, Vatican City State

**Special Notes:**
- Canada and UK passport holders may extend from 90 days to 180 days (reciprocity)
- Japan and US passport holders only need passport valid for duration of stay (not 6 months)

**14-Day Trial Visa-Free (extended to July 31, 2026):**
- Thailand, Brunei, Philippines (normal passports only, must show proof of funds + hotel)

**Source**: [BOCA Visa-Exempt Entry](https://www.boca.gov.tw/cp-149-4486-7785a-2.html)

#### Age-Based Income Tiers

| Category | Age | Income Requirement (past 2 years, meet 1 year) |
|----------|-----|------------------------------------------------|
| Young Digital Nomad | 20-29 | ≥ USD $20,000/year |
| Experienced Digital Nomad | 30+ | ≥ USD $40,000/year |
| Previous DNV Holder | Any age | Income requirement WAIVED (show other country's DNV) |

### 2.4 Required Documents

#### 1. Basic Identity
- Online application form (printed from visawebapp.boca.gov.tw, signed)
- Passport: 6+ months validity, blank visa pages, color copy of photo page
- Photos: 2 pieces, 45×35mm, white background, within 6 months
- **Temporary/emergency passports NOT accepted**

#### 2. Remote Work Proof
- Resume/CV or Portfolio (detailed education + work history)
- Employed: Valid employment contract with overseas employer
- Freelancer: Active project contracts (multiple)
- Activity Plan: "Description of Intended Activities" form — work plan during Taiwan stay

#### 3. Financial Proof (Dual Verification)
- **Income**: Tax certificates (W-2, P60, T4, PAYG, etc.) OR employer salary certificate proving age-tier income
- **Bank Balance**: Past 6 months **average monthly balance ≥ USD $10,000** — bank statement required

#### 4. Insurance
- **Comprehensive international health + hospitalization insurance** covering ENTIRE planned stay
- DNV holders are **excluded from Taiwan NHI** — NO exceptions
- Must cover the full period stated in Activity Plan

### 2.5 Application Routing

#### Overseas Applicants
- Submit to ROC diplomatic mission (embassy/representative office)
- Mission sends to NDC for eligibility review
- On approval, mission issues visa

#### Domestic Applicants (already in Taiwan visa-exempt)
- Submit at BOCA offices: Taipei HQ, or Central/Southwest/South/East branches
- **Must apply at least 10 business days before current stay expires**
- If overstay occurs during processing → Visa denied + fines

### 2.6 Fee Structure

| Application Location | Single Entry (NTD) | Multiple Entry (NTD) |
|---------------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Domestic (Taiwan) | 1,600 | 3,200 |
| Overseas (USD) | $50 | $100 |
| US Citizens (reciprocity) | NT$7,090 (domestic) | NT$7,090 (domestic) |
| US Citizens (overseas) | USD $185 | USD $185 |

**US Citizen Reciprocity Detail**: Taiwan charges US citizens reciprocity-based fees. BOCA policy states: "Visa applications made by passport-holders from countries that charge a special handling fee to holders of a Taiwan passport shall be charged the equivalent additional amount." Per TECO Denver, US citizen fee is USD $185 for overseas applications. Domestic (Taiwan): NT$7,090 based on reciprocity schedule.

**Non-refundable** regardless of outcome.
**Processing time**: 5-15 working days (standard)

### 2.7 Extension Rules

- **Extension unit**: 6 months at a time
- **Where**: NIA regional service center (no need to leave Taiwan)
- **Required**: Proof of residential address in Taiwan
- **Maximum cumulative stay**: **2 years** (updated Jan 8, 2026 — previously shorter)
- No dependents allowed under DNV

### 2.8 Tax Implications by Stay Duration

| Stay Duration | Tax Status | Implications |
|--------------|-----------|-------------|
| ≤ 90 days | Minimal | Only Taiwan-sourced income taxed (most DNV income is overseas-sourced) |
| 91-183 days | Partial | Remote work income performed in Taiwan becomes reportable; must file before departure |
| ≥ 183 days | Full Tax Resident | Progressive tax rates same as Taiwan nationals |

**Cross-sell logic**: High-income DNV holders planning 183+ day stay should check TGC eligibility first → TGC's 50% tax exemption on income >NT$3M is far more advantageous.

### 2.9 Community Tips (from expat blogs, forums)

1. **Insurance**: Get SafetyWing, World Nomads, or Cigna Global — must explicitly cover Taiwan and hospitalization
2. **Bank balance**: Start maintaining $10K+ average 6 months BEFORE applying — last-minute deposits don't count
3. **Activity Plan**: Be specific about projects and clients — vague plans get questioned
4. **Taipei vs. elsewhere**: Taipei has best coworking/transit/expat community; Taichung/Kaohsiung are cheaper
5. **Monthly costs**: USD $1,200-2,000 (rent, food, coworking, transport)
6. **Housing deposits**: Landlords typically require 2 months' rent upfront
7. **Don't overstay**: If processing takes longer than expected, you're responsible for overstay fines
8. **Community**: Join "Digital Nomads Taiwan" and "Expats in Taiwan" Facebook groups
9. **Prior DNV trick**: If you held DNV from another country (e.g., Thailand, Portugal), income requirement is waived
10. **No NHI**: Budget for private insurance — Taiwan hospitals accept foreign insurance but you may need to pay upfront and claim later

## 5. Visa Transition Paths (for JSON pathsTo/pathsFrom)

### Gold Card (TGC) Paths
**pathsTo** (from other statuses → TGC):
- Visa-exempt entry → TGC (apply domestically, most common)
- Visitor visa → TGC (apply domestically)
- Work ARC → TGC (upgrade, cancel old ARC)
- Student visa → TGC (if qualified)
- DNV → TGC (apply while on DNV)
- ❌ Blue-collar visa → TGC (must exit first)
- ❌ APEC Business Card → TGC (must exit first)
- ❌ Working Holiday → TGC (must exit first)
- ❌ Language study visa → TGC (must exit first)

**pathsFrom** (TGC → other statuses):
- TGC → APRC (1-3 years depending on income/education)
- TGC → Job-seeking ARC (if unable to work, within 3 months of expiry)
- TGC → Regular work ARC (downgrade, rare)

### DNV Paths
**pathsTo** (from other statuses → DNV):
- Visa-exempt entry → DNV (apply at BOCA, 10+ business days before expiry)
- Visitor visa → DNV (if eligible)
- Other country's DNV → Taiwan DNV (income waived)
- ❌ Any work visa → DNV (would be a downgrade, illogical)

**pathsFrom** (DNV → other statuses):
- DNV → TGC (if qualified, common upgrade path)
- DNV → Work visa (if found Taiwan employer)
- DNV → Visa-exempt (let DNV expire, revert)
- ❌ DNV → APRC (no direct path)
