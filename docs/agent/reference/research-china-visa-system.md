# China Visa & Work Permit System Research (2025-2026)

**Document Date:** March 3, 2026
**Research Scope:** Comprehensive visa types, work permits, policy changes, and application requirements for foreigners entering and working in China

---

## Executive Summary

China's visa system is undergoing significant modernization as of 2025-2026, with the introduction of new visa categories (K visa for STEM professionals) and expansion of visa-free arrangements. The system operates through:

1. **Embassy-level visa issuance** (multiple visa categories)
2. **Work permit classification system** (Categories A/B/C based on talent tier)
3. **Residence permit conversion** (mandatory post-arrival for long-term stays)
4. **PSB registration requirements** (24-hour rule)

Key trend: China is actively attracting high-level talent and STEM professionals through streamlined processes and new visa categories.

## 1. MAJOR VISA TYPES FOR FOREIGNERS

### 1.1 Z Visa (Employment/Work Visa)

**Official Name:**
- Chinese: 工作签证 (Gōngzuò qiānzhèng)
- English: Work Visa / Employment Visa

**Purpose:** Issued to foreign employees hired by Chinese employers to work in China

**Duration & Extension:**
- Initial visa: typically 1-2 years
- Can be extended up to 5 years total
- Must convert to Residence Permit within 30 days of arrival

**Key Requirements:**
- Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity, 2+ blank pages)
- Confirmed job offer from China-registered employer
- Age: 18-60 years old
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent professional qualifications
- No criminal record
- Good health (passing medical examination)
- PU Letter (Invitation Letter) from Provincial Foreign Affairs Office
- Work Permit Notification from employer
- Criminal background check (legalized/notarized by Chinese embassy)
- Health examination (TB, HIV, hepatitis testing)

**Work Permit Classification:**
Once approved for Z visa, applicant falls into one of three categories:

| Category | Tier | Salary Threshold | Processing | Benefits |
|----------|------|------------------|-----------|----------|
| A | High-end talent | Typically ≥500,000 RMB/year | 5 working days (expedited "green channel") | Easier approval, fewer documentation requirements |
| B | Professional | Bachelor's + 2+ years experience | 10-15 working days | Standard processing |
| C | Ordinary | No minimum education | 20+ working days, subject to quotas | Longer processing, limited availability |

**Application Fees:**
- Work permit: 800-1,000 RMB (approximately $115-145 USD)
- Z visa consular fee: $30-140 USD (varies by nationality and consulate)
- Health examination: 400-600 RMB ($60-85 USD)
- Total typical cost: $200-350 USD (excluding document authentication)

**Processing Timeline:**
- PU Letter: 2-3 months (employer-driven)
- Work permit notification: 1-3 weeks
- Z visa: 1-2 weeks (after work permit approval)
- Residence permit conversion: 7-10 days post-arrival
- **Total end-to-end:** 2-3 months from initial application

**Required Documents (Complete List):**
- Passport + photocopy
- Visa application form (COVA system)
- Passport-sized photo (white background, full face, no hat)
- PU Letter (Invitation Letter from Provincial FAO)
- Work permit notification
- Educational degree certificate (legalized)
- Criminal background check (legalized by Chinese embassy)
- Medical examination results (from designated clinic)
- Employer business license copy
- Employment contract
- Job description/role details
- Proof of professional qualifications

**Residence Permit (Post-Arrival):**
- Must apply within 30 days of arrival
- Validity: up to 5 years
- Requires: passport, residence permit application, medical exam, registration form
- Processing: 7-10 working days

### 1.2 R Visa (High-Level Talent/Residence Visa)

**Official Name:**
- Chinese: 人才签证 (Réncái qiānzhèng)
- English: Talent Visa / High-Level Talent Visa / Residence Visa

**Purpose:** For foreign high-level talents, urgently-needed professionals, and investors

**Duration & Extension:**
- Residence permit: up to 5 years validity
- Can be extended for maximum 180 days beyond initial period
- Post-arrival registration: within 30 days

**Key Requirements:**
- Must be certified as "high-level talent" or "urgently-needed talent" by authorized Chinese organizations:
  - Organization Department of the CPC Central Committee
  - Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security (MOHRSS)
  - State Administration of Foreign Experts Affairs
  - Other relevant talent authorities
- Proof of investor status (for investment track)
- No serious mental disorder, tuberculosis, or infectious diseases
- Clean criminal record
- Good health

**Who Qualifies:**
- High-level scientists and researchers
- Technology leaders and entrepreneurs
- Specialized talent urgently needed for economic/social development
- Foreign investors with stable investment situation (3+ years)

**Work Restrictions:**
R visa holders can work, but must apply for work permit with employing organization after arrival

**Application Fees:**
- Generally lower than standard Z visa path
- Residence permit processing: standard fees apply

**Processing Timeline:**
- Certification process: varies (3-6 months typical)
- Visa issuance: 1-2 weeks after certification
- Post-arrival residence permit: 7-10 days

**Required Documents:**
- Passport + photocopy
- Certification letter of high-level talent status
- Medical certificate (for permits >1 year validity)
- Application form
- Proof of qualifications/credentials

**Post-Arrival Requirements:**
- Apply for Residence Permit within 30 days
- Work permit application (if intending to work)
- Medical examination (if initial residence permit >1 year)

### 1.3 X Visa (Student Visa)

**Official Name:**
- Chinese: 学习签证 (Xuéxí qiānzhèng)
- English: Student Visa / Study Visa

**Purpose:** For international students pursuing education in China (bachelor's, master's, PhD, language programs)

**Types:**
- **X1**: Study duration >180 days (semester or longer programs)
- **X2**: Study duration ≤180 days (short-term programs, language courses)

**Duration & Extension:**
- X1: up to 5 years (aligned with program length)
- X2: up to 180 days (non-extendable)
- X1 holders must apply for Temporary Residence Permit within 30 days

**Key Requirements:**
- Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity)
- Admission notice from Chinese university/institution
- JW202 or JW201 form:
  - **JW201**: For government-sponsored/scholarship students
  - **JW202**: For self-sponsored international students
- Completed visa application form
- Recent passport-sized photo (white background)
- Good health (may require medical exam for longer programs)

**Educational Institutions Recognized:**
- Bachelor's degree programs
- Master's and PhD programs
- Short-term language/exchange programs
- Professional training courses

**Application Fees:**
- Visa fee: varies by nationality ($30-140 USD typical range)
- Residence permit (X1 only): standard fees apply
- No work permit required for study visa

**Processing Timeline:**
- University admission + JW forms: 2-4 weeks
- Visa application: 1-2 weeks after documents ready
- Residence permit (X1): 7-10 days post-arrival

**Required Documents:**
- Valid passport
- Completed visa application form
- Passport photo
- Admission notice (original)
- JW201/JW202 form (original and photocopy)
- Medical exam results (if required by institution)
- Proof of financial means (bank statements)
- High school/previous degree certificates

**Residence Permit (X1 Only):**
- Must apply within 30 days of arrival
- Validity: up to 5 years
- Issued as Temporary Residence Permit

### 1.4 Q Visa (Family Reunion/Dependent Visa)

**Official Name:**
- Chinese: 亲属签证 (Qīnshǔ qiānzhèng)
- English: Family Reunion Visa / Dependent Visa / Relative Visa

**Purpose:** For relatives of Chinese citizens or foreigners with permanent residence

**Types:**
- **Q1**: Long-term family reunion (>180 days)
- **Q2**: Short-term family visit (≤180 days)

**Eligible Family Members (Q1):**
- Spouse
- Children (minors or adult dependents)
- Parents
- Parents-in-law
- Siblings
- Grandparents
- Grandchildren

**Duration & Extension:**
- Q1: 1-5 years (varies by case)
- Q2: 30 days (non-extendable, single/double entry)
- Q1 requires Residence Permit application within 30 days

**Key Requirements:**
- Valid passport (6+ months validity)
- Marriage certificate (if spouse applying)
- Birth certificate (if dependent child)
- Proof of family relationship
- Chinese family member's identification (ID/passport)
- Financial sponsorship proof (for main applicant)
- Medical examination (for Q1, >1 year validity)
- Visa application form

**Work Restrictions:**
- Q1 can work if employer arranges work permit
- Q2 cannot work

**Application Fees:**
- Visa fee: CNY 160-250 equivalent (varies by location)
- Residence permit: standard fees

**Processing Timeline:**
- Visa: 1-2 weeks
- Residence permit (Q1): 7-10 days post-arrival

**Special Notes:**
- Q visa not extendable beyond original grant
- Q1 can be renewed in different periods
- S visa available for immediate family of Z visa/R visa holders

### 1.5 S Visa (Dependent/Family Member Visa for Work/Study Permit Holders)

**Official Name:**
- Chinese: 随行家属签证 (Suíxíng jiāshǔ qiānzhèng)
- English: Dependent Visa / Spouse/Family Visa

**Purpose:** For family members accompanying Z visa (work) or X visa (study) holders

**Eligible Dependents:**
- Spouse
- Children under 18 years old
- Parents (in some cases)
- Parents-in-law (in some cases)

**Duration & Extension:**
- Same validity as primary visa holder's permit
- Typically up to 5 years
- Must register for Residence Permit within 30 days

**Key Requirements:**
- Valid passport (6+ months validity)
- Marriage certificate (if spouse)
- Birth certificate (if child)
- Proof of family relationship
- Primary visa holder's residence permit copy
- Medical examination
- Application form

**Work Restrictions:**
- Cannot work independently
- Spouse/adult dependents can apply for work permit if employer sponsors

**Application Fees:**
- Similar to Q visa fees
- Processed as family unit

**Processing Timeline:**
- Visa: 1-2 weeks
- Residence permit: 7-10 days post-arrival

### 1.6 K Visa (STEM Talent Visa) - **NEW as of October 1, 2025**

**Official Name:**
- Chinese: 科技人才签证 (Kējì réncái qiānzhèng)

**Launch Date:** October 1, 2025

**Eligibility Criteria:**
- Bachelor's degree or higher in STEM fields from renowned domestic or international universities/research institutions
- Foreign youth engaged in education, research, or professional work in STEM-related fields
- Age: No strict upper limit stated, but targets "young" professionals
- No prior sponsorship requirement needed

**Key Advantages Over Z Visa:**
- **No employer sponsorship required** - can apply independently
- **No job offer needed** - based on educational background/credentials
- More flexibility in entry frequency and validity periods
- Faster processing than standard work permits
- Enhanced eligibility for both research and business activities

**Scope of Activities:**
- Education and research exchanges
- Science and technology collaboration
- Cultural exchanges
- Entrepreneurship and business activities
- Technology innovation and development

**Duration & Extension:**
- Expected 1-3 years initially (formal details being finalized)
- Anticipated renewable based on continued employment/research

**Key Requirements:**
- Bachelor's degree or higher in STEM field
- Valid passport (6+ months validity)
- Educational credentials (degree certificates, transcripts)
- Research/professional experience documentation
- Visa application form
- Passport photo

**Application Fees:**
- Expected similar to Z visa range ($30-140 USD)
- Exact fees still being finalized by immigration authorities

**Processing Timeline:**
- Expected faster than Z visa (tentatively 1-2 weeks)
- Details still being formalized

**Strategic Context:**
- China's response to attracting global STEM talent as US immigration policies tighten
- Part of broader workforce development strategy
- Focus on facilitating cooperation among young professionals internationally

**Status:** This visa category is very new; detailed implementation guidelines and processing procedures are still being finalized by National Immigration Administration

### 1.7 M Visa (Business/Commercial Visa)

**Official Name:**
- Chinese: 商务签证 (Shānglù qiānzhèng)
- English: Business Visa / Commercial Visa

**Purpose:** For business visitors, managers, traders conducting short-term business activities

**Duration & Extension:**
- Single entry: 30 days
- Double/multiple entry: 90 days
- Can be extended once before expiration
- Extension limit: cannot exceed original duration

**Key Requirements:**
- Valid passport (6+ months validity)
- Letter of invitation from Chinese company (can also be issued by CITS or hotel)
- Completed visa application
- Passport photo
- Financial proof (bank statements showing sufficient funds)
- Round-trip flight reservation
- Hotel or accommodation booking

**Work Restrictions:**
- Cannot legally work on M visa
- For actual employment, Z visa required
- Business visitors only (meetings, negotiations, training)

**Application Fees:**
- Consular visa fee: $30-140 USD (varies by nationality)
- Extension: CNY 160-250

**Processing Timeline:**
- Visa: 1-2 weeks
- Extension: 7 days

**Extension Requirements:**
- Apply at least 7 days before expiration
- Proof of business activities (meeting notes, contracts, etc.)
- Employer invitation letter
- PSB decision within 7 working days

## 2. WORK PERMIT SYSTEM (Post-Visa Approval)

### 2.1 Work Permit Classification System

**Overview:**
After obtaining a Z visa (or certain other visas), applicants must obtain a **Foreigner Work Permit** classified as Category A, B, or C based on talent tier and qualifications. This is a **separate approval** from the visa itself.

**Scoring System:**
- **Score 85+**: Category A (encourage/attract)
- **Score 60-85**: Category B (control/regulate)
- **Score <60**: Category C (restrict)

**Factors in Scoring:**
- Educational qualifications (bachelor's, master's, PhD)
- Years of relevant work experience
- Salary level/compensation
- Professional certifications
- Language proficiency
- Job position seniority
- Industry demand/criticality
- Employer size and reputation

---

### 2.2 Category A (High-End Talent - Encouraged)

**Definition:**
- Scientists and technology leaders
- Senior executives and managers
- International entrepreneurs
- Specialized talent urgently needed for China's economic/social development
- High-salary professionals (typically ≥500,000 RMB/year)

**Benefits:**
- "Green channel" expedited approval
- Processing time: ~5 working days (vs. 10-15 for B, 20+ for C)
- Fewer documentation requirements
- Priority consideration for residence permits
- May exempt from certain medical/background check requirements
- Easier visa extension renewals

**Requirements:**
- Bachelor's degree minimum (or equivalent expertise)
- Relevant professional experience
- Salary meeting thresholds
- Employer sponsorship
- PU Letter
- Medical examination
- Criminal background check

---

### 2.3 Category B (Professional Talent - Controlled)

**Definition:**
- Bachelor's degree + 2+ years relevant work experience
- Skilled professionals in regulated industries
- Subject to labor market demand controls

**Common Qualifications:**
- Bachelor's degree (any field)
- 2+ years full-time work experience post-graduation
- Relevant professional certifications
- Technical skills in high-demand areas

**Processing:**
- Standard processing: 10-15 working days
- Subject to quotas and industry restrictions
- May be denied if sector is oversaturated
- Easier approval in critical sectors (tech, engineering, healthcare)

**Requirements:**
- Same as Z visa standard requirements
- Educational credentials
- Work experience documentation
- Employer sponsorship
- Medical exam
- Criminal check

---

### 2.4 Category C (Ordinary Foreigners - Restricted)

**Definition:**
- Foreign nationals without advanced qualifications
- Short-term assignments (typically <1 year)
- Non-specialized roles
- Approximately 22% of expat workforce
- Subject to strict quotas and industry restrictions

**Characteristics:**
- Limited availability, subject to government quotas
- Longer processing: 20+ working days
- Short validity periods (typically 1 year max)
- Higher scrutiny in application review
- May be denied more easily than A/B categories
- Restricted to specific industries/roles

**When C Permits Issued:**
- Company representatives on temporary assignment
- Non-specialized staff in key positions
- Expat managers in joint ventures
- Intra-company transferees without advanced skills

---

### 2.5 Work Permit Application Process & Timeline

**Two-Step Process:**

**Step 1: Work Permit Notification (Employer-Driven)**
- Employer applies to Foreign Experts Bureau or Foreigners Working Management Bureau
- Timeline: 1-3 weeks
- Required employer documents:
  - Business license copy
  - Application form
  - Foreigner profile/CV
  - Job description
  - Employment contract (draft)
  - Proof of business capacity to hire

**Step 2: Z Visa + Residence Permit (Employee-Driven)**
- Once work permit notification received, employee applies for Z visa at embassy
- Employee applies for residence permit within 30 days of arrival
- Timeline: visa 1-2 weeks + residence permit 7-10 days

**Total End-to-End Timeline:** 2-3 months

## 3. POST-ARRIVAL RESIDENCE PERMIT SYSTEM

### 3.1 Residence Permit Overview

**Definition:**
A Residence Permit (居留许可) is the legal authorization for foreign nationals to live and stay in China for extended periods. It's **mandatory for all long-term visas** (>30 days).

**Requirement:**
- Must apply within **30 days of arrival** in China
- Issued by Public Security Bureau (PSB) Exit-Entry Administration
- Replaces the visa for day-to-day legal status

**Validity Periods:**
- Short-term: 30 days to 1 year
- Medium-term: 1-3 years
- Long-term: 3-5 years

**Types Issued:**
- **Temporary Residence Permit** (for work, study, family visas - X1, Z, Q1, etc.)
- **Residence Permit** (for high-level talent R visa holders)

---

### 3.2 Residence Permit Application Process

**Timeline:**
- Must apply within 30 days of arrival
- Processing: 7-10 working days typical
- PSB decides within 7 working days of complete application

**Required Documents:**
- Valid passport
- Visa/entry stamp
- Completed Residence Permit Application Form
- Recent passport-sized photo (white background)
- Proof of accommodation (lease agreement, hotel receipt, property ownership certificate)
- Temporary Residence Registration (from landlord/hotel)
- Employment contract or school enrollment letter
- Medical examination results
- Proof of financial means
- Employer letter (for work visa holders)

**Application Location:**
- Local Public Security Bureau (PSB) Exit-Entry Administration
- City/district level PSB office
- Some cities offer online pre-registration

**Fees:**
- 30-180 days: CNY 100-200
- 1-3 years: CNY 300-500
- 3-5 years: CNY 500-800
- (Prices vary by city and permit type)

## 4. PSB REGISTRATION & TEMPORARY RESIDENCE RULES

### 4.1 24-Hour Accommodation Registration

**Rule:**
All foreign nationals must register their temporary residence with the Public Security Bureau (PSB) within **24 hours of arrival** in China.

**Applies To:**
- All tourists and business visitors
- All long-term visa holders
- Hong Kong, Macao, Taiwan residents
- ALL accommodation types

**Registration Method by Accommodation Type:**

**Hotels/Hostels:**
- Hotel staff automatically register guest passport within 24 hours
- No action needed by guest
- Registration completed during check-in

**Private Residences (Apartments/Houses):**
- **Landlord responsibility**: Landlord or property owner typically registers
- **If self-arranging**: Foreign national may need to visit local police station
- **Many cities now**: Online registration available via mobile app or police website
- Paper form: Form for Temporary Residence of Foreigners (usually in Chinese)

**With Friends/Family:**
- Host responsible for registering guest
- Both parties may need to provide ID
- Some police stations require in-person registration

**Employer Housing:**
- Employer typically handles registration
- Proof of employer housing arranged

---

### 4.2 Online Registration Options

**Digital Services Available:**
- English, French, Japanese, Korean, Russian language options
- Mobile app registration in major cities
- WeChat/Alipay mini-programs in some cities
- Official police websites
- Reduces need for in-person visits

**Major Cities with Online Systems:**
- Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Chengdu, Hangzhou, Nanjing, Wuhan

---

### 4.3 Penalties for Non-Registration

**Consequences:**
- **Warning**: First-time non-registration within 24 hours
- **Fine**: Up to CNY 2,000 (~$280 USD) per violation
- **Serious violations**: May result in visa denial, overstay status, deportation
- **Blocks services**: Cannot apply for residence permit, extensions, or new visas without current registration

**Important:**
- Registration is **prerequisite** for all PSB applications (residence permits, extensions, new visas)
- Even tourists must register or cannot extend visas

## 5. PERMANENT RESIDENCE PERMIT (Chinese Green Card - 永久居留证)

### 5.1 Overview

**Official Name:**
- Chinese: 永久居留证 (Yǒngjiǔ jūliú zhèng) / 五星卡 (Five-Star Card)
- English: Permanent Residence Permit / Chinese Green Card

**Purpose:**
Allows foreign nationals to reside in China indefinitely without need for regular visa renewals.

**Duration:**
- **Adults**: 10-year validity card, renewed upon expiry
- **Minors**: 5-year validity, renewed with 10-year "Permanent Resident Identity Card" upon turning 18

**Eligibility Tracks:**

**Track 1: Investment Track**
- Direct investments in China for 3+ consecutive years
- Stable investment situation
- Good tax payment record

**Track 2: Employment Track (Most Common)**
- Senior management position (VP, Deputy Factory Director or higher)
- OR associate professor/associate researcher title or higher
- Bachelor's degree minimum
- Good salary/tax record

**Track 3: Tenure Track**
- Continuous service in China for 4+ years
- Cumulative residence in China for 3+ years within past 4 years
- Good tax record
- Recognized professional contributions

**Track 4: Talent/Contribution Track**
- Significant and outstanding contributions to China
- Urgently needed by China
- Recognized internationally in field

### 5.2 2025 Requirements Update (Shanghai Example)

**Salary Requirements (2025):**
- Minimum annual salary: 886,104 RMB (~$122,000 USD)
- Minimum annual tax paid: 177,221 RMB (~$24,400 USD)
- Very high bar compared to other countries' permanent residence

**Residency Requirements:**
- Must physically reside in China minimum 6 months per year
- Requirement applies for 4 years before application
- Absences ≤3 months per absence period acceptable

**Timeline:**
- Total period to eligibility: 4 years minimum (5-6 years typical)
- Application processing: 3-6 months

---

### 5.3 Benefits of Permanent Residence

- **Unlimited stay**: No visa/residence permit renewals needed
- **Work freedom**: Can work without work permit; can change employers freely
- **Business rights**: Can establish/operate companies in China
- **Property ownership**: Can purchase residential property in major cities (subject to local rules)
- **Education**: Children can attend public schools like Chinese residents
- **Healthcare**: Access to public healthcare system
- **Travel**: Multiple entries/exits without additional visas or permits

---

### 5.4 Application Process

**Step 1: Determine Eligibility Track**
- Gather documents proving track qualifications
- Timeline: ongoing assessment

**Step 2: Prepare Application (3-4 months)**
Required documents:
- Completed application form
- Passport (original + photocopy)
- Residence permit/visa pages
- Educational credentials
- Tax records (3-4 years)
- Employment contract and letters
- Proof of residence (rental agreement, property deed)
- Medical examination (no serious mental disorder, TB, infectious disease)
- Criminal background check (notarized)
- Investment proof (if investment track)
- References/recommendation letters

**Step 3: Submit to Relevant Authority**
- Provincial Public Security Bureau (Immigration Management Department)
- OR National Immigration Administration
- Different cities may have different submission points

**Step 4: Processing (3-6 months)**
- Background check
- Tax record verification
- Employment verification
- Address verification
- Interview may be required

**Step 5: Card Issuance**
- Notification of approval
- Card printing and delivery
- 10-year validity (adults), 5-year (minors)

## 8. COMMON APPLICATION DOCUMENTS & REQUIREMENTS

### 8.1 Medical Examination Requirements

**For What Visa Types:**
- All work visas (Z visa)
- All study visas (X visa) for 1+ year programs
- Family visas (Q1, S visa) for 1+ year validity
- Residence permits for 1+ year validity
- Not required for short tourism/business visas

**Standard Tests:**
- **Chest X-ray**: Screen for tuberculosis (TB)
- **Blood test**: HIV, syphilis (RPR), hepatitis B, hepatitis C
- **Urine test**: General health screening
- **Physical examination**: Height, weight, blood pressure, general health assessment
- **Electrocardiogram (ECG)**: For applicants over 40 or with cardiac history

**Conditions That May Result in Denial:**
- Active tuberculosis
- HIV positive
- Syphilis (untreated)
- Hepatitis B or C (active)
- Serious mental disorder
- Other infectious diseases

**Previous TB History:**
- Past TB (treated/cured) may require additional documentation:
  - Past medical records
  - Treatment proof
  - Doctor's clearance letter
  - Extended chest X-ray monitoring
  - Case-by-case assessment

**Designated Clinics:**
- Must use PSB-designated international clinics
- Standard fee: 400-600 RMB ($60-85 USD)
- Major cities have multiple designated clinics
- Results valid for visa application for 6-12 months

**Important Note:** All results must be officially issued; self-obtained medical tests not accepted

---

### 8.2 Criminal Background Check / Police Clearance Certificate

**Requirement for:**
- All work visas (Z visa)
- All residence permits >1 year
- Permanent residence permit applications
- High-level talent (R visa) applications

**For Applicants Living Outside China:**
- Obtain from home country police/government agency
- Must be **notarized/legalized** by Chinese embassy or consulate in applicant's country
- Hague Convention countries: Can use Apostille certification
- Non-Hague countries: Full legalization process required

**Cost:** ~$50-150 USD (varies by country and service provider)

**Timeline:** 4-12 weeks (varies by country bureaucracy)

**For Applicants Who Lived in China:**
- Can request from local PSB
- Eligibility: Continuous residence in China for 6+ months or more
- Applies to Z visa, X1 student visa, J journalist visa, R talent visa holders
- Processed at PSB of city where applicant resided

**Required Documents for China-based Request:**
- Valid passport
- Photocopy of passport bio page
- Visa/residence permit pages
- Foreigner Temporary Accommodation Registration form
- Foreigner information form
- Power of attorney (if applicable)

**Cost:** CNY 100-200 (~$15-30 USD)

**Timeline:** 5-10 working days

---

### 8.3 Invitation Letter / PU Letter

**What is a PU Letter?**
- Government-issued invitation letter (邀请函)
- Issued by Provincial Foreign Affairs Office
- Demonstrates legitimate business reason for foreigner entry
- Required for Z visa (work), some business visas

**Who Issues:**
- Provincial Foreign Affairs Office (not employer)
- Only available to Chinese employers/organizations
- Employer must apply on behalf of employee

**Required for:**
- Z visa (work) - **mandatory**
- Some M visa (business) applications
- Not required for: L (tourism), X (study - admission letter used instead), Q (family - relationship proof used)

**Employer Application Requirements (for PU Letter):**
- Written application by employer (template provided by FAO)
- Company information form
- Foreigner information form (name, passport, nationality, position, salary, etc.)
- Proposed entry date and length of stay (max 180 days listed)
- Place where visa will be applied
- Foreigner's passport copy (scanned, valid 6+ months)
- Family relationship certificate (if family members also applying)

**Validity:**
- Single use (tied to specific foreigner and timeframe)
- Typically valid 3-6 months from issue
- Cannot be transferred to another person

**Timeline:** 2-3 months typical

**Cost:** Usually no direct cost; embedded in overall hiring process

**Important:** PU Letter is **issued to employer, not employee**; employee presents letter at embassy

---

### 8.4 Employment Contract Requirements

**Content Must Include:**
- Both parties (employer and foreigner)
- Position title and job description
- Salary and benefits
- Duration of contract
- Start date
- Workplace location
- Working hours
- Term and termination conditions
- Dispute resolution

**Must Be:**
- Bilingual (Chinese and employee's native language or English)
- Signed by both parties
- Employer company stamp/seal
- Match PU Letter details (position, salary, timeframe)

**Used For:**
- Residence permit application
- Work permit notification
- Visa application supporting documents

---

### 8.5 Educational Credentials

**Requirement:**
- Bachelor's degree minimum (Category B work permit)
- Higher degrees preferred (Category A)

**Must Be:**
- **Notarized/legalized**: By issuing country's education ministry or embassy
- **Translated**: Into Chinese by official translator or embassy
- **Verified**: Original + certified copy both submitted
- **Recent**: Usually acceptable if within 10 years of issue

**Hague Convention Countries:** Apostille certification acceptable

**Non-Hague Countries:** Full legalization through Chinese embassy required

**Cost:** $100-300 per document (translation + legalization)

**Timeline:** 4-8 weeks for legalization

## 9. COMMON REJECTION REASONS & APPLICANT PAIN POINTS

### 9.1 Top Rejection Reasons

**1. Application Form Errors (20-25% of rejections)**
- Spelling mistakes in names, addresses
- Wrong passport numbers
- Inconsistent dates
- Mismatched information across documents
- Incomplete sections

**2. Incomplete Documentation (15-20%)**
- Missing required documents
- Expired documents (passport <6 months validity, old medical exams)
- Missing photographs or wrong photo specifications
- Missing invitation letters or supporting letters
- Unsigned or unsealed documents

**3. Passport Issues (10-15%)**
- Passport expiration date within 6 months of application
- Fewer than 2 blank pages available
- Damaged or illegible visa pages
- Multiple nationalities without all passports submitted

**4. Photograph Defects (8-12%)**
- Wrong size (not standard 35x45mm)
- Wrong background (not plain white)
- Poor quality/blurry
- Applicant wearing glasses or hat
- Not taken recently (>3 months old)
- Wrong coloring or extreme filters

**5. Lack of Financial Evidence (5-10%)**
- Insufficient bank balance
- No bank statements provided
- Large/unexplained money transfers
- Inconsistent financial status with visa type

**6. Prior Immigration Violations (5-8%)**
- Previous overstay in China or other countries
- Prior deportation or visa cancellation
- Illegal work history
- Travel ban
- Fraud or misrepresentation history

**7. Unclear Travel Purpose (3-5%)**
- Inconsistent story (visa type doesn't match itinerary)
- Ambiguous job description or unclear employment
- No clear business reason for business visa
- Mismatch between invitation letter content and actual plans

**8. Medical/Health Issues (2-4%)**
- Positive for tuberculosis, HIV, syphilis, hepatitis
- Serious mental health condition
- Other infectious disease
- Medical exam report inconsistent with declaration

**9. Criminal Issues (1-3%)**
- Criminal record in background check
- Fraud or dishonesty in application
- Sanctions or legal problems
- Political sensitivity (journalist, activist history)

**10. Employer Verification Failures (2-4%)**
- Employer cannot be verified as legitimate
- PU Letter inconsistency
- Employment cannot be verified
- Company is under investigation
- Mismatch between employer and job location

---

### 9.2 Common Applicant Pain Points

**#1: Long Timeline (2-3 months)**
- PU Letter takes 2-3 months from employer
- Visa processing 1-2 weeks
- Residence permit 7-10 days post-arrival
- Total 2-3 months unpredictable
- Businesses want faster processing

**#2: Document Legalization Complexity**
- Different countries have different processes
- Hague Apostille vs. full legalization confusion
- Translation services vary in quality
- Cost can be $100-300 per document
- 4-8 week timeline for legalization
- Many applicants get steps wrong and must restart

**#3: PU Letter Dependency**
- Employer applies, not employee
- Employer often delays or forgets
- Cannot apply for visa without PU Letter
- Applicant has limited control
- Communication gaps between employer and FAO
- No transparency on PU Letter processing status

**#4: Medical Exam Logistics**
- Must use PSB-designated clinics (limited in some areas)
- Results must be official (not DIY health tests)
- Tuberculosis complications (past TB may require extensive documentation)
- Only valid 6-12 months (must retake if visa not processed in time)
- Cost varies by city (400-600 RMB)
- Health anxiety if any results are ambiguous

**#5: Confusing Document Requirements**
- Different cities have slightly different requirements
- City-specific forms not clearly explained
- Online COVA system has bugs/clarity issues
- Lost-in-translation documents
- Multiple sources giving conflicting information
- No single reliable source for city-specific requirements

**#6: Work Permit Classification Confusion**
- Scoring system not transparent
- Applicants don't know if they'll get A/B/C
- Quota system unpredictable
- Some industries arbitrarily denied
- No appeal process if classified as Category C

**#7: 24-Hour PSB Registration Burden**
- Tourists often unaware of registration requirement
- Hotels may not register immediately
- Private landlords sometimes unaware of requirement
- Online registration not available everywhere
- Some police stations have limited English
- Penalties for non-registration (fine up to 2,000 RMB) catch people off guard

**#8: Residence Permit Processing Post-Arrival**
- Must submit within 30 days of arrival
- Must coordinate with employer/school for required letters
- Must get medical exam done in China
- Cannot leave China during processing (typically)
- Anxiety about legal status during 7-10 day processing window
- Incomplete applications rejected with resubmission required

**#9: Visa-Specific Information Gaps**
- New K visa (STEM, Oct 2025) details still being finalized
- Each visa type has subtly different requirements
- Embassy-specific variations not documented
- Unofficial fees and timelines from agent services confusing
- No official English guides from all embassies

**#10: Cost Uncertainty**
- Multiple fees: visa, work permit, residence permit, medical, legalization, translation
- Total cost unpredictable (official fees + service provider fees)
- Some cities cheaper than others (no transparency)
- Service providers (visa agencies) mark up significantly
- Hidden costs (notarization, courier, interviews)
- Total cost: $1,000-3,000 USD typical for skilled workers (work visa route)
