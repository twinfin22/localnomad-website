### Banking Services

**Source:** Talent Taiwan (2025), Wise (2026), Taiwan Banking FAQ (2025)

#### Opening a Bank Account
- **Primary Banks:** Cathay United Bank, CTBC Bank, E.SUN Bank, Taiwan Bank
- **CTBC Bank** highlights:
  - Largest privately held bank in Taiwan by assets
  - Fastest digital banking growth (303% account expansion since 2020)
  - "My Way" digital account with automated bookkeeping, bill payments
  - Online account opening available

- **Requirements for Foreigners:**
  - Passport/ID
  - Taiwan address (can be accommodation address)
  - Some banks require work permit or residence certificate
  - Digital nomad visa acceptable at most major banks (2025 update)

#### Remittance Options
**Source:** Wise (2026), Taiwan Banking FAQ (2025)

- **International Transfers:**
  - Use foreign currency deposit account with SWIFT code
  - NT$5 million ($170,637 USD) monthly limit for personal remittances
  - Multiple bank options: CTBC, Cathay, E.SUN
  - Wise (TransferWise) accepted for receiving funds
  - Usually 1-3 business days for transfers

- **Receiving Foreign Currency:**
  - Open foreign currency deposit account at same bank as NTD account
  - Request SWIFT code for international transfers
  - Easier for digital nomads to receive payments from overseas clients

#### Banking for Nomads
- Many banks support digital nomad visa holders
- Online banking widely available
- Mobile banking apps standard (LINE Bank, digital accounts)
- No formal work permit required for account opening (new in 2025)

### Healthcare

**Source:** Wise (2026), TDN Asia (2025), Nomad Flag (2025), Talent Taiwan (2025)

#### National Health Insurance (NHI)
- **Timeline:** 6-month wait before eligible
- **Eligibility:** Requires ARC (Alien Resident Certificate) or residency requirement
- **Digital Nomad Visa:** Does NOT include NHI coverage (must get private)
- **Cost when eligible:** Very affordable (~NT$600-1,200/month for individuals)
- **Coverage:** Extensive - includes outpatient, inpatient, dental, traditional Chinese medicine

#### Private Insurance (Required for Digital Nomads)
- **Requirement:** Mandatory for digital nomad visa application
- **Duration:** Must cover full visa stay
- **Cost estimate:** $20-50 USD/month based on age/coverage level
- **Providers:** International insurance companies (AXA, Allianz, Cigna, etc.)
- **Coverage:** Typically includes hospitalization, emergency care, some outpatient

#### Hospitals & Healthcare Quality
- **Quality:** High-quality healthcare system
- **Availability:** Modern hospitals in all major cities
- **Cost (without insurance):**
  - Outpatient visit: NT$500-1,500 ($17-50 USD)
  - Hospital stay: Varies, but affordable by Western standards
  - Prescription drugs: Inexpensive
- **Specialists:** Readily available, shorter wait times than many Western countries

#### Timeline for NHI Access After Arriving
1. **Month 1-2:** Register for NHI application (need ARC)
2. **Month 2-3:** Complete waiting period
3. **Month 6+:** NHI becomes active
4. **Until then:** Rely on private insurance and out-of-pocket payments

### Transportation

**Source:** Wise (2026), Nomad Flag (2025), P.S. I'm On My Way (2025), Numbeo (2026)

#### EasyCard (Payment System)
- **Cost:** NT$500 (USD $16) with NT$400 pre-loaded credit + NT$100 deposit (non-refundable)
- **Machine purchase:** NT$100 with no pre-loaded credit
- **Used for:** MRT, buses, convenience stores, restaurants
- **Convenience:** Most efficient for daily transportation

#### MRT (Mass Rapid Transit)
- **Fares:** Vary by distance (specific fares not detailed in guides)
- **Single journey ticket:** Approximately NT$20-60 depending on distance
- **Transfer discount (EasyCard):** NT$8 discount when transferring between Metro and bus within 1 hour
- **Coverage:** Extensive in Taipei, growing in other cities
- **Frequency:** Trains every 2-4 minutes during peak hours

#### Alternative Pass: TPASS (Northern Taiwan Megacity Pass)
- **Cost:** TWD 1,200/month
- **Coverage:** Taipei City, New Taipei City, Keelung, Taoyuan (including Airport MRT)
- **Value:** Good for frequent travelers using multiple cities

#### Other Transportation
**Source:** Wise (2026), Holafly (2025)
- **Taxis:** Metered, affordable, readily available
- **YouBike (bike sharing):** Cheap option for short distances
- **Scooter rental:** GoShare, iRent available (popular locally but less common for digital nomads)
- **HSR (High-Speed Rail):** Connects major cities, faster intercity option
- **Buses:** Extensive network, cheapest option but requires Mandarin

#### Monthly Transportation Budget
- **Taipei Metro only:** NT$1,200-2,000/month (USD $40-67) if using TPASS or EasyCard regularly
- **Includes occasional taxis:** Add NT$500-1,000/month

### Tax Implications

**Source:** Wise (2026), Talent Taiwan (2025)

#### Tax Residency Rules
- **183-day rule:** Become tax resident after 183 days in Taiwan in a calendar year
- **Tax implications:** Liable for Taiwan income tax on worldwide income once resident
- **Treaty considerations:** Check for double taxation treaty with home country
- **Key point:** Digital nomad visa doesn't exempt you from taxes after 183 days

#### Tax Brackets (2025)
Not detailed in found guides. Would require Taiwan tax authority sources.

#### Gold Card Tax Benefits
**Source:** Taiwan Travel Blog (2025), TDN Asia (2025)

- **Special Status:** Gold Card holders receive favorable tax treatment
- **Benefits:** Reduced tax rates or exemptions (specifics vary, requires official documentation)
- **Qualification Routes:**
  - Professional workers (technology, finance, etc.)
  - Investors
  - Managers/executives
  - Age requirements and experience thresholds apply
- **Advantage:** More stable long-term option than digital nomad visa
- **Process:** Application through Taiwan government (National Development Council)

#### Double Taxation Treaties
- Taiwan has treaties with multiple countries
- Nomads from covered countries may have protection against double taxation
- **Action needed:** Consult tax professional for specific country

#### Key Tax Takeaway
"After 183 days in Taiwan, you're a tax resident liable for worldwide income tax. Plan accordingly." - Based on all guides

### Cultural Tips & Social Norms

**Source:** TDN Asia (2025), Holafly (2025), P.S. I'm On My Way (2025)

#### Language
- **Mandarin Chinese:** Official language, Pinyin based
- **Taiwanese:** Local dialect, understood by older generations
- **English:** Growing in Taipei and coworking spaces, limited outside central areas
- **Practical tip:** Learn basic phrases (hello, thank you, excuse me)
- **Tools:** Google Translate, Pleco app helpful
- **Assumption:** Younger generation (under 40) in Taipei speaks some English

#### Temple Etiquette
- **Removing shoes:** Not required (only in certain inner sanctums)
- **Photography:** Generally allowed but ask first in inner areas
- **Offerings:** Can participate or observe respectfully
- **Bowing:** Not required for foreigners, but appreciated if interested
- **Incense:** Used in many temples, can be overwhelming to new visitors

#### Social Norms
- **Directness:** Taiwanese prefer indirect communication
- **Respect for hierarchy:** Age and seniority respected
- **Punctuality:** Important in business but more relaxed socially
- **Personal space:** Close in crowded areas (MRT, markets) is normal
- **Eye contact:** Less important than in Western cultures
- **Tipping:** NOT customary in Taiwan (no tips in restaurants, taxis, hotels)

#### Dining Culture
- **Eating out:** Very normal, cheap, and social activity
- **Family-style meals:** Common in group dining, sharing dishes
- **Street food:** Accepted and excellent quality
- **Alcohol:** Legal drinking age is 18, drinking culture present but moderate
- **Tea culture:** Important, Taiwanese bubble tea famous
- **Manners:** Slurping noodles is normal and encouraged

#### Drinking Culture
- **Bars and clubs:** Concentrated in specific districts (Da'an, Zhongshan)
- **Drinking age:** 18+
- **Acceptance:** Social drinking accepted, public intoxication less tolerated
- **Cost:** Beers NT$150-300, cocktails NT$300-500
- **Safety:** Considered very safe for solo drinkers (highly safe country overall)

#### Technology & Online
- **LINE:** Dominant messaging app, often preferred over WhatsApp
- **WeChat:** Used mainly by Chinese visitors
- **Facebook:** Popular, used for community groups and local info
- **Google Maps:** Works well, MRT integrated
- **Payment apps:** LINE Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay widely accepted

### Legal Gotchas & Important Warnings

**Source:** Wise (2026), Taiwan Travel Blog (2025), Taiwan Digital Nomad Visa info (2025)

#### Working on Tourist Visa Waiver
- **Current situation:** Some DNs work on 90-day visa-free entry
- **Legal status:** Technically gray area - not explicitly permitted
- **Risk:** Theoretically could be asked to leave
- **Safer approach:** Use digital nomad visa for legitimate work authorization
- **Recommendation:** Official visa protection over visa-free entry

#### 90-Day Visa-Free Limit
- **Duration:** Visitors from visa-exempt countries get 90 days without visa
- **Renewal:** Can be extended once through immigration (additional 90 days = 180 days total)
- **Beyond:** After 180 days, must leave and re-enter (or use DN visa)
- **Documentation:** Immigration has records of entries/exits

#### Visa Runs to Nearby Countries
- **Traditional method:** Leave Taiwan every 90 days, return for another 90 days
- **Cost:** Cheaper than official DN visa (airfare only)
- **Hassle:** Requires travel every 3 months
- **Countries:** Hong Kong, Macau, Japan, South Korea nearby options
- **Current trend:** Digital nomad visa making this unnecessary

#### Digital Nomad Visa Limitations
- **NOT included:** NHI, labor insurance, Taiwan pension, work permit
- **Work restriction:** Can work for foreign employer, not Taiwan companies
- **Extension:** Can extend to 2 years maximum (as of Jan 2026)
- **Multiple stays:** Can apply again after visa expires

#### Important Clarifications
- **No automatic work rights:** DN visa doesn't authorize working for Taiwan employers
- **Independent contractor status:** Still required for legitimate remote work
- **Tax filing:** Must file Taiwan taxes after 183 days (no exemption in DN visa)
- **Insurance:** Must have health insurance (not included in visa)

#### Key Gotchas Summary
1. Official DN visa is safest option (not tourist visa waiver)
2. After 183 days, you owe Taiwan income tax
3. Health insurance is mandatory and not included
4. Can't legally work for Taiwan companies on DN visa
5. Gold Card better if you have qualifying profession
6. Plan for visa extension or departure after 6 months
