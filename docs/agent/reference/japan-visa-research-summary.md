## Key Findings

### Most Popular Visa Categories (by holder count, 2024)

| Rank | Category | Est. Holders | Growth |
|------|----------|------|--------|
| 1 | Permanent Resident | 918,116 | Stable |
| 2 | Specified Skilled Worker (SSW1+SSW2) | 284,466 | **+282,845 in 5 years** ← Fastest growing |
| 3 | Engineer/Specialist in Humanities | High volume | Stable, professional pathway |
| 4 | Student (Ryugaku) | 167,000 (new permits) | Primary feeder to work visa |
| 5 | Highly Skilled Professional (Points) | Growing | Accelerated PR path |

**Total foreign residents: 3.77 million** (10.5% annual growth)

**Top nationalities**: China (~35-40%), Philippines (~25-30%), Vietnam (~20-25%)

### 7 Major Visa Types Documented

1. **Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services** — Most sought-after work visa; 1-5 year initial stay; bachelor's degree + sponsorship required
2. **Intra-Company Transferee** — For multinational company employees; 4-6 week COE processing; salary must match Japanese market rates
3. **Highly Skilled Professional (Points)** — 70+ points triggers HSP status; 1-3 year path to permanent residency (vs. 10 years normally)
4. **Specified Skilled Worker (SSW)** — 16 sectors (caregiving, construction, agriculture, etc.); SSW1 (5-year max), SSW2 (indefinite renewal)
5. **Digital Nomad** — New (April 2024); ¥10M annual income requirement; 6-month non-renewable; remote work for foreign companies only
6. **Long-Term Resident** — Discretionary humanitarian/family-based; high work permission flexibility; renewed at immigration discretion
7. **Instructor/Teaching** — Bachelor's degree + job offer; JLPT not strictly required for work visa (but practical teaching needs N2+)

### Critical 2024-2026 Policy Changes

**Business Manager Visa — Stricter Oct 2025:**
- Capital increased from ¥5M → **¥30M** (6× jump)
- Japanese language now mandatory (N2/B2 level)
- Expected to significantly reduce foreign entrepreneur approvals

**Permanent Residency — Tightened Feb 2026:**
- Must hold **5-year visa** before eligible (mandatory hold period)
- Immigration now scrutinizes **past unpaid taxes/insurance** (negative even if paid off)
- Path to PR now 5+ years minimum (significantly longer)

**Other Key Changes:**
- Digital Nomad Visa launched April 2024 (6-month, non-renewable)
- SSW expanded to 16 sectors (added automotive, railways, forestry, timber in 2024)
- eVISA available as of Dec 2025 (USA, Canada, UK, Australia, NZ, Taiwan, etc.)
- Fee increases expected FY2026: visa change ~¥40K, PR ~¥100K (from current ¥4K)
- International graduates (Dec 2025+) can apply for Engineer visa with reduced documentation

**2027 Implementation:**
- Technical Intern Training Program being phased out
- New "Employment for Skill Development (ESD)" system replacing it

### Government Target

Japan aims to accept **1.23 million foreign workers** by 2027-2030 (announced 2025) to address labor shortages and demographic decline.

## Common Rejection Reasons & Pain Points

**Most Frequent Issues:**
1. **Outdated visa forms** — Forms rejected if not current version (download from MOFA only)
2. **Missing translations** — Every foreign-language document requires certified Japanese translation
3. **Educational mismatch** — Degree subject doesn't align with job duties → COE rejection
4. **Inconsistent financial proof** — Bank statements with low balance, irregular deposits, or insufficient 6-month history
5. **Document quality** — Handwritten corrections, overwriting, mismatched ink = immediate rejection
6. **Previous visa violations** — Overstays in any country heavily scrutinized
7. **Insufficient documentation** — Missing criminal background check, medical exam, or employment verification

**Important**: Immigration does NOT disclose rejection reasons (Administrative Procedure Act). Reapplication within 6 months of same visa type not accepted.

## Digital Nomad Visa Details (2024 Launch)

**Best for**: Remote workers, freelancers, business owners working for foreign companies

**Key Stats:**
- **Duration**: 6 months (non-renewable)
- **Income required**: ¥10 million annually
- **Insurance required**: ¥10 million coverage
- **Eligible nationalities**: USA, Canada, most Europe, Australia, NZ, Singapore, Hong Kong (expanding)
- **NOT eligible**: Most of Asia (China, India, Philippines, Thailand not on list)
- **Family members**: Can be included without additional income

**Critical**: Work must be **entirely remote for foreign company/clients**. No Japanese employment or on-site work allowed.

## Highly Skilled Professional (HSP) Points System

**How It Works:**
- Calculate points across education, experience, salary, publications, awards, etc.
- **70+ points** = Recognized as Highly Skilled Professional
- **80+ points** = Especially Highly Skilled Professional (1-year PR eligibility)

**Permanent Residency Benefits:**
- **70-79 points**: PR eligible after **3 years** (vs. 10 years normally)
- **80+ points**: PR eligible after **1 year** (vs. 10 years normally)

**Income Floor**: ¥3 million minimum for categories (i)(b) and (i)(c)

**Categories:**
1. Advanced Academic Research
2. Advanced Specialized/Technical
3. Advanced Business Management

## SSW System: SSW1 vs SSW2

| Aspect | SSW1 | SSW2 |
|--------|------|------|
| **Skill Level** | Considerable knowledge/experience | Proficient (supervisory) |
| **Requirements** | Pass Category 1 test OR complete 3-year internship | Pass Category 2 test OR Grade 1 certificate |
| **Language** | N4 (basic) | N3 (intermediate) |
| **Duration per stay** | Up to 1 year | Up to 3 years |
| **Cumulative limit** | **5 years maximum** | **No limit** (indefinite) |
| **Family dependents** | Not allowed | May sponsor |
| **PR eligibility** | No | Yes (after 10 years) |
| **Sectors available** | 16 industries | 16 industries |

**16 Authorized Sectors** (as of 2024):
Caregiving, Construction, Hospitality, Agriculture, Fisheries, Food Processing, Manufacturing, Electricity/Gas, Semiconductors, Automotive, Railways, Forestry, Timber, Food Service, Cleaning, Human Resources
