# i18n Glossary

Terminology reference for consistent translations across all LocalNomad locales.
Supported locales: **en**, **ja**, **zh-cn**

## Brand Terms

| Term | Rule |
|------|------|
| LocalNomad | Always one word. Never "Local Nomad" or "Local Nomad Club" |
| LocalNomads | Plural form. Never translated |
| localnomad.club | Domain. Never translated |

## Legal-Sensitive Terms

### Prohibited Terms (Taiwan)

| Term | Why |
|------|-----|
| 咨询 (consulting) | Immigration Act §56 — never describe LocalNomad features as consulting |
| "you qualify" / "you are eligible" | Attorney Act §127 — no eligibility determination |
| Match scores / percentages / probability | No ranking or scoring of visa fit for Taiwan |

### Required Disclaimer Phrasings

| Context | en | ja | zh-cn |
|---------|-----|-----|-------|
| General | Based on published requirements. Not legal advice. | 公開されている要件に基づきます。法的アドバイスではありません。 | 依据公开要求整理，不构成法律建议。 |

### Government Terminology (Keep Original in Parentheses)

| Term | Language | Usage |
|------|----------|-------|
| 행정사 | Korean | Licensed administrative agent — keep untranslated in parentheses |
| 변호사 | Korean | Licensed attorney — keep untranslated in parentheses |
| 移民业务机构 | Chinese | Licensed immigration business entity — keep untranslated in Taiwan disclaimers |
