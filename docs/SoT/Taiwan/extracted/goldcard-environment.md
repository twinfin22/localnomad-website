# Taiwan Gold Card — Environmental Protection (8 tracks)

## Track 1: Industry Experience + Salary
- Work experience in environmental sectors (pollution control, waste management, conservation, etc.)
- Monthly salary >= NT$160,000
- Docs: salary documentation, service certificates, resume

## Track 2: Exceptional Talent & R&D
- Path A: Academic qualifications + 3yr environmental field experience + research materials (past 3yr)
- Path B: 3yr environmental field experience + technical certificates/patents/publications
- Senior execs/technical leads of publicly traded or acquired firms; VC leadership also qualifies

## Track 3: International Award Recipients
- Nobel Prize, Tang Prize, Wolf Prize, or equivalent global recognition
- Docs: award documentation

## Track 4: Academy Members
- Academicians from national academies of sciences or equivalent
- Docs: academy membership certificates

## Track 5: Academic Professionals
- University chairs, professors, associate professors, or research institution researchers
- Significant academic contributions or internationally recognized publications within 3yr
- Path A: Doctorate + 3yr experience + research materials
- Path B: 3yr experience + technical/professional credentials

## Track 6: Doctoral Professionals (4yr+ Experience)
- Doctorate + 4yr in environmental R&D, management, or professional practice
- Path A: Doctorate + research materials
- Path B: 4yr experience + technical credentials

## Track 7: Doctoral Specialists (Scarce Expertise)
- Doctorate with expertise lacking domestically in environmental sectors
- 4yr+ experience + technical/professional certificates

## Track 8: Government-Recommended
- Recommended by competent authorities as having potential to contribute to Taiwan's environmental industry
- Docs: professional qualification documentation
