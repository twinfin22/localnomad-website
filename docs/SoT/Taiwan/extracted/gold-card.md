## Income Requirement
- amount: 160,000
- currency: TWD
- period: monthly
- notes: NT$160,000/month is the salary-track threshold available across most professional fields. Other qualification tracks (experience, PhD, awards) may not require income proof.

## Duration
- initial: "1, 2, or 3 years (applicant chooses at application)"
- extension: "Renewable before expiration; apply within 4 months of expiry"
- max_total: "Renewable indefinitely; APRC eligible after 3 years (1 year for high earners NT$6M+/year)"

## Fees
- application:
  - Foreign passport holders (non-US, domestic or overseas): NT$3,700 (1-yr), NT$4,700 (2-yr), NT$5,700 (3-yr)
  - US passport holders (domestic): NT$3,700 (1-yr), NT$4,700 (2-yr), NT$5,700 (3-yr)
  - US passport holders (overseas): NT$7,790 (1-yr), NT$8,790 (2-yr), NT$9,790 (3-yr)
  - Hong Kong/Macau residents: NT$3,100 (all durations, new applicants), NT$800 (extensions)
  - Existing ARC holders (domestic): NT$1,500 (1-yr), NT$2,500 (2-yr), NT$3,500 (3-yr)
- extension:
  - Foreign passport holders: NT$1,500 (1-yr), NT$2,500 (2-yr), NT$3,500 (3-yr)
- notes: "Payment via JCB/VISA/MasterCard only (no AMEX/UnionPay). All fees non-refundable regardless of outcome."

## Eligibility
- employer_sponsorship: not_required (self-sponsored, apply directly online)
- education: "PhD from world top-500 university (QS/THE/US News) qualifies for multiple fields. No formal education required for salary or experience tracks."
- experience: "Varies by field: 3+ years (most fields), 4+ years (post-doctoral tracks), 8+ years (Digital field experience track). No minimum for awards/startup tracks."
- special_conditions: |
    Must qualify in one of 12 designated professional fields.
    Cannot convert from: blue-collar work visa, APEC Business Travel Card, Working Holiday visa, or Chinese language study visa (must exit Taiwan and re-apply from overseas).

### 12 Qualification Field Categories (from goldcard.nat.gov.tw/en/qualification/)
1. Science & Technology — AI, IoT, blockchain, software, biotech, nanotech, optoelectronics. Sub-tracks: salary >= NT$160K/mo, 3+ yrs experience, doctoral degree, patents/publications, startup/VC experience.
2. Digital — Software development, IT services, gaming, e-commerce. Sub-tracks: (a) 8+ yrs experience, (b) salary >= NT$160K/mo, (c) PhD from top-500 university, (d) PhD + 4 yrs experience or awards.

## Processing Time
- total: "Over 60 business days (assuming complete documentation). Standard ~30 days; complex applications 50-60 days."
- passport_inspection: "~7 working days (overseas applicants only)"

## Key Policy Details
- work_permission: open (4-in-1 card includes open work permit — any employer, freelance, multiple jobs, start businesses)
- family_allowed: true
  - Spouses and children: dependent ARC (legal residency)
  - Parents and grandparents: extended visitor visas (up to 1 year)
- dependent_visa: Dependent ARC for spouse/children
- tax_benefits: |
    50% income tax exemption on annual salary exceeding NT$3,000,000 for first 5 years.
    Conditions: (1) first-time employment in Taiwan, (2) 183+ days/year residency, (3) no Taiwan household registration in prior 5 years, (4) must work in field matching Gold Card expertise.
    Calculation: (Salary - NT$200K special deduction - NT$3M threshold) x 50%.
    Overseas income may be excluded from basic income tax if below NT$7.5M threshold.
- nhi_access: "Gold Card holders can join National Health Insurance from Day 1 (no 6-month waiting period)"
- aprc_pathway: |
    Standard: 3 years continuous residency (vs 5 years for regular foreigners).
    High earners (NT$6M+/year): 1 year.
    Taiwan PhD: additional 2-year reduction.
    Taiwan Master's: 1-year reduction.
- four_in_one: "Work Permit + Resident Visa + Alien Resident Certificate (ARC) + Re-entry Permit"
