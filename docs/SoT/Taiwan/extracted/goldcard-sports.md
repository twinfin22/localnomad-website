# Taiwan Gold Card — Sports (4 tracks)

## Track 1: National Team Athletes
- Former national team athletes with outstanding skills who achieved:
  - Top 8 at Olympic Games
  - Top 3 at Continental Games (Asian, Africa, European, Pan-American, Pacific)
  - Top 3 at World Games
  - Top 3 at Universiade
  - Top 3 at World Championship or World Cup
- Docs: host organizer achievement certificates + supporting materials

## Track 2: National Team Coaches
- Coaches who guided national teams to:
  - Top 8 at Olympic Games
  - Top 3 at Continental Games
  - Top 3 at World Games
  - Top 3 at Universiade
  - Top 3 at World Championship or World Cup
- Docs: host organizer achievement certificates + supporting materials

## Track 3: International Sports Referees
- Served as referee for official sport at Olympic Games, Continental Games, World Games, or Universiade
- Docs: host organizer achievement certificates + supporting materials

## Track 4: Sports Industry Professionals
- Current or former sports industry professionals
- Recommendation letters from ROC national sports organizations, professional leagues, training centers, or universities
- No specific salary or experience threshold stated
- Docs: reference letters, work experience documentation, achievement records
