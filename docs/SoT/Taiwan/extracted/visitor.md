- employer_sponsorship: N/A (no work permitted)

- special_conditions: |

### Immigration Act Provisions (from law.moj.gov.tw, PCode=D0080132)
- visitor_definition: "Staying in Taiwan for less than 6 months" (Article 3.7)
- prohibited_activities: "Aliens cannot engage in unpermitted activities inconsistent with their visit purpose" (Article 29.1) — specifically prohibits unauthorized employment
- extension_rules: "Must apply before expiration if continuing stay" (Article 31.1). Overstays under 30 days allow reapplication with fines per Article 74-1.
- overstay_penalties: |
    - Overstaying triggers deportation and re-entry bans lasting 1-7 years (Article 18.3).
    - Overstays 30+ days face mandatory deportation (Article 36).
    - Fines range from NT$10,000 to NT$50,000 depending on length/circumstances.
    - Entry bans up to 10 years for serious violations.
- conversion_to_residence: "Those with 60+ day visitor visas may apply for residence if they meet specific criteria" (Article 23).
- deportation_process: "NIA executes deportations; written notice with grounds required (Article 36). Temporary detention may not exceed 15 days unless courts authorize extensions (Article 38)."
