# Taiwan Gold Card — All 12 Professional Fields

Applicants must submit under **ONE field and ONE regulation** only.
Architecture is the only field requiring BOTH conditions met (AND logic).

## 1. Science & Technology (13 tracks)
- Track 1: Monthly salary ≥ NT$160,000
- Track 2-3: Cutting-edge skills (software, nanotech, AI, IoT, biotech) + 3yr experience
- Track 4: Nobel Prize, Tang Prize, Wolf Prize, Fields Medal recipients
- Track 5: National Academy membership
- Track 6-7: University professors/researchers with PhD
- Track 9: PhD with expertise lacking in Taiwan
- Track 10-12: Senior execs from IPO/M&A ($5M+) companies or VC firms
- Track 13: Other demonstrable contributions

## 2. Digital (4 tracks)
- Track 1: 8+ years in digital economy + embassy recommendation or supporting docs
- Track 2: Monthly salary ≥ NT$160,000 in digital industry
- Track 3: PhD from QS/THE/US News top 500 university
- Track 4: PhD + patents/research + 4yr experience or awards
- Target sectors: software, IT, gaming, e-commerce

## 3. Finance (6 tracks)
- Track 1: Monthly salary ≥ NT$160,000
- Track 2: 3yr managerial + CFA/CFP/FRM/CIIA/CIA/FSA/FCAS/FIA/FIAA certifications
- Track 3: FSC or industry association recommendation
- Track 4: Senior leadership (chair, GM, director, 5+ yr managerial)
- Track 5: 3yr fintech/e-commerce/green energy/asset management expertise
- Track 6: FSC-reviewed potential contributor

## 4. Education (4 tracks)
- Track 1: PhD from QS/THE/US News top 500
- Track 2: 3yr teaching/research at top 500 university (within past 5yr)
- Track 3: Yushan Fellow Program recipient
- Track 4: 5yr education employment + monthly salary ≥ NT$160,000

## 5. Architecture (AND logic — BOTH required)
- Requirement 1: Foreign/ROC architect license OR 5yr design/construction experience
- Requirement 2: Monthly salary ≥ NT$160,000

## 6. Culture and Arts (7 sub-fields)
- Performing Arts, Visual Arts, Publishing, Film/Broadcast/Pop Music, Handicraft, Cultural Administration, New Cultural Industries
- Each has specific tracks (international awards, executive positions, MOC validation)

## 7. Economy (8 tracks)
- Track 1: Monthly salary ≥ NT$160,000
- Track 2: Executive at strategic R&D hubs/MNCs
- Track 3: PhD + key industry tech + 4yr experience
- Track 4: 8yr semiconductors/optoelectronics/communications/medical/precision machinery
- Track 5: 8yr creative industries (advertising, product design, digital content)
- Track 6: 8yr retail brand management
- Track 7: Startup with ≥$1M VC investment
- Track 8: Government-certified specialized talent

## 8. Law (4 tracks)
- Prerequisite: ROC attorney license or ROC foreign legal affairs attorney permit
- Track 1: Monthly salary ≥ NT$160,000
- Track 2: Chair professor/professor/associate professor/assistant professor at university
- Track 3: Executive legal role at firm or corporation
- Track 4: Bar Association recommendation

## 9. Biotechnology (4 tracks)
- Track 1: Monthly salary ≥ NT$160,000 in biotech
- Track 2: PhD + molecular biology/cell biology/genomics/genetic engineering expertise + international award or 4yr experience
- Track 3: 8yr professional/cross-disciplinary biotech experience
- Track 4: Certified by central authorities as specialized/transnational talent

## 10. Sports (4 tracks)
- Track 1: National team athlete — Top 8 Olympics, Top 3 Continental/World Games/Universiade/World Championship
- Track 2: National team coach — same achievement thresholds as Track 1
- Track 3: International referee at Olympics/Continental Games/World Games/Universiade
- Track 4: Sports industry professional with ROC sports organization recommendation

## 11. National Defense (4 tracks)
- Track 1: PhD + 5yr defense aerospace/military shipbuilding + key professional knowhow
- Track 2: 5yr machinery/electrical/R&D in defense aerospace (expertise uncommon in Taiwan)
- Track 3: 5yr shipborne weaponry/platform design/construction (expertise uncommon in Taiwan)
- Track 4: Ministry of National Defense certification

## 12. Environmental Protection (8 tracks)
- Track 1: Environmental sector experience + monthly salary ≥ NT$160,000
- Track 2: Exceptional R&D talent (Path A: academic + 3yr exp; Path B: 3yr exp + patents/publications)
- Track 3: Nobel/Tang/Wolf Prize recipients
- Track 4: National academy members
- Track 5: University professors/researchers with significant contributions (3yr)
- Track 6: PhD + 4yr environmental R&D/management
- Track 7: PhD with scarce domestic expertise + 4yr experience
- Track 8: Government-recommended candidates

## Common Documentation
- Tax statements (within 3 years) for salary verification
- Certificate of Employment with full name, company, position, duration, stamp/signature
- Non-English/Mandarin documents require certified translations
- Certain countries require ROC representative office verification
