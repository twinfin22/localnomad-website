# Thailand — Destination Thailand Visa (DTV)

## Source Documents

1. **DTV Overview PDF** — Royal Thai Embassy (via MFA image server)
2. **DTV Checklist PDF** — Royal Thai Consulate-General (via MFA image server)

Note: The DTV overview PDF appears to be from the Royal Thai Embassy in Tokyo (contains Japanese references like TOKIBOTOHON, yen-denominated fee). The checklist PDF appears to be from the Royal Thai Embassy in Kuala Lumpur (contains MYR equivalents). Core requirements are the same across embassies; fees vary by location.

## Key Data Extracted

### Visa Category & Purpose
Three DTV entry purposes:
1. **Workcation** — digital nomad, remote worker, foreign talent, freelancer
2. **Thai soft power-related activities** — Muay Thai, Thai culinary training, medical treatment
3. **Dependents** — spouses and children under 20 years old of DTV visa holders

### Duration & Stay
- **Validity**: Multiple entry, **5 years from issue date**
- **Duration per entry**: **180 days each time**
- Extension: not mentioned in these documents (JSON claims 180-day extension at THB 1,900 — not verified from these PDFs; likely from immigration bureau rules)

### Financial Requirement
- **Bank statements for the past 3 months with ending balance of no less than 500,000 THB** (or 65,000 MYR equivalent per KL embassy)
- Proof of salary slip/monthly income for the last 6 months

### Application Fee
- **52,000 yen** (Tokyo embassy pricing)
- Note: JSON claims THB 10,000 (~USD 300). The 52,000 yen figure (~USD 340 at current rates) is consistent with THB 10,000. Fees vary by embassy and are paid via e-payment (credit card, Visa or Mastercard only). Non-refundable.

### Required Documents (Workcation category)
1. Original passport (valid 6+ months)
2. Recent photograph (within 6 months)
3. Completed visa application form
4. Passport biodata page copy
5. Proof of prolonged residence in Thailand for at least 6 months (condo rental agreement, lease, etc.)
6. Bank statement (3 months, 500,000 THB minimum balance)
7. Salary slip/monthly income proof (6 months)
8. Foreign employment contract or employment certificate (authenticated by embassy of company's country)
9. Company registration/business license (authenticated by embassy of company's country)
10. Professional portfolio showcasing digital nomad/remote worker/foreign talent/freelancer status

### Dependents
- **Spouses and children under 20** of DTV holders
- Required: DTV holder's personal details (passport, DTV approval) + proof of relationship (family registration, certificate of residence)

### Health Insurance
- **Not mentioned** in either PDF as a requirement
- JSON states: "Not a national DTV requirement. Some embassies may request at discretion." — consistent with absence from official checklist.
