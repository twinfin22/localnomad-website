# Indonesia — E33G Remote Worker Visa (Visa Pekerja Jarak Jauh)

## Source Documents

1. **E-Visa FAQ page** — evisa.imigrasi.go.id (official e-visa portal)
2. **Immigration classification page** — imigrasi.go.id (partial content, JS-rendered)
3. **WebSearch results** — corroborating data from molina.imigrasi.go.id

Note: The main imigrasi.go.id site is heavily JS-rendered and returned limited data via WebFetch. The e-visa FAQ page provided the most complete requirements. Some details from the classification page supplemented the FAQ data.

## Key Data Extracted

### Visa Category
- **E33G — Visa Pekerja Jarak Jauh** (Remote Worker Visa)
- Classification: Visa Tinggal Terbatas (Limited Stay Visa)
- Purpose: "bekerja sebagai pekerja jarak jauh" (work as a remote worker)

### Duration & Stay
- **Maximum duration: 1 year**
- Must enter Indonesia within **90 days of issue**

### Financial Requirements
- **Income: minimum USD 60,000 per year** (salary or income from foreign employer)
- **Bank savings: minimum USD 2,000** in personal bank statements for last 3 months

### Application Fee
- **IDR 7,000,000** (~USD 450) for 1-year stay

### Required Documents
- Passport valid 6+ months
- Personal bank statements (minimum USD 2,000, last 3 months)
- Bank account proving income of at least USD 60,000/year
- Recent photograph
- Curriculum Vitae
- Travel itinerary
- Employment contract with company established outside Indonesian territory

### Permitted Activities
- Carry out assignments from overseas company
- Travel within Indonesia
- Bring family members to reside (per immigration regulations)
- Exit and re-enter with valid Re-entry Permit
- Pursue education through Multiple Activity Permit mechanism
- Investment-related activities
- Tourism and visiting friends/relatives

### Key Restrictions
- **No selling goods or services allowed**
- **"Prohibited from working by receiving compensation, wages, or any similar from individuals or companies in Indonesia"**

### Health Insurance
- **Not listed** as an official requirement in the e-visa FAQ

### Dependents
- Family members can reside in Indonesia per immigration regulations (mentioned on classification page)
- JSON claims: "spouse, children, parents, siblings (Dec 2025)" — the official page mentions family broadly but does not enumerate specific eligible relationships in the content extracted

### MERP (Multiple Exit Re-Entry Permit)
- Not detailed in FAQ page
- Classification page confirms: "Exit and re-enter Indonesia while the Re-entry Permit remains valid"
- JSON claims separate fee of IDR 1-2M — not verified from these official sources
