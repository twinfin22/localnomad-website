## Key Data Extracted

### Visa Category
- **Philippine Digital Nomad Visa (DNV)**
- Authorized by Executive Order No. 86, signed April 24, 2025

### Eligibility Requirements (8 conditions per EO)
1. At least 18 years old
2. Demonstrate remote work via digital technology
3. Show sufficient income generated outside the Philippines
4. Clean criminal record
5. Valid health insurance maintained throughout stay
6. National of a country that offers DNVs to Filipinos AND where Philippines maintains a Foreign Service Post (dual eligibility)
7. Pose no security threat
8. Not employed locally

### Duration & Stay
- **Maximum 1 year** per DNV
- **Renewable** for additional years under identical terms
- Note: JSON claims "renewable once (24 months maximum total)" — EO text says "may renew for additional years" without specifying a cap of 2 years. The 24-month cap may come from implementing rules.

### Income Requirement
- EO text: **"Must show proof of sufficient income which is generated outside the Philippines"**
- **No specific USD amount in the EO itself**
- JSON claims USD 24,000 — this likely comes from implementing guidelines/IRR, not the EO

### Health Insurance
- **Required** — "have health insurance valid for the period of the DNV"
- No specific coverage minimums in the EO

### Application Fee
- **Not specified in the EO**
- JSON estimates USD 200-300, notes "Official fee schedule pending IRR publication"

### Dependents
- **Not addressed in the EO**
- JSON states: "Not yet confirmed for initial program"

### Tax Treatment
- **No tax provisions in the EO**
- JSON notes: "no BIR guidance has been published"

### Dual Eligibility Requirement
- **CONFIRMED** — applicant must be "a national of a country that offers DNVs to Filipinos"
- Country must also have a Philippine Foreign Service Post

### Implementation Timeline (per EO)
- DFA must issue implementing guidelines within **30 days** of effectivity
- Pilot implementation within **60 days** of effectivity
- JSON states: "Pilot phase began June 2025" — consistent with May 5, 2025 effectivity + 60 days

### Local Work Prohibition
- Cannot be "employed locally" — one of the 8 eligibility conditions
