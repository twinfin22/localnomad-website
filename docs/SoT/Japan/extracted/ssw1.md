## Eligibility
- employer_sponsorship: "required (must have employment contract with an accepting organization)"
- education: "none specified (skill-based, not education-based)"
- experience: "Must demonstrate considerable degree of knowledge or experience through skills examination in the specified industrial field"
- age: "18 years or older"
- skill_test: "Required. Confirmed by field-specific skills examinations. Those who have completed Technical Intern Training (ii) are exempted from skills exams."
- japanese_language: "Required. Japan Foundation Test for Basic Japanese (JFT-Basic) or Japanese Language Proficiency Test (JLPT) N4 or higher. Those who completed Technical Intern Training (ii) are exempted. Additional requirements apply for Nursing care (nursing care Japanese language evaluation test), Automobile transportation business (taxi/bus drivers), and Railway (train operation works) requiring N3 or higher."
- other_requirements:
  - "Has not stayed for more than 5 years as Specified Skilled Worker (i)"
  - "No security deposit has been collected"
  - "No contract has been signed which sets out penalties"
  - "Be in good health"
  - "Must self-support and fully understand responsibilities"
