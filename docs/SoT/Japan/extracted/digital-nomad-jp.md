## Key Policy Details
- work_permission: "Classified under 'Designated Activities' status of residence. The Guidebook (Chapter 1, Section 1, p.1) specifically lists 'Digital Nomad (a person who stays in Japan for the purpose of international remote work, etc.)' as one of the cases where a residence card is NOT issued."
- family_allowed: true
- family_details: "The Guidebook states that Digital Nomad 'and his/her spouse/children' fall under Designated Activities where a residence card is not issued."
- dependent_visa: "Not applicable (no residence card issued; family enters under same Designated Activities framework)"
- residence_card: "NOT issued. Digital Nomad is explicitly listed among six cases where a residence card is not issued, alongside: persons staying 3 months or less, Temporary Visitors, Diplomats/Officials, certain Designated Activities staff, special permanent residents, and persons with no status."
- status_category: "Designated Activities (specific sub-category for digital nomads conducting international remote work)"
- limitations: "Cannot engage in work activities for Japanese companies/clients. Work must be for overseas entities only."
- note: "This visa type is relatively new and the ISA Guidebook (7th edition, March 2025) contains only a brief mention. Detailed eligibility criteria (income threshold, eligible nationalities, insurance requirements) are not covered in these source PDFs and should be verified from the ISA dedicated digital nomad page."
