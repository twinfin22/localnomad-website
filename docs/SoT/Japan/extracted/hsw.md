## Income Requirement
- amount: "3,000,000 JPY minimum for Highly Skilled Professional (Advanced Professional/Technical) and Highly Skilled Professional (Advanced Business Management) categories"
- currency: JPY
- period: annual
- note: "Income points vary by age bracket. Annual income of JPY 10,000,000+ earns 40 points; JPY 9,000,000 earns 35; JPY 8,000,000 earns 30; JPY 7,000,000 earns 25 (under age 40 only for 7M); JPY 6,000,000 earns 20 (under age 40 only); JPY 5,000,000 earns 15 (under age 35 only); JPY 4,000,000 earns 10 (under age 30 only). Advanced Academic Research field has no minimum income requirement."

## Duration
- initial: "5 years"
- extension: "renewable"
- max_total: "no fixed maximum"

## Eligibility
- employer_sponsorship: "required (must have accepting organization, except for some Advanced Business Management activities)"
- education: "Doctoral degree: 30 points (Academic Research) / 20 points (Professional-Technical, Business Management). Master's degree: 20 points. Bachelor's degree or equivalent: 10 points. Multiple degrees in relevant fields: +5 bonus points."
- experience: "Academic Research: 7+ years = 15 pts, 5+ = 10, 3+ = 5. Professional-Technical: 10+ years = 20 pts, 7+ = 15, 5+ = 10, 3+ = 5. Business Management: 10+ years = 25 pts, 7+ = 20, 5+ = 15, 3+ = 10."
- passing_score: "70 points or more required (out of total calculated from education, career, income, age, and bonus categories)"

## Point System Details (from point calculation PDF)
- categories: "Three tracks: (1) Advanced Academic Research, (2) Advanced Professional/Technical, (3) Advanced Business Management"
- age_points: "Under 30: 15 pts; 30-34: 10 pts; 35-39: 5 pts (same across all tracks)"
- bonus_items:
  - "Bonus 1 (Research achievements): See detailed criteria (3 sub-items)"
  - "Bonus 2 (Position): Representative director/CEO = 10 pts; Director/Executive = 5 pts"
  - "Bonus 3: Japanese national qualification related to work = 10 pts (Professional-Technical track)"
  - "Bonus 4: Employment at innovation-designated organization (MOJ notice) = 10 pts"
  - "Bonus 5: R&D expenditure ratio >3% at SME = 5 pts"
  - "Bonus 6: Foreign qualification related to work = 5 pts"
  - "Bonus 7: Degree from Japanese higher education institution = 10 pts"
  - "Bonus 8: JLPT N1 or university degree in Japanese from foreign university = 15 pts"
  - "Bonus 9: JLPT N2 (if not already getting Bonus 7 or 8 points) = 10 pts"
  - "Bonus 10: Employment in growth-sector business (MOJ-designated) = 10 pts"
  - "Bonus 11: Graduate of MOJ-designated university = 10 pts"
  - "Bonus 12: Completion of MOJ-designated training program = 5 pts"
  - "Bonus 13: Business investment of JPY 100M+ = 5 pts (Business Management track)"
  - "Bonus 14: Investment management business = 10 pts (Business Management track)"
  - "Bonus 15: Employment at local government-designated organization for advanced talent = 10 pts"
- research_achievements: "Patents (1+) = 20/15 pts (Academic/Professional). Pre-entry grants from public institutions with 3+ years research = 20/15 pts. Published papers (3+ as lead author) in recognized academic databases = 20/15 pts. Other equivalent achievements (case-by-case, requires MOJ review) = 20/15 pts. For Academic Research: 2+ of above = 25 pts."

## Key Policy Details
- work_permission: "Permission to engage in activities covering multiple statuses of residence. Described as 'Highly-skilled human resources in accordance with the points system.'"
- preferential_treatment:
  - "Permission to engage in activities covering multiple statuses of residence"
  - "5-year period of stay granted"
  - "Relaxation of requirements for permanent residence concerning period of stay in Japan"
- family_allowed: true
- dependent_visa: "Dependent (spouse or child supported by the foreign resident)"
- j_skip_system: "A separate system called 'Japan System for Special Highly-Skilled Professionals' (J-Skip) was introduced in April 2023. Applicants meeting specific academic background, professional career, and exceeding specific annual income are regarded as 'Special Highly-Skilled Professional' with additional preferential treatment. This is separate from the point-based system."
- notifications: "Must notify Regional Immigration Services Office within 14 days of changes to: (1) accepting organization (for HSP (i)(c), HSP (ii)), and (2) organization of affiliation (for HSP (i)(a), HSP (i)(b), HSP (ii) limited cases)"
