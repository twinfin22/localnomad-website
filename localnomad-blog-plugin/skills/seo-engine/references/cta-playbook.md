# CTA Playbook — Community-First

## CTA Tone Rules

Priority: Community tone > Conversion optimization

### Primary CTA (end of post)
- Placed after Closing paragraph
- Community-oriented, never salesy
- Specific about what happens next
- Reduce risk ("no spam, just good stuff" energy)

**Good examples**:
- "비자 미로에서 같이 헤엄치자 → [newsletter link]"
- "더 궁금하면 → [related guide link]"
- "이 글이 도움이 됐다면, 같은 고민 하는 친구한테 보내줘"
- "Let's swim together, enchovy school → [community link]"

**Bad examples** (never use):
- "Subscribe now for weekly visa updates!"
- "Don't miss out — join 500+ nomads today!"
- "Get exclusive access to our visa toolkit"

### Inline CTAs (1-2 in body)
- Contextual links woven into narrative
- Must feel like a natural recommendation, not an ad
- Placed where the reader would naturally want more info

**Good examples**:
- "E-7과 D-8 중 고민이라면 [비교 페이지]에서 나란히 볼 수 있다"
- "If you're wondering how the points system works, I broke it down [here]"
- "이 동네 이야기가 궁금하다면 [neighborhood guide] 참고"

**Bad examples** (never use):
- "Click here to compare visas"
- "Read our comprehensive guide"
- "Check out our amazing comparison tool"

## CTA Placement Rules

1. **Never** in the first paragraph
2. **Primary CTA**: after Closing paragraph (end of post)
3. **Inline CTAs**: after establishing context/value in a section
4. When contextually relevant: mention a related guide or tool naturally

## Principles Adopted (from content-creation)
- "Specific about what happens next" — tell them exactly what they'll get
- "Reduce risk" — no hard commitments, no pressure

## Principles Rejected
- "Create urgency" — no fake scarcity, no countdown energy
- Corporate action verbs (Get, Start, Download, Join, Try) — replace with conversational alternatives
