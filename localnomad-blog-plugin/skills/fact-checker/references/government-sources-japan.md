**Japan-specific notes**:
- ISA = residence status (in Japan). MOFA = visa issuance (at embassies abroad). Different jurisdictions.
- Digital Nomad Visa official name: "Designated Activities (Notification 53)"
- SSW1 and SSW2 are separate categories with different rules
- NTA tax circulars (通達) are the authoritative source for tax residency interpretation — cite circular number (e.g., NTA No.12006)
- mhlw.go.jp English pages are very limited — most social insurance rules require Japanese pages
- JETRO's "Setting Up Business in Japan" section is the primary source for Business Manager visa investment requirements
- e-Gov is JA only — use japaneselawtranslation.go.jp for English law text

**Japan blocklist** (NOT Tier 1):
- japanvisa.com — private visa service, not government
- japan-guide.com — community/travel site (Tier 2 at best for non-visa factual claims like rail passes)
