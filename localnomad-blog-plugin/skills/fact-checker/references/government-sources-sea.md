# Government Sources — Southeast Asia & China

Last updated: 2026-03-14

Use this as the authoritative list of Tier 1 sources when verifying SEA and China-related claims. Any URL on this list is automatically Tier 1. URLs NOT on this list require manual tier classification.

**China-specific notes**:
- WeChat Pay / Alipay foreign card limits are set by PBoC regulations — these change frequently (changed multiple times 2023-2025)
- COVA launched Sep 2025 — replaces older embassy-specific visa application portals
- EN versions of Chinese government sites are often months behind ZH versions
- VPN-related claims ("no foreigner prosecuted for personal VPN use") are structurally unsourceable — reframe editorially

- BOI is the sole issuing authority for SMART visa and LTR visa endorsement
- Thailand's remittance tax policy changed Jan 1, 2024 — all foreign income remitted to Thailand is now taxable **regardless of when earned** (previously only same-calendar-year income was taxed)
- LTR visa tax exemptions are specific to BOI-endorsed categories — verify which sub-type applies
- immigration.go.th has frequent downtime — check EN version first as fallback
