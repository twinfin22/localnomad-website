**Korea-specific notes**:
- MOJ press releases count as Tier 1 (K-STAR, Top-Tier, and other programs are announced this way)
- Check immigration.go.kr announcement section (공지사항) for recent policy changes
- Visa sub-category codes matter: F-2-7 vs F-2-T are different pathways
- HiKorea = service portal (applications), immigration.go.kr = information portal (rules)
- KIIP test portal: kiiptest.org (placement tests) — also Tier 1 (run by MOJ)
- nhis.or.kr English pages are limited — Korean pages have detailed premium tables and enrollment conditions
- GNI per capita is announced by the **Bank of Korea** (typically early March) — directly affects F-1-D income threshold calculation. kostat.go.kr publishes related demographic/economic statistics
- investkorea.org is operated by KOTRA (government trade agency), not a private company

**Korea blocklist** (NOT Tier 1):
- visa-kr.com, visa-korea.com — commercial/scam visa services (사칭 사이트)
- Always verify .go.kr domain for government sources
