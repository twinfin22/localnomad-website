## Search Formula

```
[country modifier] + [category keyword] + [topic-specific term]
```

Examples:
- guides + korea + visa → "seoul government office visa"
- tips + japan + coworking → "tokyo coworking space laptop"
- stories + taiwan + neighborhood → "taipei street market local"

## Quality Filters

When selecting from Unsplash results:
1. **Resolution**: Must be ≥960px wide (landscape orientation ensures this)
2. **Relevance**: Image should relate to post topic, not just country
3. **Diversity**: Avoid repeating the same Unsplash photographer across posts
4. **Sensitivity**: No images that could be culturally insensitive or stereotyping
5. **Composition**: Prefer images where the focal point is center or slightly off-center (works well with 2:1 crop)
