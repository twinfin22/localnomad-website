# Fact-Check Source Tiers

## Tier 1 — Government / Official (Required for visa claims)

These sources are mandatory for any visa requirement, process, or legal claim.

For the **full country-by-country URL directory**, see `fact-checker/references/government-sources.md`.

### Quick Reference (Primary Immigration Authorities)
- **Korea**: immigration.go.kr, hikorea.go.kr, law.go.kr, mofa.go.kr, visa.go.kr, socinet.go.kr
- **Japan**: isa.go.jp, mofa.go.jp, japaneselawtranslation.go.jp, ssw.go.jp
- **Taiwan**: immigration.gov.tw, boca.gov.tw, goldcard.nat.gov.tw, law.moj.gov.tw, digitalnomad.ndc.gov.tw
- **China**: nia.gov.cn, consular.mfa.gov.cn
- **Thailand**: immigration.go.th, thaievisa.go.th
- **Vietnam**: immigration.gov.vn, evisa.gov.vn
- **Indonesia**: imigrasi.go.id, evisa.imigrasi.go.id
- **Malaysia**: imi.gov.my, malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my
- **Singapore**: ica.gov.sg, mfa.gov.sg
- **Philippines**: immigration.gov.ph

### Auto Tier 1 Rule
Any URL with a government domain (.go.kr, .go.jp, .gov.tw, .gov.cn, .go.th, .gov.vn, .go.id, .gov.my, .gov.sg, .gov.ph) is automatically Tier 1.

### General
- Official embassy/consulate websites
- Government gazette publications
- Published legislation text
- Official government press releases (even on news sites, if linking to original)

## Tier 2 — Established Media

Acceptable for general context, trends, statistics. NOT sufficient alone for visa requirements.

- Major news outlets (Reuters, AP, local major newspapers)
- Official government press conferences (reported)
- Established industry publications
- Academic papers and research institutions
- International organizations (OECD, World Bank, IOM)

## Tier 3 — Community / Expert

Acceptable for anecdotal evidence, community experiences, practical tips. Must be clearly attributed.

- Verified expat community forums
- Established nomad blogs with track record
- Expert commentary (immigration lawyers, tax advisors)
- Social media posts from verified accounts
- Personal experience (LibaD's own)

## Freshness Rules

| Content Type | Max Age | Action if Older |
|-------------|---------|-----------------|
| Visa requirements | 6 months | Re-verify with Tier 1 source |
| Statistics | 12 months | Add "as of [date]" qualifier |
| General info | 24 months | Flag for review |
| Historical facts | No limit | N/A |

## Cross-Verification Rules

- **Critical claims** (visa requirements, legal obligations): ≥2 independent sources, at least 1 Tier 1
- **Statistics**: source attribution required (cite the number AND where it came from)
- **Process descriptions** ("how to apply"): must match current official process, verified within 6 months
- **Cost/fee claims**: must be current fiscal year or explicitly dated
