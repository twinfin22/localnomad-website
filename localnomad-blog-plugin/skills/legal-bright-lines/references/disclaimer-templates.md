# Disclaimer Templates

Copy-paste ready blocks for different content types.

## Standard Blog Disclaimer (Korea/Japan)

```mdx
---
*The information in this post is based on publicly available requirements as of {date}. Immigration rules change frequently. This is not legal advice. Always verify current requirements through official government sources before making any decisions.*
---
```

## Taiwan Blog Disclaimer (EN + 繁中) — REQUIRED

Place this ABOVE any quiz/comparison results AND at the bottom of the post.

```mdx
---
*LocalNomad is not a licensed immigration consulting firm (移民業務機構). The information below is based on published requirements from official Taiwan government sources. This is not an eligibility assessment or legal advice. For personalized guidance, consult a licensed immigration consultant.*

*LocalNomad 並非持有執照的移民業務機構。以下資訊係根據台灣政府官方公開之規定整理而成，不構成資格評估或法律建議。如需個人化諮詢，請洽詢持有執照之移民業務機構。*

---
```

## Taiwan Quiz Result Disclaimer

Place ABOVE and BELOW quiz results.

```mdx
> **This is not an eligibility assessment.** The table below shows published requirements alongside your answers for comparison purposes only. LocalNomad cannot determine your eligibility.
>
> **以下並非資格評估。** 下表僅將官方公開要求與您的回答並列比較，供參考之用。LocalNomad 無法判斷您的申請資格。
```

## Tax Content — 3-Layer

### Layer 1: Top of content
```mdx
> **General Information Only**: This post discusses tax-related topics for informational purposes. This is not tax advice. Tax laws vary by jurisdiction and change frequently.
```

### Layer 2: Inline (where specific tax info appears)
```mdx
*Note: Tax obligations depend on your specific situation, residency status, and applicable treaties. Consult a licensed tax professional (세무사/税理士/會計師) for personalized advice.*
```

### Layer 3: Bottom of content
```mdx
---
*LocalNomad is not a tax advisory service. The tax information in this post is general in nature and may not reflect the most current legislation. Professional tax consultation is recommended before making any financial decisions based on this content.*
---
```

## Quiz/Tool Generic Disclaimer

For any interactive tool or calculator:

```mdx
> Based on published requirements. Not legal advice. Verify all information through official government sources.
```
